# Hadith Bank — books and source editions

[Read and search online](https://blaghtayt-alt.github.io/Hadith-Bank/) · [Browse book pages](https://blaghtayt-alt.github.io/Hadith-Bank/browse/) · [Repository overview](../README.md)

This directory is generated from the published material catalog. It lists the collection, actual source-edition name, preserved occurrence count, reading pages and available original downloads. A collection may be represented by selected quotations, excerpts, one volume or several editions. An entry does not assert that the entire original book is present.

Counts describe source occurrences, including distinct variants and Companion reports; they are not counts of unique canonical hadiths. Source wording, numbering, translation origin and review qualifications remain attached to each record.
