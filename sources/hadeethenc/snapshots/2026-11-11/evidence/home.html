Goals

Project of Prophetic Hadith Encyclopedia and Its Translations

An integrated project of selecting frequently repeated Prophetic Hadiths in the Islamic content, explaining them in a simplified and adequate way, presenting high quality translation based on accurate procedures into famous languages, then providing them for free through all available means.

01

Providing a developed, authentic, free, international reference for the Prophetic hadith translations.

02

Providing an electronic memory of Hadith translations for translators while translating

03

Providing translations to the target audience through all available means

Terms and Policies

Contents of the translations can be downloaded and republished under the following terms:

1. No modification, addition, or deletion of the content.
2. Clearly refer to the publisher and source (HadeethEnc.com).
3. Mention the version number when republishing the translation.
4. Keep the translation version information inside the document.
5. Notify the source (HadeethEnc.com) of any note on the translation.
6. Update translations according to the latest version issued by the source.
7. Do not include inappropriate advertisements when displaying the content.
