<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English · full narration</h3>

<p dir="auto">Full narration; the selected source preserves an excerpt or reported meaning.</p>

<p dir="auto">‘Awf ibn Mālik al-Ashja‘i (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: I stood in prayer one night with the Messenger of Allah (may Allah’s peace and blessings be upon him). He stood up and recited Sūrat al-Baqarah. Whenever he came across a verse mentioning mercy, he stopped and asked (Allah for mercy); and whenever he came across a verse mentioning punishment, he stopped and sought refuge (with Allah from it). Then he bowed and paused as long as he stood, and said while bowing: &quot;Glory be to the Possessor of greatness, dominion, grandeur, and majesty.&quot; Then he prostrated himself and paused as long as he stood and said while prostrating the same thing he said while bowing. He then stood up and recited Sūrat Āl-‘Imrān, and then recited other Sūrahs, one after another.</p>

<details>
<summary>Filipino · notes and commentary</summary>

<p dir="auto">Ang pag-alaala at pagbanggit kay Allāh ay hindi pag-uulit na hiwalay sa kahulugang ito. Sa pagyuko at pagpapatirapa, sinasabi ng Propeta: “Napakamaluwalhati ng may kapangyarihan, paghahari, dangal, at kadakilaan”. Sinabi rin niya: “At sa pagyuko,ay dakilain ninyo rito ang Panginoon-Kamahal-mahalan Siya at Kapita-pitagan”. Sa pagyuko ay sinasabi niya: “Napakamaluwalhati ng Panginoon ko, ang Dakila.” Sa pagpapatirapa naman: “Napakamaluwalhati ng Panginoon ko ang Pinakamataas.” [Tala H8–H10] Pinagsasama ng dalawang pangungusap na magaan sa dila, mabigat sa timbangan, at minamahal ng Napakamaawain ang pagpupuri at pagdakila. Ang kahulugan ng una ay: “Kaluwalhatian kay Allah at kalakip ng papuri sa Kanya.” Ang ikalawa naman ay: “Kaluwalhatian kay Allah, ang Dakila.” [Tala H11] Sa ganito nabubuhay ang mga puso at lumalakas ang taimtim na paggalang kay Allāh sa mga ito.</p>
