<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Rafi’ bin Khadij said:“We were with the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) in Dhul-Hulaifah in (the land of) Tihamah. We acquired sheep and camels and the people hastened to put cooking pots on the fires before they had been distributed. The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) came to us and ordered that they be overturned,* then he made one camel equivalent to ten sheep.”</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Ibn Majah · 3137]</p>
