<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Musa:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;I saw in a dream that I moved a sword and its blade got broken, and that symbolized the casualties which the believers suffered on the day of Uhud. Then I moved it again, and it became as perfect as it had been, and that symbolized the Conquest (of Mecca) which Allah helped us to achieve, and the union of all the believers. I (also) saw cows in the dream, and what Allah does is always beneficial. Those cows appeared to symbolize the faithful believers (who were martyred) on the day of Uhud</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih al Bukhari · 4081]</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Khabbab:We migrated with the Prophet (ﷺ) for Allah&#x27;s Cause, so our reward became due with Allah. Some of us passed away (i.e. died) without enjoying anything from their reward, and one of them was Mus`ab bin `Umair who was killed (i.e. martyred) on the day of Uhud. He did not leave behind except a sheet of striped woolen cloth. If we covered his head with it, his feet became naked, and if we covered his feet with it, his head became naked. The Prophet (ﷺ) said to us, &quot;Cover his head with it and put Idhkhir (i.e. a kind of grass) over his feet,&quot; or said, &quot;Put some Idhkhir over his feet.&quot; But some of us have got their fruits ripened, and they are collecting them</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Anas:That the Prophet (ﷺ) sent his uncle, the brother of Um Sulaim at the head of seventy riders. The chief of the pagans, &#x27;Amir bin at-Tufail proposed three suggestions (to the Prophet (ﷺ) ) saying, &quot;Choose one of three alternatives: (1) that the bedouins will be under your command and the townspeople will be under my command; (2) or that I will be your successor, (3) or otherwise I will attack you with two thousand from Bani Ghatafan.&quot; But &#x27;Amir was infected with plague in the House of Um so-and-so. He said, &quot;Shall I stay in the house of a lady from the family of so-and-so after having a (swelled) gland like that she-camel? Get me my horse.&quot; So he died on the back of his horse. Then Haram, the brother of Um Sulaim and a lame man along with another man from so-and-so (tribe) went towards the pagans (i.e. the tribe of &#x27;Amir). Haram said (to his companions), &quot;Stay near to me, for I will go to them. If they (i.e. infidels) should give me protection, you will be near to me, and if they should kill me, then you should go back to your companions. Then Haram went to them and said, &quot;Will you give me protection so as to convey the message of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) ?&quot; So, he started talking to them&#x27; but they signalled to a man (to kill him) and he went behind him and stabbed him (with a spear). He (i.e. Haram) said, &quot;Allahu Akbar! I have succeeded, by the Lord of the Ka`ba!&quot; The companion of Haram was pursued by the infidels, and then they (i.e. Haram&#x27;s companions) were all killed except the lame man who was at the top of a mountain. Then Allah revealed to us a verse that was among the cancelled ones later on. It was: &#x27;We have met our Lord and He is pleased with us and has made us pleased.&#x27; (After this event) the Prophet (ﷺ) invoked evil on the infidels every morning for 30 days. He invoked evil upon the (tribes of) Ril, Dhakwan, Bani Lihyan and Usaiya who disobeyed Allah and His Apostle</p>

<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا عُبَيْدُ بْنُ إِسْمَاعِيلَ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو أُسَامَةَ، عَنْ هِشَامٍ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ قَالَتِ اسْتَأْذَنَ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَبُو يَقُولُ ‏&quot;‏ إِنِّي لأَرْجُو ذَلِكَ ‏&quot;‏ قَالَتْ فَانْتَظَرَهُ أَبُو بَكْرٍ فَأَتَاهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ذَاتَ يَوْمٍ ظُهْرًا فَنَادَاهُ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ أَخْرِجْ مَنْ عِنْدَكَ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ فَقَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ إِنَّمَا هُمَا ابْنَتَاىَ‏.‏ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ أَشَعَرْتَ أَنَّهُ قَدْ أُذِنَ لِي فِي الْخُرُوجِ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ فَقَالَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ الصُّحْبَةُ‏.‏ فَقَالَ <section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Aisha:Abu Bakr asked the Prophet (ﷺ) to allow him to go out (of Mecca) when he was greatly annoyed (by the infidels). But the Prophet (ﷺ) said to him, &#x27;&#x27;Wait.&quot; Abu Bakr said, O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! Do you hope that you will be allowed (to migrate)?&quot; Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) replied, &quot;I hope so.&quot; So Abu Bakr waited for him till one day Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) came at noon time and addressed him saying &quot;Let whoever is present with you, now leave you.&quot; Abu Bakr said, &quot;None is present but my two daughters.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;Have you noticed that I have been allowed to go out (to migrate)?&quot; Abu Bakr said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Apostle, I would like to accompany you.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;You will accompany me.&quot; Abu Bakr said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! I have got two she-camels which I had prepared and kept ready for (our) going out.&quot; So he gave one of the two (she-camels) to the Prophet (ﷺ) and it was Al-Jad`a . They both rode and proceeded till they reached the Cave at the mountain of Thaur where they hid themselves. Amir bin Fuhaira was the slave of `Abdullah bin at-Tufail bin Sakhbara `Aisha&#x27;s brother from her mother&#x27;s side. Abu Bakr had a milch she-camel. Amir used to go with it (i.e. the milch she-camel) in the afternoon and come back to them before noon by setting out towards them in the early morning when it was still dark and then he would take it to the pasture so that none of the shepherds would be aware of his job. When the Prophet (and Abu Bakr) went away (from the Cave), he (i.e. &#x27;Amir) too went along with them and they both used to make him ride at the back of their camels in turns till they reached Medina. &#x27;Amir bin Fuhaira was martyred on the day of Bir Ma&#x27;una. Narrated `Urwa: When those (Muslims) at Bir Ma&#x27;una were martyred and `Amr bin Umaiya Ad- Damri was taken prisoner, &#x27;Amir bin at-Tufail, pointing at a killed person, asked `Amr, &quot;Who is this?&quot; `Amr bin Umaiya said to him, &quot;He is &#x27;Amir bin Fuhaira.&quot; &#x27;Amir bin at-Tufail said, &quot;I saw him lifted to the sky after he was killed till I saw the sky between him and the earth, and then he was brought down upon the earth. Then the news of the killed Muslims reached the Prophet (ﷺ) and he announced the news of their death saying, &quot;Your companions (of Bir Ma&#x27;una) have been killed, and they have asked their Lord saying, &#x27;O our Lord! Inform our brothers about us as we are pleased with You and You are pleased with us.&quot; So Allah informed them (i.e. the Prophet (ﷺ) and his companions) about them (i.e. martyrs of Bir Mauna). On that day, `Urwa bin Asma bin As-Salt who was one of them, was killed, and `Urwa (bin Az-Zubair) was named after `Urwa bin Asma and Mundhir (bin Az-Zubair) was named after Mundhir bin `Amr (who had also been martyred on that day)</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Jabir:We were digging (the trench) on the day of (Al-Khandaq ( i.e. Trench )) and we came across a big solid rock. We went to the Prophet (ﷺ) and said, &quot;Here is a rock appearing across the trench.&quot; He said, &quot;I am coming down.&quot; Then he got up, and a stone was tied to his belly for we had not eaten anything for three days. So the Prophet (ﷺ) took the spade and struck the big solid rock and it became like sand. I said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! Allow me to go home.&quot; (When the Prophet (ﷺ) allowed me) I said to my wife, &quot;I saw the Prophet (ﷺ) in a state that I cannot treat lightly. Have you got something (for him to eat?&quot; She replied, &quot;I have barley and a she goat.&quot; So I slaughtered the she-kid and she ground the barley; then we put the meat in the earthenware cooking pot. Then I came to the Prophet (ﷺ) when the dough had become soft and fermented and (the meat in) the pot over the stone trivet had nearly been well-cooked, and said, &quot;I have got a little food prepared, so get up O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ), you and one or two men along with you (for the food).&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) asked, &quot;How much is that food?&quot; I told him about it. He said, &quot;It is abundant and good. Tell your wife not to remove the earthenware pot from the fire and not to take out any bread from the oven till I reach there.&quot; Then he said (to all his companions), &quot;Get up.&quot; So the Muhajirin (i.e. Emigrants) and the Ansar got up. When I came to my wife, I said, &quot;Allah&#x27;s Mercy be upon you! The Prophet came along with the Muhajirin and the Ansar and those who were present with them.&quot; She said, &quot;Did the Prophet (ﷺ) ask you (how much food you had)?&quot; I replied, &quot;Yes.&quot; Then the Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;Enter and do not throng.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) started cutting the bread (into pieces) and put the cooked meat over it. He covered the earthenware pot and the oven whenever he took something out of them. He would give the food to his companions and take the meat out of the pot. He went on cutting the bread and scooping the meat (for his companions) till they all ate their fill, and even then, some food remained. Then the Prophet (ﷺ) said (to my wife), &quot;Eat and present to others as the people are struck with hunger</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Jabir bin `Abdullah:When the Trench was dug, I saw the Prophet (ﷺ) in the state of severe hunger. So I returned to my wife and said, &quot;Have you got anything (to eat), for I have seen Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) in a state of severe hunger.&quot; She brought out for me, a bag containing one Sa of barley, and we had a domestic she animal (i.e. a kid) which I slaughtered then, and my wife ground the barley and she finished at the time I finished my job (i.e. slaughtering the kid). Then I cut the meat into pieces and put it in an earthenware (cooking) pot, and returned to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) . My wife said, &quot;Do not disgrace me in front of Allah&#x27;s Apostle and those who are with him.&quot; So I went to him and said to him secretly, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! I have slaughtered a she-animal (i.e. kid) of ours, and we have ground a Sa of barley which was with us. So please come, you and another person along with you.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) raised his voice and said, &quot;O people of Trench ! Jabir has prepared a meal so let us go.&quot; Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said to me, &quot;Don&#x27;t put down your earthenware meat pot (from the fireplace) or bake your dough till I come.&quot; So I came (to my house) and Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) too, came, proceeding before the people. When I came to my wife, she said, &quot;May Allah do so-and-so to you.&quot; I said, &quot;I have told the Prophet (ﷺ) of what you said.&quot; Then she brought out to him (i.e. the Prophet (ﷺ) the dough, and he spat in it and invoked for Allah&#x27;s Blessings in it. Then he proceeded towards our earthenware meat-pot and spat in it and invoked for Allah&#x27;s Blessings in it. Then he said (to my wife). Call a lady-baker to bake along with you and keep on taking out scoops from your earthenware meat-pot, and do not put it down from its fireplace.&quot; They were onethousand (who took their meals), and by Allah they all ate, and when they left the food and went away, our earthenware pot was still bubbling (full of meat) as if it had not decreased, and our dough was still being baked as if nothing had been taken from it</p>
