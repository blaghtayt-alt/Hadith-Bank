<p dir="ltr">Narrated Anas:I know (about) the Hijab (the order of veiling of women) more than anybody else. Ubai bin Ka`b used to ask me about it. Allah&#x27;s Apostle remained sitting and some people remained sitting with him after the other guests had left.
