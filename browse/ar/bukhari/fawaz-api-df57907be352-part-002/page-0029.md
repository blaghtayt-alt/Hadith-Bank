<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Ibn `Umar:We were talking about Hajjat-ul-Wada`, while the Prophet (ﷺ) was amongst us. We did not know what Hajjat-ul-Wada` signified. The Prophet (ﷺ) praised Allah and then mentioned Al-Masih Ad-Dajjal and described him extensively, saying, &quot;Allah did not send any prophet but that prophet warned his nation of Al-Masih Ad-Dajjal. Noah and the prophets following him warned (their people) of him. He will appear amongst you (O Muhammad&#x27;s followers), and if it happens that some of his qualities may be hidden from you, but your Lord&#x27;s State is clear to you and not hidden from you. The Prophet (ﷺ) said it thrice. Verily, your Lord is not blind in one eye, while he (i.e. Ad-Dajjal) is blind in the right eye which looks like a grape bulging out (of its cluster). No doubt,! Allah has made your blood and your properties sacred to one another like the sanctity of this day of yours, in this town of yours, in this month of yours.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) added: No doubt! Haven&#x27;t I conveyed Allah&#x27;s Message to you? &quot; They replied, &quot;Yes,&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) said thrice, &quot;O Allah! Be witness for it.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) added, &quot;Woe to you!&quot; (or said), &quot;May Allah be merciful to you! Do not become infidels after me (i.e. my death) by cutting the necks (throats) of one another</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Sa`d:The Prophet (ﷺ) visited me during Hajjat ul-Wada` while I was suffering from a disease which brought me to the verge of death. I said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! My ailment has reached such a (bad) state as you see, and I have much wealth, but I have no-one to inherit from me except my only daughter. Shall I give 2/3 of my property as alms (in charity)?&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;No,&quot; I said, &quot;Shall I give half of my property as alms?&quot; He said, &quot;No.&quot; I said, &quot;(Shall I give) 1/3 of it? &quot; He replied, &quot; 1/3, and even 1/3 is too much. It is better for you to leave your inheritors wealthy rather than to leave them poor, begging people (for their sustenance); and whatever you spend for Allah&#x27;s Sake, you will get reward for it even for the morsel of food which you put in your wives mouth.&quot; I said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! Should I remain (in Mecca) behind my companions (who are going with you to Medina)?&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;If you remain behind, any good deed which you will do for Allah&#x27;s Sake, will upgrade and elevate you. May be you will live longer so that some people may benefit by you and some other (i.e. infidels) may get harmed by you.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) then added, &quot;O Allah! Complete the Migration of my companions and do not turn them on their heels. But the poor Sa`d bin Khaula (not the above mentioned Sa`d) (died in Mecca) .&quot; Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) pitied Sa`d for he died in Mecca</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Musa:My Companions sent me to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) to ask him for some animals to ride on as they were accompanying him in the army of Al-Usra, and that was the Ghazwa (Battle) of Tabuk, I said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Prophet! My companions have sent me to you to provide them with means of transportation.&quot; He said, &quot;By Allah! I will not make you ride anything.&quot; It happened that when I reached him, he was in an angry mood, and I didn&#x27;t notice it. So I returned in a sad mood because of the refusal of the Prophet (ﷺ) and for the fear that the Prophet (ﷺ) might have become &#x27;angry with me. So I returned to my companions and informed them of what the Prophet (ﷺ) had said. Only a short while had passed when I heard Bilal calling, &quot;O `Abdullah bin Qais!&quot; I replied to his call. Bilal said, &quot;Respond to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) who is calling you.&quot; When I went to him (i.e. the Prophet), he said, &quot;Take these two camels tied together and also these two camels tied together,&quot;&#x27; referring to six camels he had brought them from Sa`d at that time. The Prophet (ﷺ) added, &quot;Take them to your companions and say, &#x27;Allah (or Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) ) allows you to ride on these,&#x27; so ride on them.&quot; So I took those camels to them and said, &quot;The Prophet (ﷺ) allows you to ride on these (camels) but by Allah, I will not leave you till some of you proceed with me to somebody who heard the statement of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ). Do not think that I narrate to you a thing which Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) has not said.&quot; They said to me, &quot;We consider you truthful, and we will do what you like.&quot; The sub-narrator added: So Abu Musa proceeded along with some of them till they came to those who have heard the statement of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) wherein he denied them (some animals to ride on) and (his statement) whereby he gave them the same. So these people told them the same information as Abu Musa had told them</p>
