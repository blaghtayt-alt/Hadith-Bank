<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Huraira:The Prophet (ﷺ) or Abul-Qasim said, &quot;If the Ansar took their way through a valley or a mountain pass, I would take Ansar&#x27;s valley. <section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Sa`d&#x27;s father:When the emigrants reached Medina. Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) established the bond of fraternity between `Abdur-Rahman and Sa`d bin Ar-Rabi. Sa`d said to `Abdur-Rahman, &quot;I am the richest of all the Ansar, so I want to divide my property (between us), and I have two wives, so see which of the two you like and tell me, so that I may divorce her, and when she finishes her prescribed period (i.e. &#x27;Idda) of divorce, then marry her.&quot; `Abdur-Rahman said, &quot;May Allah bless your family and property for you; where is your market?&quot; So they showed him the Qainuqa&#x27; market. (He went there and) returned with a profit in the form of dried yogurt and butter. He continued going (to the market) till one day he came, bearing the traces of yellow scent. The Prophet (ﷺ) asked, &quot;What is this (scent)?&quot; He replied, &quot;I got married.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) asked, &quot;How much Mahr did you give her?&quot; He replied, &quot;I gave her a datestone of gold or a gold piece equal to the weight of a date-stone.&quot; (The narrator, Ibrahim, is in doubt as to which is correct)</p>
