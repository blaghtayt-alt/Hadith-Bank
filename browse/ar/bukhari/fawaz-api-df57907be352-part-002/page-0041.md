<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Ali:While we were in a funeral procession in Baqi Al-Gharqad, Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) came and sat down, and we sat around him. He had a small stick in his hand and he bent his head and started scraping the ground with it. He then said, &quot;There is none among you, and no created soul but has his place written for him either in Paradise or in the Hell-Fire, and also has his happy or miserable fate (in the Hereafter) written for him.&quot; A man said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! Shall we depend upon what is written for us and give up doing (good) deeds? For whoever among us is destined to be fortunate (in the Hereafter), will join the fortunate peoples and whoever among us is destined to be miserable will do such deeds as are characteristic of the people who are destined to misery.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;Those who are destined to be happy (in the Hereafter) will find it easy and pleasant to do the deeds characteristic of those destined to happiness, while those who are to be among the miserable (in the Hereafter), will find it easy to do the deeds characteristic of those destined to misery.&quot; Then he recited: &#x27;As for him who gives (in charity) and keeps his duty to Allah and believes in the Best reward from Allah</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said, &quot; Horses are kept for one of three purposes: A man may keep them (for Allah&#x27;s Cause) to receive a reward in the Hereafter; another may keep them as a means of protection; and a third may keep them to be a burden for him. As for the man for whom the horse is a source of reward, he is the one who ties it for Allah&#x27;s Cause, and he ties it with a long rope in a pasture or a garden, then, whatever it eats or drinks in that pasture or garden will be added to his good deeds. And if it breaks its rope and jumps over one or two hills, then, for all its footsteps and its manure, good deeds will be written for him. And if it passes by a river and drinks of its water though its owner had no intention to water it from that river, even then he will have good deeds written for him. So that horse will be (a source of) reward for such a man. If a man ties a horse for earning his livelihood and abstaining from asking others for help and he does not forget Allah&#x27;s right, i.e. pays its Zakat and gives it to be used in Allah&#x27;s Cause, then that horse will be a means of protection for him. But if a man ties it out of pride and to show off and to excite others, then that horse will be a burden (of sins) for him.&quot; Then Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) was asked regarding donkeys. <h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Zaid bin Thabit:Abu Bakr As-Siddiq sent for me when the people of Yamama had been killed (i.e., a number of the Prophet&#x27;s Companions who fought against Musailima). (I went to him) and found `Umar bin Al- Khattab sitting with him. Abu Bakr then said (to me), &quot;`Umar has come to me and said: &quot;Casualties were heavy among the Qurra&#x27; of the Qur&#x27;an (i.e. those who knew the Qur&#x27;an by heart) on the day of the Battle of Yamama, and I am afraid that more heavy casualties may take place among the Qurra&#x27; on other battlefields, whereby a large part of the Qur&#x27;an may be lost. Therefore I suggest, you (Abu Bakr) order that the Qur&#x27;an be collected.&quot; I said to `Umar, &quot;How can you do something which Allah&#x27;s Apostle did not do?&quot; `Umar said, &quot;By Allah, that is a good project.&quot; `Umar kept on urging me to accept his proposal till Allah opened my chest for it and I began to realize the good in the idea which `Umar had realized.&quot; Then Abu Bakr said (to me). &#x27;You are a wise young man and we do not have any suspicion about you, and you used to write the Divine Inspiration for Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ). So you should search for (the fragmentary scripts of) the Qur&#x27;an and collect it in one book.&quot; By Allah If they had ordered me to shift one of the mountains, it would not have been heavier for me than this ordering me to collect the Qur&#x27;an. Then I said to Abu Bakr, &quot;How will you do something which Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) did not do?&quot; Abu Bakr replied, &quot;By Allah, it is a good project.&quot; Abu Bakr kept on urging me to accept his idea until Allah opened my chest for what He had opened the chests of Abu Bakr and `Umar. So I started looking for the Qur&#x27;an and collecting it from (what was written on) palme stalks, thin white stones and also from the men who knew it by heart, till I found the last Verse of Surat at-Tauba (Repentance) with Abi Khuzaima Al-Ansari, and I did not find it with anybody other than him. The Verse is: &#x27;Verily there has come unto you an Apostle (Muhammad) from amongst yourselves. It grieves him that you should receive any injury or difficulty..(till the end of Surat-Baraa&#x27; (at-Tauba) (9.128-129). Then the complete manuscripts (copy) of the Qur&#x27;an remained with Abu Bakr till he died, then with `Umar till the end of his life, and then with Hafsa, the daughter of `Umar</p>
