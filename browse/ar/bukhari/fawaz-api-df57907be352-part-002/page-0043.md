<p dir="ltr">Narrated Ibn `Abbas:Regarding His (Allah&#x27;s) Statement:-- &#x27;Move not your tongue concerning (the Qur&#x27;an) to make haste therewith.&#x27; (75.16) And whenever Gabriel descended to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) with the Divine Inspiration, Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) used to move his tongue and lips, and that used to be hard for him, and one could easily recognize that he was being inspired Divinely.
