<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Aisha:Allah, the brother of Abu Al-Qu&#x27;ais asked my permission to enter after the verses of Al-Hijab (veiling the ladies) was revealed, and I said, &quot;By Allah, I will not admit him unless I take permission of Allah&#x27;s Apostle for it was not the brother of Al-Qu&#x27;ais who had suckled me, but it was the wife of Al-Qu&#x27;ais, who had suckled me.&quot; Then Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) entered upon me, and I said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! The man has not nursed me but his wife has nursed me.&quot; He said, &quot;Admit him because he is your uncle (not from blood relation, but because you have been nursed by his wife), Taribat Yaminuki.&quot; `Urwa said, &quot;Because of this reason, &#x27; Aisha used to say: Foster suckling relations render all those things (marriages etc.) illegal which are illegal because of the corresponding blood relations.&quot; (See Hadith No. 36, Vol)</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Aisha:The Prophet (ﷺ) intended to return home after the performance of the Hajj, and he saw Safiya standing at the entrance of her tent, depressed and sad because she got her menses. <section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Sa`id Al-Khudri:While the Prophet (ﷺ) was distributing (war booty etc.) one day, Dhul Khawaisira, a man from the tribe of Bani Tamim, said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! Act justly.&quot; The Prophets said, &quot;Woe to you! Who else would act justly if I did not act justly?&quot; `Umar said (to the Prophet (ﷺ) ), &quot;Allow me to chop his neck off.&quot; The Prophet said, &quot;No, for he has companions (who are apparently so pious that) if anyone of (you compares his prayer with) their prayer, he will consider his prayer inferior to theirs, and similarly his fasting inferior to theirs, but they will desert Islam (go out of religion) as an arrow goes through the victim&#x27;s body (games etc.) in which case if its Nasl is examined nothing will be seen thereon, and if its Nady is examined, nothing will be seen thereon, and if its Qudhadh is examined, nothing will be seen thereon, for the arrow has gone out too fast even for the excretions and blood to smear over it. Such people will come out at the time of difference among the (Muslim) people and the sign by which they will be recognized, will be a man whose one of the two hands will look like the breast of a woman or a lump of flesh moving loosely.&quot; Abu Sa`id added, &quot;I testify that I heard that from the Prophet (ﷺ) and also testify that I was with `Ali when `Ali fought against those people. The man described by the Prophet was searched for among the killed, and was found, and he was exactly as the Prophet (ﷺ) had described him.&quot; (See Hadith No. 807, Vol)</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Ibn `Abbas:When the delegation of `Abdul Qais came to the Prophet, he said, &quot;Welcome, O the delegation who have come! We are a group from the tribe of Ar-Rabi`a, and between you and us there is the tribe of Mudar and we cannot come to you except in the sacred months.
