<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Wail: Abdullah (bin Mas&#x27;ud) said, &quot;Whoever took a false oath in order to grab somebody&#x27;s property will meet Allah while Allah will be angry with him.&quot; Allah revealed the following verse to confirm that:--&quot;Verily! Those who purchase a small gain at the cost of Allah&#x27;s covenant And their oaths...a painful torment.&quot; (3.77) Al-Ash&#x27;ath bin Qais came to us and asked as to what Abu Abdur-Rehman (i.e. Ibn Mas&#x27;ud) was telling you.&quot; We related the story to him. On that he said, &quot;He has told the truth. This verse was revealed about me. I had some dispute with another man regarding a well and we took the case before Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ). Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said (to me), &quot;Produce two witnesses (to support your claim); otherwise the defendant has the right to take an oath (to refute your claim).&#x27; I said, &#x27;The defendant would not mind to take a false oath.&quot; Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) then said, &#x27;Whoever took a false oath in order to grab someone else&#x27;s property will meet Allah, Allah will be angry with him.&#x27; Allah then revealed what Confirmed it.&quot; Al-Ash&#x27;ath then recited the following Verse:--&quot;Verily! Those who purchase a small gain at the cost of Allah&#x27;s covenant, And their oaths . . . (to) . . . they shall have a painful torment!&#x27; (3.77) (See Hadith No)</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Aisha:`Utba bin Abi Waqqas authorized his brother Sa`d bin Abi Waqqas to take the son of the slave-girl of Zam`a into his custody, telling him that the boy was his own (illegal) son.
