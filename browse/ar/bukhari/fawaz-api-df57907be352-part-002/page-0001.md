<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Abdullah bin `Amr:Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) was informed about my fasts, and he came to me and I spread for him a leather cushion stuffed with palm fibres, but he sat on the ground and the cushion remained between me and him, and then he said, &quot;Isn&#x27;t it sufficient for you to fast three days a month?&quot; I replied, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Apostle! (I can fast more).&quot; He said, &quot;Five?&quot; I replied, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! (I can fast more).&quot; He said, &quot;Seven?&quot; I replied, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! (I can fast more).&quot; He said, &quot;Nine (days per month)?&quot; I replied, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! (I can fast more)&quot; He said, &quot;Eleven (days per month)?&quot; And then the Prophet said, &quot;There is no fast superior to that of the Prophet David (ﷺ) it was for half of the year. So, fast on alternate days</p>
