<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Sa`id Al-Khudri:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;The (planet of) earth will be a bread on the Day of Resurrection, and The resistible (Allah) will topple turn it with His Hand like anyone of you topple turns a bread with his hands while (preparing the bread) for a journey, and that bread will be the entertainment for the people of Paradise.&quot; A man from the Jews came (to the Prophet) and said, &quot;May The Beneficent (Allah) bless you, O Abul Qasim! Shall I tell you of the entertainment of the people of Paradise on the Day of Resurrection?&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;Yes.&quot; The Jew said, &quot;The earth will be a bread,&quot; as the Prophet (ﷺ) had said. Thereupon the Prophet (ﷺ) looked at us and smiled till his premolar tooth became visible. Then the Jew further said, &quot;Shall I tell you of the udm (additional food taken with bread) they will have with the bread?&quot; He added, &quot;That will be Balam and Nun.&quot; The people asked, &quot;What is that?&quot; He said, &quot;It is an ox and a fish, and seventy thousand people will eat of the caudate lobe (i.e. extra lobe) of their livers</p>
