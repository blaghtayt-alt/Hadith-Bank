<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا عَمْرُو بْنُ عَلِيٍّ، حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبِي، عَنْ عَبَايَةَ بْنِ رِفَاعَةَ بْنِ رَافِعِ بْنِ خَدِيجٍ، عَنْ رَافِعِ بْنِ خَدِيجٍ، قَالَ قُلْتُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ إِنَّا لاَقُو الْعَدُوِّ غَدًا، وَلَيْسَتْ مَعَنَا مُدًى فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ اعْجَلْ أَوْ أَرِنْ مَا أَنْهَرَ الدَّمَ وَذُكِرَ اسْمُ اللَّهِ فَكُلْ، لَيْسَ السِّنَّ وَالظُّفُرَ، وَسَأُحَدِّثُكَ، أَمَّا السِّنُّ فَعَظْمٌ، وَأَمَّا الظُّفُرُ فَمُدَى الْحَبَشَةِ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ وَأَصَبْنَا نَهْبَ إِبِلٍ وَغَنَمٍ فَنَدَّ مِنْهَا بَعِيرٌ، فَرَمَاهُ رَجُلٌ بِسَهْمٍ فَحَبَسَهُ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏&quot;‏ إِنَّ لِهَذِهِ الإِبِلِ أَوَابِدَ كَأَوَابِدِ الْوَحْشِ، فَإِذَا غَلَبَكُمْ مِنْهَا شَىْءٌ، فَافْعَلُوا بِهِ هَكَذَا ‏&quot;‏‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Rafi` bin Khadij:I said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! We are going to face the enemy tomorrow and we do not have knives.&quot; He said, &quot;Hurry up (in killing the animal). If the killing tool causes blood to flow out, and if Allah&#x27;s Name is mentioned, eat (of the slaughtered animal). But do not slaughter with a tooth or a nail. I will tell you why: As for the tooth, it is a bone; and as for the nail, it is the knife of Ethiopians.&quot; Then we got some camels and sheep as war booty, and one of those camels ran away, whereupon a man shot it with an arrow and stopped it. Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said, &quot;Of these camels there are some which are as wild as wild beasts, so if one of them (runs away and) makes you tired, treat it in this manner</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Zahdam:We were in the company of Abu Musa Al-Ash`ari and there were friendly relations between us and this tribe of Jarm. Abu Musa was presented with a dish containing chicken. Among the people there was sitting a red-faced man who did not come near the food. We asked for mounts but he took an oath that he would not give us any mounts, and added, &#x27;I have nothing to mount you on&#x27; In the meantime some camels of booty were brought to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) and he asked twice, &#x27;Where are Al-Ash`ariyin?&quot; So he gave us five white camels with big humps. We stayed for a short while (after we had covered a little distance), and then I said to my companions, &quot;Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) has forgotten his oath. By Allah, if we do not remind Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) of his oath, we will never be successful.&quot; So we returned to the Prophet (ﷺ) and said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)!
