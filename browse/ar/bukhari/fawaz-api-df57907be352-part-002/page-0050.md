<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Anas bin Seereen:Ibn `Umar said: &quot;I divorced my wife while she was menstruating. `Umar mentioned that to the Prophet . The Prophet (ﷺ) said, (to my father), &quot;Let your son take her back.&quot; I asked (Ibn `Umar), &quot;Is such a divorce counted (i.e. as one legal divorce)?&quot; Ibn `Umar said, &quot;Of course.&quot; Narrated Yunus bin Jubair: Ibn `Umar said, &quot;The Prophet (ﷺ) said to `Umar, &#x27;Order him (Ibn `Umar) to take her back.&#x27; &quot; I asked, &quot;Is such a divorce counted (as one legal divorce)?&quot; Ibn `Umar said, &quot;What do you think if someone becomes helpless and foolish?</p>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو نُعَيْمٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ غَسِيلٍ، عَنْ حَمْزَةَ بْنِ أَبِي أُسَيْدٍ، عَنْ أَبِي أُسَيْدٍ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ خَرَجْنَا مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم حَتَّى انْطَلَقْنَا إِلَى حَائِطٍ يُقَالُ لَهُ الشَّوْطُ، حَتَّى انْتَهَيْنَا إِلَى حَائِطَيْنِ فَجَلَسْنَا بَيْنَهُمَا فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏&quot;‏ اجْلِسُوا هَا هُنَا ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ وَدَخَلَ وَقَدْ أُتِيَ بِالْجَوْنِيَّةِ، فَأُنْزِلَتْ فِي بَيْتٍ فِي نَخْلٍ فِي بَيْتٍ أُمَيْمَةُ بِنْتُ النُّعْمَانِ بْنِ شَرَاحِيلَ وَمَعَهَا دَايَتُهَا حَاضِنَةٌ لَهَا، فَلَمَّا دَخَلَ عَلَيْهَا النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ هَبِي نَفْسَكِ لِي ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ قَالَتْ وَهَلْ تَهَبُ الْمَلِكَةُ نَفْسَهَا لِلسُّوقَةِ‏.‏ قَالَ فَأَهْوَى بِيَدِهِ يَضَعُ يَدَهُ عَلَيْهَا لِتَسْكُنَ فَقَالَتْ أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ مِنْكَ‏.‏ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ قَدْ عُذْتِ بِمَعَاذٍ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ ثُمَّ خَرَجَ عَلَيْنَا، فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ يَا أَبَا أُسَيْدٍ اكْسُهَا رَازِقِيَّتَيْنِ وَأَلْحِقْهَا بِأَهْلِهَا‏&quot;‏‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Usaid:We went out with the Prophet (ﷺ) to a garden called Ash-Shaut till we reached two walls between which we sat down. The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;Sit here,&quot; and went in (the garden). The Jauniyya (a lady from Bani Jaun) had been brought and lodged in a house in a date-palm garden in the home of Umaima bint An- Nu`man bin Sharahil, and her wet nurse was with her. When the Prophet (ﷺ) entered upon her, he said to her, &quot;Give me yourself (in marriage) as a gift.&quot; She said, &quot;Can a princess give herself in marriage to an ordinary man?&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) raised his hand to pat her so that she might become tranquil. She said, &quot;I seek refuge with Allah from you.&quot; He said, &quot;You have sought refuge with One Who gives refuge. Then the Prophet (ﷺ) came out to us and said, &quot;O Abu Usaid! Give her two white linen dresses to wear and let her go back to her family</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Aisha:Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) was fond of honey and sweet edible things and (it was his habit) that after finishing the `Asr prayer he would visit his wives and stay with one of them at that time. Once he went to Hafsa, the daughter of `Umar and stayed with her more than usual. I got jealous and asked the reason for that.
