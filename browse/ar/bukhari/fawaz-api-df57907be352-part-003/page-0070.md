<p dir="ltr">Narrated Shaddad bin &#x27;Aus:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;The most superior way of asking for forgiveness from Allah is: &#x27;Allahumma anta Rabbi la ilaha illa anta. Khalaqtani wa ana `Abduka, wa ana &#x27;ala &#x27;ahdika wa Wa&#x27;dika mastata&#x27;tu abu&#x27;u Laka bi ni &#x27;matika wa abu&#x27;u Laka bidhanbi; faghfirli fa&#x27;innahu la yaghfiru-dh-dhunuba ill a ant a.
