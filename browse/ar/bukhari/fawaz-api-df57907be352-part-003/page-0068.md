<p dir="ltr">Narrated Ubaidullah bin Hafs:that `Umar bin Nafi` told him that Nafi`, Maula `Abdullah had heard `Umar saying, &quot;I heard Allah&#x27;s Apostle forbidding Al-Qaza&#x27;.&quot; &#x27;Ubaidullah added: I said, &quot;What is Al-Qaza&#x27;?&quot; &#x27;Ubaidullah pointed (towards his head) to show us and added, &quot;Nafi` said, &#x27;It is when a boy has his head shaved leaving a tuft of hair here and a tuft of hair there.&quot; Ubaidullah pointed towards his forehead and the sides of his head. &#x27;Ubaidullah was asked, &quot;Does this apply to both girls and boys?&quot; He said, &quot;I don&#x27;t know, but Nafi` said, &#x27;The boy.&#x27;&quot; &#x27;Ubaidullah added, &quot;I asked Nafi` again, and he said, &#x27;As for leaving hair on the temples and the back part of the boy&#x27;s head, there is no harm, but Al-Qaza&#x27; is to leave a tuft of hair on his forehead unshaved while there is no hair on the rest of his head, and also to leave hair on either side of his head</p>
