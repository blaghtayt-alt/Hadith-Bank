<p dir="ltr">Narrated Ibn &#x27;Abbas: The Prophet (ﷺ) used to invoke Allah at night, saying, &quot;O Allah: All the Praises are for You: You are the Lord of the Heavens and the Earth. O Allah! Forgive me my sins that I did in the past or will do in the future, and also the sins I did in secret or in public.
