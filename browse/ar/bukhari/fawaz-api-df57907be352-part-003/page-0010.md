<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدَانُ، أَخْبَرَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ، أَخْبَرَنَا مَعْمَرٌ، عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ، عَنْ خَارِجَةَ بْنِ زَيْدِ بْنِ ثَابِتٍ، عَنْ أُمِّ الْعَلاَء ِ ـ وَهْىَ امْرَأَةٌ مِنْ نِسَائِهِمْ بَايَعَتْ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ـ قَالَتْ طَارَ لَنَا عُثْمَانُ بْنُ مَظْعُونٍ فِي السُّكْنَى حِينَ اقْتَرَعَتِ الأَنْصَارُ عَلَى سُكْنَى الْمُهَاجِرِينَ، فَاشْتَكَى فَمَرَّضْنَاهُ حَتَّى تُوُفِّيَ، ثُمَّ جَعَلْنَاهُ فِي أَثْوَابِهِ فَدَخَلَ عَلَيْنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقُلْتُ رَحْمَةُ اللَّهِ عَلَيْكَ أَبَا السَّائِبِ، فَشَهَادَتِي عَلَيْكَ لَقَدْ أَكْرَمَكَ اللَّهُ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ وَمَا يُدْرِيكِ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ قُلْتُ لاَ أَدْرِي وَاللَّهِ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ أَمَّا هُوَ فَقَدْ جَاءَهُ الْيَقِينُ، إِنِّي لأَرْجُو لَهُ الْخَيْرَ مِنَ اللَّهِ، وَاللَّهِ مَا أَدْرِي وَأَنَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ مَا يُفْعَلُ بِي وَلاَ بِكُمْ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ قَالَتْ أُمُّ الْعَلاَءِ فَوَاللَّهِ لاَ أُزَكِّي أَحَدًا بَعْدَهُ‏.‏ قَالَتْ وَرَأَيْتُ لِعُثْمَانَ فِي النَّوْمِ عَيْنًا تَجْرِي، فَجِئْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَذَكَرْتُ ذَلِكَ لَهُ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ ذَاكِ عَمَلُهُ يَجْرِي لَهُ ‏&quot;‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Kharija bin Zaid bin Thabit:Um Al-`Ala an Ansari woman who had given the Pledge of allegiance to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said, &quot;`Uthman bin Maz&#x27;un came in our share when the Ansars drew lots to distribute the emigrants (to dwell) among themselves, He became sick and we looked after (nursed) him till he died. Then we shrouded him in his clothes. Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) came to us, I (addressing the dead body) said, &quot;May Allah&#x27;s Mercy be on you, O Aba As-Sa&#x27;ib! I testify that Allah has honored you.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &#x27;How do you know that?&#x27; I replied, &#x27;I do not know, by Allah.&#x27; He said, &#x27;As for him, death has come to him and I wish him all good from Allah. By Allah, though I am Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ), I neither know what will happen to me, nor to you.&#x27;&quot; Um Al-`Ala said, &quot;By Allah, I will never attest the righteousness of anybody after that.&quot; She added, &quot;Later I saw in a dream, a flowing spring for `Uthman. So I went to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) and mentioned that to him. He said, &#x27;That is (the symbol of) his good deeds (the reward for) which is going on for him</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Ibn `Umar:Men from the companions of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) used to see dreams during the lifetime of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) and they used to narrate those dreams to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ). Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) would interpret them as Allah wished. I was a young man and used to stay in the mosque before my wedlock. I said to myself, &quot;If there were any good in myself, I too would see what these people see.&quot; So when I went to bed one night, I said, &quot;O Allah! If you see any good in me, show me a good dream.&quot; So while I was in that state, there came to me (in a dream) two angels. In the hand of each of them, there was a mace of iron, and both of them were taking me to Hell, and I was between them, invoking Allah, &quot;O Allah! I seek refuge with You from Hell.&quot; Then I saw myself being confronted by another angel holding a mace of iron in his hand. He said to me, &quot;Do not be afraid, you will be an excellent man if you only pray more often.&quot; So they took me till they stopped me at the edge of Hell, and behold, it was built inside like a well and it had side posts like those of a well, and beside each post there was an angel carrying an iron mace. I saw therein many people hanging upside down with iron chains, and I recognized therein some men from the Quraish. Then (the angels) took me to the right side. I narrated this dream to (my sister) Hafsa and she told it to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ). Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said, &quot;No doubt, `Abdullah is a good man.&quot; (Nafi` said, &quot;Since then `Abdullah bin `Umar used to pray much)</p>
