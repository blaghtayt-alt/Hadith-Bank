<p dir="ltr">Narrated Sa`d:Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) came to visit me during my ailment which had been aggravated during Hajjat-al- Wada`. I said to him, &quot;You see how sick I am. I have much property but have no heir except my only daughter May I give two thirds of my property in charity?&quot;! He said, &quot;No.&quot; I said, &quot;Half of it?&quot; He said, &quot;No.&quot; I said &quot;One third?&quot; He said, &quot;One third is too much, for to leave your heirs rich is better than to leave them poor, begging of others.
