<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ بُكَيْرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا اللَّيْثُ، عَنْ عُقَيْلٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو سَلَمَةَ، أَنَّ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ نَهَى رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنِ الْوِصَالِ فَقَالَ لَهُ رِجَالٌ مِنَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ فَإِنَّكَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ تُوَاصِلُ‏.‏ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏&quot;‏ أَيُّكُمْ مِثْلِي إِنِّي أَبِيتُ يُطْعِمُنِي رَبِّي وَيَسْقِينِ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ فَلَمَّا أَبَوْا أَنْ يَنْتَهُوا عَنِ الْوِصَالِ وَاصَلَ بِهِمْ يَوْمًا ثُمَّ يَوْمًا ثُمَّ رَأَوُا الْهِلاَلَ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ لَوْ تَأَخَّرَ لَزِدْتُكُمْ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ كَالْمُنَكِّلِ بِهِمْ حِينَ أَبَوْا‏.‏ تَابَعَهُ شُعَيْبٌ وَيَحْيَى بْنُ سَعِيدٍ وَيُونُسُ عَنِ الزُّهْرِيِّ‏.‏ وَقَالَ عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ خَالِدٍ عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ عَنْ سَعِيدٍ عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ عَنِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم‏.‏</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) forbade Al-Wisal (fasting continuously for more than one day without taking any meals). A man from the Muslims said, &quot;But you do Al-Wisal, O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)!&quot; Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said, &quot;Who among you is similar to me? I sleep and my Lord makes me eat and drink.&quot; When the people refused to give up Al-Wisal, the Prophet (ﷺ) fasted along with them for one day, and did not break his fast but continued his fast for another day, and when they saw the crescent, the Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;If the crescent had not appeared, I would have made you continue your fast (for a third day),&quot; as if he wanted to punish them for they had refused to give up Al-Wisal</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Usama bin Zaid bin Haritha:Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) sent us (to fight) against Al-Huraqa (one of the sub-tribes) of Juhaina. We reached those people in the morning and defeated them. A man from the Ansar and I chased one of their men and when we attacked him, he said, &quot;None has the right to be worshipped but Allah.&quot; The Ansari refrained from killing him but I stabbed him with my spear till I killed him. When we reached (Medina), this news reached the Prophet. He said to me, &quot;O Usama! You killed him after he had said, &#x27;None has the right to be worshipped but Allah?&quot;&#x27; I said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! He said so in order to save himself.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;You killed him after he had said, &#x27;None has the right to be worshipped but Allah.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) kept on repeating that statement till I wished I had not been a Muslim before that day</p>
