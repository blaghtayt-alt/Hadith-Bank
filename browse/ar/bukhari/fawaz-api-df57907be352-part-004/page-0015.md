<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Aisha:When Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) was healthy, he used to say, &quot;No prophet dies till he is shown his place in Paradise, and then he is given the option (to live or die).&quot; So when death approached him(during his illness), and while his head was on my thigh, he became unconscious for a while, and when he recovered, he fixed his eyes on the ceiling and said, &quot;O Allah! (Let me join) the Highest Companions (see Qur&#x27;an 4:69),&quot; I said, &quot;So, he does not choose us.&quot; Then I realized that it was the application of the statement he used to relate to us when he was healthy. <h3>Bengali</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Anas bin Malik:The Prophet (ﷺ) said to Abu Talha, &quot;Choose one of your boys to serve me.&quot; So Abu Talha took me (to serve the Prophet (ﷺ) ) by giving me a ride behind him (on his camel).
