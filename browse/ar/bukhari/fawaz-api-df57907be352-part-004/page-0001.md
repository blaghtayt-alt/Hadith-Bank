<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Huraira:Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said, &quot;While a man was walking on a road. he became very thirsty. Allah thanked him for that deed and forgave him.&quot; The people asked, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)!
