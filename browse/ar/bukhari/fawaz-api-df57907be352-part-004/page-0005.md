<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Huraira:A man came to the Prophet (ﷺ) and said, &quot;I have been ruined for I have had sexual relation with my wife in Ramadan (while I was fasting)&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) said (to him), &quot;Manumit a slave.&quot; The man said, &quot; I cannot afford that.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;(Then) fast for two successive months continuously&quot;. The man said, &quot;I cannot do that.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;(Then) feed sixty poor persons.&quot; The man said, &quot;I have nothing (to feed them with).&quot; Then a big basket full of dates was brought to the Prophet. The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;Where is the questioner? Go and give this in charity.&quot; The man said, &quot;(Shall I give this in charity) to a poorer person than l? By Allah, there is no family in between these two mountains (of Medina) who are poorer than we.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) then smiled till his premolar teeth became visible, and said, &quot;Then (feed) your (family with it)</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Anas:A man came to the Prophet (ﷺ) on a Friday while he (the Prophet) was delivering a sermon at Medina, and said, &quot;There is lack of rain, so please invoke your Lord to bless us with the rain.&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) looked at the sky when no cloud could be detected. Then he invoked Allah for rain. Clouds started gathering together and it rained till the Medina valleys started flowing with water. It continued raining till the next Friday. Then that man (or some other man) stood up while the Prophet (ﷺ) was delivering the Friday sermon, and said, &quot;We are drowned; Please invoke your Lord to withhold it (rain) from us&quot; The Prophet smiled and said twice or thrice, &quot;O Allah! Please let it rain round about us and not upon us.&quot; The clouds started dispersing over Medina to the right and to the left, and it rained round about Medina and not upon Medina. <h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدٌ، حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ جَعْفَرٍ، أَخْبَرَنَا رَبِيعَةُ بْنُ أَبِي عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ يَزِيدَ، مَوْلَى الْمُنْبَعِثِ عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ خَالِدٍ الْجُهَنِيِّ، أَنَّ رَجُلاً، سَأَلَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنِ اللُّقَطَةِ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ عَرِّفْهَا سَنَةً، ثُمَّ اعْرِفْ وِكَاءَهَا وَعِفَاصَهَا، ثُمَّ اسْتَنْفِقْ بِهَا، فَإِنْ جَاءَ رَبُّهَا فَأَدِّهَا إِلَيْهِ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ قَالَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ فَضَالَّةُ الْغَنَمِ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ خُذْهَا، فَإِنَّمَا هِيَ لَكَ، أَوْ لأَخِيكَ، أَوْ لِلذِّئْبِ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ قَالَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ فَضَالَّةُ الإِبِلِ قَالَ فَغَضِبَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم حَتَّى احْمَرَّتْ وَجْنَتَاهُ ـ أَوِ احْمَرَّ وَجْهُهُ ـ ثُمَّ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ مَالَكَ وَلَهَا، مَعَهَا حِذَاؤُهَا وَسِقَاؤُهَا، حَتَّى يَلْقَاهَا رَبُّهَا ‏&quot;‏‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Zaid bin Khalid Al-Juhani:A man asked Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) about &quot;Al-Luqata&quot; (a lost fallen purse or a thing picked up by somebody). The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;You should announce it publicly for one year, and then remember and recognize the tying material of its container, and then you can spend it. If its owner came to you, then you should pay him its equivalent.&quot; The man said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! What about a lost sheep?&quot; The Prophet said, &quot;Take it because it is for you, for your brother, or for the wolf.&quot; The man again said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! What about a lost camel?&quot; Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) became very angry and furious and his cheeks became red (or his face became red), and he said, &quot;You have nothing to do with it (the camel) for it has its food and its water container with it till it meets its owner</p>
