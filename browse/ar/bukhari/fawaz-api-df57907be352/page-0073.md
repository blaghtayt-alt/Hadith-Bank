<p dir="ltr">Narrated An-Nu`man bin Bashir:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;The example of the person abiding by Allah&#x27;s order and restrictions in comparison to those who violate them is like the example of those persons who drew lots for their seats in a boat. Some of them got seats in the upper part, and the others in the lower. When the latter needed water, they had to go up to bring water (and that troubled the others), so they said, &#x27;Let us make a hole in our share of the ship (and get water) saving those who are above us from troubling them. So, if the people in the upper part left the others do what they had suggested, all the people of the ship would be destroyed, but if they prevented them, both parties would be safe</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih al Bukhari · 2493]</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Ibn `Abbas:The Prophet (along with his companions) reached Mecca in the morning of the fourth of Dhul-Hijja assuming Ihram for Hajj only. When this news reached the Prophet (ﷺ) he delivered a sermon and said, &quot;I have been informed that some peoples were saying so and so; By Allah I fear Allah more than you do, and am more obedient to Him than you. If I had known what I know now, I would not have brought the Hadi (sacrifice) with me and had the Hadi not been with me, I would have finished the Ihram.&quot; At that Suraqa bin Malik stood up and asked &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! <p dir="ltr">Narrated Abaya bin Rifaa:My grandfather, Rafi` bin Khadij said, &quot;We were in the valley of Dhul-Hulaifa of Tihama in the company of the Prophet (ﷺ) and had some camels and sheep (of the booty).
