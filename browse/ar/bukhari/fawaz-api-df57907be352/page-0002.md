<p dir="ltr">Narrated Ibn &#x27;Abbas: The Prophet (ﷺ) said: &quot;I was shown the Hell-fire and that the majority of its dwellers were women who were ungrateful.&quot; It was asked, &quot;Do they disbelieve in Allah?&quot; (or are they ungrateful to Allah?) He replied, &quot;They are ungrateful to their husbands and are ungrateful for the favors and the good (charitable deeds) done to them. If you have always been good (benevolent) to one of them and then she sees something in you (not of her liking), she will say, &#x27;I have never received any good from you</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Al-Ma&#x27;rur: At Ar-Rabadha I met Abu Dhar who was wearing a cloak, and his slave, too, was wearing a similar one. I asked about the reason for it. <h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Al-Bara&#x27; (bin &#x27;Azib): When the Prophet (ﷺ) came to Medina, he stayed first with his grandfathers or maternal uncles from Ansar. He offered his prayers facing Baitul-Maqdis (Jerusalem) for sixteen or seventeen months, but he wished that he could pray facing the Ka&#x27;ba (at Mecca). The first prayer which he offered facing the Ka&#x27;ba was the &#x27;Asr prayer in the company of some people. Then one of those who had offered that prayer with him came out and passed by some people in a mosque who were bowing during their prayers (facing Jerusalem). He said addressing them, &quot;By Allah, I testify that I have prayed with Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) facing Mecca (Ka&#x27;ba).&#x27; Hearing that, those people changed their direction towards the Ka&#x27;ba immediately. Jews and the people of the scriptures used to be pleased to see the Prophet (ﷺ) facing Jerusalem in prayers but when he changed his direction towards the Ka&#x27;ba, during the prayers, they disapproved of it. Al-Bara&#x27; added, &quot;Before we changed our direction towards the Ka&#x27;ba (Mecca) in prayers, some Muslims had died or had been killed and we did not know what to say about them (regarding their prayers.) Allah then revealed: And Allah would never make your faith (prayers) to be lost (i.e. the prayers of those Muslims were valid).&#x27; &quot; (2:)</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Jamra: I used to sit with Ibn &#x27;Abbas and he made me sit on his sitting place. He requested me to stay with him in order that he might give me a share from his property. So I stayed with him for two months. Once he told (me) that when the delegation of the tribe of &#x27;Abdul Qais came to the Prophet, the Prophet (ﷺ) asked them, &quot;Who are the people (i.e. you)? (Or) who are the delegate?&quot; They replied, &quot;We are from the tribe of Rabi&#x27;a.&quot; Then the Prophet (ﷺ) said to them, &quot;Welcome! O people (or O delegation of &#x27;Abdul Qais)! Neither will you have disgrace nor will you regret.&quot; They said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! We cannot come to you except in the sacred month and there is the infidel tribe of Mudar intervening between you and us. So please order us to do something good (religious deeds) so that we may inform our people whom we have left behind (at home), and that we may enter Paradise (by acting on them).&quot; Then they asked about drinks (what is legal and what is illegal). The Prophet (ﷺ) ordered them to do four things and forbade them from four things.
