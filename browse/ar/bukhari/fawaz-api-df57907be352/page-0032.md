<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Abdullah bin `Umar:&quot;I saw Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) delaying the Maghrib prayer till he offered it along with the `Isha&#x27; prayer whenever he was in a hurry during the journey.&quot; Salim narrated, &quot;Ibn `Umar used to do the same whenever he was in a hurry during the journey.&quot; And Salim added, &quot;Ibn `Umar used to pray the Maghrib and `Isha&#x27; prayers together in Al-Muzdalifa.&quot; Salim said, &quot;Ibn `Umar delayed the Maghrib prayer because at that time he heard the news of the death of his wife Safiya bint Abi `Ubaid. I said to him, &#x27;The prayer (is due).&#x27; He said, &#x27;Go on.&#x27; Again I said, &#x27;The prayer (is due).&#x27; He said, &#x27;Go on,&#x27; till we covered two or three miles. Then he got down, prayed and said, &#x27;I saw the Prophet (ﷺ) praying in this way, whenever he was in a hurry during the journey.&#x27; `Abdullah (bin `Umar) added, &quot;Whenever the Prophet was in a hurry, he used to delay the Maghrib prayer and then offer three rak`at (of the Maghrib) and perform Taslim, and after waiting for a short while, Iqama used to be pronounced for the `Isha&#x27; prayer when he would offer two rak`at and perform Taslim. He would never offer any optional prayer till the middle of the night (when he used to pray the Tahajjud)</p>
