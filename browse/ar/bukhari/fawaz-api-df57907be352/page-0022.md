<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Huraira:Once Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) prayed two rak`at (instead of four) and finished his prayer. Dhul-Yadain asked him whether the prayer had been reduced or whether he had forgotten. Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) asked the people whether Dhul-Yadain was telling the truth. The people replied in the affirmative. Then Allah&#x27;s Apostle stood up, offered the remaining two rak`at and then finished his prayer with Taslim and then said, &quot;Allahu Akbar.&quot; He followed it with two prostrations like ordinary prostrations or a bit longer</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Aisha:the mother of the faithful believers: Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) in his last illness said, &quot;Tell Abu Bakr to lead the people in the prayer.&quot; I said, &quot;If Abu Bakr stood in your place, he would not be able to make the people hear him owing to his weeping. So please order `Umar to lead the prayer.&quot; He said, &quot;Tell Abu Bakr to lead the people in the prayer.&quot; I said to Hafsa, &quot;Say to him, &#x27;Abu Bakr is a softhearted man and if he stood in your place he would not be able to make the people hear him owing to his weeping. So order `Umar to lead the people in the prayer.&#x27; &quot; Hafsa did so but Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said, &quot;Keep quiet. Verily you are the companions of (Prophet) Joseph.
