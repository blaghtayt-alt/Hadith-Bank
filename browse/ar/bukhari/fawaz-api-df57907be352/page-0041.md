<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Bakra:In the lifetime of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) the sun eclipsed and he went out dragging his clothes till he reached the Mosque. The people gathered around him and he led them and offered two rak`at. When the sun (eclipse) cleared, he said, &quot;The sun and the moon are two signs amongst the signs of Allah; they do not eclipse because of the death of someone, and so when an eclipse occurs, pray and invoke Allah till the eclipse is over.&quot; It happened that a son of the Prophet (ﷺ) called Ibrahim died on that day and the people were talking about that (saying that the eclipse was caused by his death)</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Aisha:The Prophet (ﷺ) recited (the Qur&#x27;an) aloud during the eclipse prayer and when he had finished the eclipse prayer he said the Takbir and bowed. When he stood straight from bowing he would say &quot;Sami &#x27;allahu liman hamidah Rabbana wa laka l-hamd.&quot; And he would again start reciting. In the eclipse prayer there are four bowing and four prostrations in two rak`at. Al-Auza&#x27;i and others said that they had heard Az-Zuhri from &#x27;Urwa from `Aisha saying, &quot;In the lifetime of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) the sun eclipsed, and he made a person to announce: &#x27;Prayer in congregation.&#x27; He led the prayer and performed four bowing and four prostrations in two rak`at.&quot; Narrated Al-Walid that `Abdur-Rahman bin Namir had informed him that he had heard the same. Ibn Shihab heard the same. Az-Zuhri said, &quot;I asked (&#x27;Urwa), &#x27;What did your brother `Abdullah bin Az-Zubair do? He prayed two rak`at (of the eclipse prayer) like the morning prayer, when he offered the (eclipse) prayer in Medina.&#x27; &#x27;Urwa replied that he had missed (i.e. did not pray according to) the Prophet&#x27;s tradition.&quot; Sulaiman bin Kathir and Sufyan bin Husain narrated from Az-Zuhri that the prayer for the eclipse used to be offered with loud recitation</p>
