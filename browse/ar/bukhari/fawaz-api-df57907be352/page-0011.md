<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Abdul `Aziz:Anas said, &#x27;When Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) invaded Khaibar, we offered the Fajr prayer there (early in the morning) when it was still dark. The Prophet (ﷺ) rode and Abu Talha rode too and I was riding behind Abu Talha. The Prophet (ﷺ) passed through the lane of Khaibar quickly and my knee was touching the thigh of the Prophet (ﷺ) . He uncovered his thigh and I saw the whiteness of the thigh of the Prophet. When he entered the town, he said, &#x27;Allahu Akbar! Khaibar is ruined. Whenever we approach near a (hostile) nation (to fight) then evil will be the morning of those who have been warned.&#x27; He repeated this thrice. The people came out for their jobs and some of them said, &#x27;Muhammad (has come).&#x27; (Some of our companions added, &quot;With his army.&quot;) We conquered Khaibar, took the captives, and the booty was collected. Dihya came and said, &#x27;O Allah&#x27;s Prophet! Give me a slave girl from the captives.&#x27; The Prophet said, &#x27;Go and take any slave girl.&#x27; He took Safiya bint Huyai. A man came to the Prophet (ﷺ) and said, &#x27;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! You gave Safiya bint Huyai to Dihya and she is the chief mistress of the tribes of Quraidha and An-Nadir and she befits none but you.&#x27; So the Prophet (ﷺ) said, &#x27;Bring him along with her.&#x27; So Dihya came with her and when the Prophet (ﷺ) saw her, he said to Dihya, &#x27;Take any slave girl other than her from the captives.&#x27; Anas added: The Prophet (ﷺ) then manumitted her and married her.&quot; Thabit asked Anas, &quot;O Abu Hamza! What did the Prophet (ﷺ) pay her (as Mahr)?&quot; He said, &quot;Her self was her Mahr for he manumitted her and then married her.&quot; Anas added, &quot;While on the way, Um Sulaim dressed her for marriage (ceremony) and at night she sent her as a bride to the Prophet (ﷺ) . So the Prophet was a bridegroom and he said, &#x27;Whoever has anything (food) should bring it.&#x27; He spread out a leather sheet (for the food) and some brought dates and others cooking butter. (I think he (Anas) mentioned As-Sawaq). So they prepared a dish of Hais (a kind of meal). And that was Walima (the marriage banquet) of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)</p>
