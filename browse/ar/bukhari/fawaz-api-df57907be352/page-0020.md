<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Anas:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;Whenever anyone of you offers his prayer he is speaking in private to his Lord. So he should not spit to his right but under his left foot.&quot; Qatada said, &quot;He should not spit in front of him but to his left or under his feet.&quot; And Shu`ba said, &quot;He should not spit in front of him, nor to his right but to his left or under his foot.&quot; Anas said: The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;He should neither spit in the direction of his Qibla nor to his right but to his left or under his foot</p>
