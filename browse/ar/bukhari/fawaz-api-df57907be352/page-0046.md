<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Kuraib Maula Ibn `Abbas:`Abdullah bin `Abbas said that he had passed a night in the house of Maimuna the mother of the faithful believers , who was his aunt. He said, &quot;I slept across the bed, and Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) along with his wife slept lengthwise. Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) slept till midnight or slightly before or after it. Then Allah&#x27;s Apostle woke up, sat, and removed the traces of sleep by rubbing his hands over his face. Then he recited the last ten verses of Surat-Al `Imran (2). Then he went towards a hanging leather watercontainer and performed a perfect ablution and then stood up for prayer.&quot; `Abdullah bin `Abbas added, &quot;I got up and did the same as Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) had done and then went and stood by his side. He offered two rak`at, then two rak`at, then two rak`at, then two rak`at, then two rak`at, then two rak`at and then offered one rak`a witr. Then he lay down till the Mu&#x27;adh-dhin came and then he prayed two light rak`at and went out and offered the early morning (Fajr) prayer</p>
