<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Abdullah bin Abu Qatada Al-Aslami:That his father said, &quot;One day I was sitting with some of the Prophet&#x27;s companions on the way to Mecca. I asked them if they could hand over to me the whip and the spear but they said, &#x27;No, by Allah, we shall not help you in that in any way.&#x27; I became angry and got down from the horse, picked up both the things and rode the horse again.
