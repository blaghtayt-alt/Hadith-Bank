<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Ibn Juraij:`Ata&#x27; said, &quot;Jabir bin `Abdullah said, &#x27;The Prophet (ﷺ) went out on the Day of `Id-ul-Fitr and offered the prayer before delivering the Khutba, Ata told me that during the early days of Ibn Az-Zubair, Ibn `Abbas had sent a message to him telling him that the Adhan for the `Id Prayer was never pronounced (in the life time of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)) and the Khutba used to be delivered after the prayer. Ata told me that Ibn `Abbas and Jabir bin `Abdullah, had said, &quot;There was no Adhan for the prayer of `Id-ul-Fitr and `Id-ul-Aqha.&quot; `Ata&#x27; said, &quot;I heard Jabir bin `Abdullah saying, &#x27;The Prophet (ﷺ) stood up and started with the prayer, and after it he delivered the Khutba. When the Prophet of Allah (ﷺ) finished (the Khutba), he went to the women and preached to them, while he was leaning on Bilal&#x27;s hand. Bilal was spreading his garment and the ladies were putting alms in it.&#x27; &quot; I said to Ata, &quot;Do you think it incumbent upon an Imam to go to the women and preach to them after finishing the prayer and Khutba?&quot; `Ata&#x27; said, &quot;No doubt it is incumbent on Imams to do so, and why should they not do so?</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Aiyub:Hafsa bint Seereen said, &quot;On Id we used to forbid our girls to go out for `Id prayer. A lady came and stayed at the palace of Bani Khalaf and I went to her. She said, &#x27;The husband of my sister took part in twelve holy battles along with the Prophet (ﷺ) and my sister was with her husband in six of them. My sister said that they used to nurse the sick and treat the wounded. Once she asked, &#x27;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)! If a woman has no veil, is there any harm if she does not come out (on `Id day)?&#x27; The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &#x27;Her companion should let her share her veil with her, and the women should participate in the good deeds and in the religious gatherings of the believers.&#x27; &quot; Hafsa added, &quot;When Um-`Atiya came, I went to her and asked her, &#x27;Did you hear anything about so-and-so?&#x27; Um-`Atiya said, &#x27;Yes, let my father be sacrificed for the Prophet (ﷺ). (And whenever she mentioned the name of the Prophet (ﷺ) she always used to say, &#x27;Let my father be sacrificed for him&#x27;). He said, &#x27;Virgin mature girls staying often screened (or said, &#x27;Mature girls and virgins staying often screened--Aiyub is not sure as which was right) and menstruating women should come out (on the `Id day). But the menstruating women should keep away from the Musalla. And all the women should participate in the good deeds and in the religious gatherings of the believers&#x27;.&quot; Hafsa said, &quot;On that I said to Um-`Atiya, &#x27;Also those who are menstruating?&#x27; &quot; Um-`Atiya replied, &quot;Yes. Do they not present themselves at `Arafat and elsewhere?</p>
