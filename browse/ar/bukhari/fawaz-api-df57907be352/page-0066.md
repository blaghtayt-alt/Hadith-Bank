<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدَانُ، قَالَ أَخْبَرَنِي أَبِي، عَنْ شُعْبَةَ، عَنْ قَيْسِ بْنِ مُسْلِمٍ، عَنْ طَارِقِ بْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ أَبِي مُوسَى ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ قَدِمْتُ عَلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَهُوَ بِالْبَطْحَاءِ‏.‏ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ أَحَجَجْتَ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ قُلْتُ نَعَمْ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ بِمَا أَهْلَلْتَ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ قُلْتُ لَبَّيْكَ بِإِهْلاَلٍ كَإِهْلاَلِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم‏.‏ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ أَحْسَنْتَ، انْطَلِقْ فَطُفْ بِالْبَيْتِ وَبِالصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةِ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ ثُمَّ أَتَيْتُ امْرَأَةً مِنْ نِسَاءِ بَنِي قَيْسٍ، فَفَلَتْ رَأْسِي، ثُمَّ أَهْلَلْتُ بِالْحَجِّ، فَكُنْتُ أُفْتِي بِهِ النَّاسَ، حَتَّى خِلاَفَةِ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ فَذَكَرْتُهُ لَهُ‏.‏ فَقَالَ إِنْ نَأْخُذْ بِكِتَابِ اللَّهِ فَإِنَّهُ يَأْمُرُنَا بِالتَّمَامِ، وَإِنْ نَأْخُذْ بِسُنَّةِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَإِنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم لَمْ يَحِلَّ حَتَّى بَلَغَ الْهَدْىُ مَحِلَّهُ‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Musa:I came upon Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) when he was at Al-Batha. He asked me, &quot;Have you intended to perform the Hajj?&quot; I replied in the affirmative. He asked, &quot;For what have you assumed Ihram?&quot; I replied,&quot; I have assumed Ihram with the same intention as that of the Prophet (ﷺ) .&quot; The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;You have done well! Go and perform Tawaf round the Ka`ba and between Safa and Marwa.&quot; Then I went to one of the women of Bani Qais and she took out lice from my head. Later, I assumed the Ihram for Hajj. So, I used to give this verdict to the people till the caliphate of `Umar. When I told him about it, he said, &quot;If we take (follow) the Holy Book, then it orders us to complete Hajj and `Umra (Hajj-at- Tamattu`) and if we follow the tradition of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) then Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) did not finish his Ihram till the Hadi had reached its destination (had been slaughtered). (i.e. Hajj-al-Qiran). (See Hadith No)</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih al Bukhari · 1724]</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Bakra:The Prophet (ﷺ) delivered to us a sermon on the Day of Nahr. He said, &quot;Do you know what is the day today?&quot; We said, &quot;Allah and His Apostle know better.&quot; He remained silent till we thought that he might give that day another name. He said, &quot;Isn&#x27;t it the Day of Nahr?&quot; We said, &quot;It is.&quot; He further asked, &quot;Which month is this?&quot; We said, &quot;Allah and His Apostle know better.&quot; He remained silent till we thought that he might give it another name. He then said, &quot;Isn&#x27;t it the month of Dhul-Hijja?&quot; We replied: &quot;Yes! It is.&quot; He further asked, &quot;What town is this?&quot; We replied, &quot;Allah and His Apostle know it better.&quot; He remained silent till we thought that he might give it another name. He then said, &quot;Isn&#x27;t it the forbidden (Sacred) town (of Mecca)?&quot; We said, &quot;Yes. It is.&quot; He said, &quot;No doubt, your blood and your properties are sacred to one another like the sanctity of this day of yours, in this month of yours, in this town of yours, till the day you meet your Lord. No doubt! Haven&#x27;t I conveyed Allah&#x27;s message to you? They said, &quot;Yes.&quot; He said, &quot;O Allah! Be witness. So it is incumbent upon those who are present to convey it (this information) to those who are absent because the informed one might comprehend it (what I have said) better than the present audience, who will convey it to him. Beware! Do not renegade (as) disbelievers after me by striking the necks (cutting the throats) of one another</p>
