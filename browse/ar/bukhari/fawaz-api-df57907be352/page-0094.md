<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Malik bin Sasaa:The Prophet (ﷺ) said, &quot;While I was at the House in a state midway between sleep and wakefulness, (an angel recognized me) as the man lying between two men. A golden tray full of wisdom and belief was brought to me and my body was cut open from the throat to the lower part of the `Abdomen and then my `Abdomen was washed with Zamzam water and (my heart was) filled with wisdom and belief. Al- Buraq, a white animal, smaller than a mule and bigger than a donkey was brought to me and I set out with Gabriel. When I reached the nearest heaven. Gabriel said to the heaven&#x27;s gate-keeper, &#x27;Open the gate.&#x27; The gatekeeper asked, &#x27;Who is it?&#x27; He said, &#x27;Gabriel.&#x27; The gate-keeper asked,&#x27; Who is accompanying you?&#x27; Gabriel said, &#x27;Muhammad.&#x27; The gate-keeper said, &#x27;Has he been called?&#x27; Gabriel said, &#x27;Yes.&#x27; Then it was said, &#x27;He is welcomed. What a wonderful visit his is!&#x27; Then I met Adam and greeted him and he said, &#x27;You are welcomed O son and a Prophet.&#x27; Then we ascended to the second heaven. It was asked, &#x27;Who is it?&#x27; Gabriel said, &#x27;Gabriel.&#x27; It was said, &#x27;Who is with you?&#x27; He said, &#x27;Muhammad.&#x27; It was asked, &#x27;Has he been sent for?&#x27; He said, &#x27;Yes.&#x27; It was said, &#x27;He is welcomed. What a wonderful visit his is!&quot; Then I met Jesus and Yahya (John) who said, &#x27;You are welcomed, O brother and a Prophet.&#x27; Then we ascended to the third heaven. It was asked, &#x27;Who is it?&#x27; Gabriel said, &#x27;Gabriel.&#x27; It was asked, &#x27;Who is with you? Gabriel said, &#x27;Muhammad.&#x27; It was asked, &#x27;Has he been sent for?&#x27; &#x27;Yes,&#x27; said Gabriel. It was said, &#x27;He is welcomed. What a wonderful visit his is!&#x27; (The Prophet (ﷺ) added:) There I met Joseph and greeted him, and he replied, &#x27;You are welcomed, O brother and a Prophet!&#x27; Then we ascended to the 4th heaven and again the same questions and answers were exchanged as in the previous heavens. There I met Idris and greeted him. He said, &#x27;You are welcomed O brother and Prophet.&#x27; Then we ascended to the 5th heaven and again the same questions and answers were exchanged as in previous heavens. there I met and greeted Aaron who said, &#x27;You are welcomed O brother and a Prophet&quot;. There I met and greeted Moses who said, &#x27;You are welcomed O brother and. a Prophet.&#x27; When I proceeded on, he started weeping and on being asked why he was weeping, he said, &#x27;O Lord!
