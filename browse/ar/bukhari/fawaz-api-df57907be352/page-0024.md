<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Jabir bin `Abdullah:Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said, &quot;Whoever after listening to the Adhan says, &#x27;Allahumma Rabba hadhihi-dda` watit-tammah, was-salatil qa&#x27;imah, ati Muhammadan al-wasilata wal-fadilah, wa b`ath-hu maqaman mahmudan-il-ladhi wa`adtahu&#x27; [O Allah!
