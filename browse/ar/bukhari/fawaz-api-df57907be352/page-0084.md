<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Sa`id Al-Khudri:Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) ascended the pulpit and said, &quot;Nothing worries me as to what will happen to you after me, except the temptation of worldly blessings which will be conferred on you.&quot; Then he mentioned the worldly pleasures.
