<p dir="ltr">Narrated Suwaid bin Al-Nu`man:In the year of the conquest of Khaibar I went with Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) till we reached Sahba, a place near Khaibar, where Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) offered the `Asr prayer and asked for food.
