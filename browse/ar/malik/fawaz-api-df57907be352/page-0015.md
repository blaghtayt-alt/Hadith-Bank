Al-Fadl began to look at her, and she at him, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, turned Fadl&#x27;s face away to the other side. The woman said, &#x27;Messenger of Allah, Allah&#x27;s making the hajj obligatory finds my father a very old man, unable to stay firm on his riding-beast. Can I do hajj for him?&#x27;, and he said, &#x27;Yes.&#x27; This was during the farewell hajj</p>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Nafi that when Abdullah ibn Umar set out for Makka during the troubles (between al-Hajjaj ibn Yusuf and Zubair ibn al-Awwam) he said, &quot;If I am blocked from going to the House we shall do what we did when we were with the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace,&quot; and he went into ihram for umra, because that was what the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, did in the year of al-Hudaybiya. But afterwards, he reconsidered his position and said, &quot;It is the same either way.&quot; After that he turned to his companions and said, &quot;It is the same either way. I call you to witness that I have decided in favour of hajj and umra together.&quot; He then got through to the House (without being stopped) and did one set of tawaf, which he considered to be enough for himself, and sacrificed an animal. </p>
