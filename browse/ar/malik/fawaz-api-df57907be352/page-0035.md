<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Nafi from Abdullah ibn Umar that Umar ibn al-Khattab saw a silk robe at the door of the mosque. He said, &quot;Messenger of Allah, would you buy this robe and wear it on jumua and when envoys come to you?&quot; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;Only a person who has no portion in the next world wears this.&quot; Then the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was brought some robes of the same material and gave Umar ibn al-Khattab one of the robes. Umar said, &quot;Messenger of Allah, do you clothe me in it when you said what you said about the robe of Utarid?&quot; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;I did not give it to you to wear.&quot; Umar gave it to a brother of his in Makka who was still an idolater</p>
