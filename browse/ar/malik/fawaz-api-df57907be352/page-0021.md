<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Malik related to me from Nafi from Abdullah ibn Umar that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;It is the duty of a muslim man who has something to be given as a bequest not to spend two nights without writing a will about it.&quot; Malik said, &quot;The generally agreed-on way of doing things in our community is that when the testator writes something in health or illness as a bequest, and it has freeing slaves or things other than that in it, he can alter it in any way he chooses, until he is on his deathbed. If he prefers to abandon a bequest or change it, he can do so unless he has made a slave mudabbar (to be freed after his death). If he has made him mudabbar, there is no way to change what he has made mudabbar. He is allowed to change his testament because the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;It is the duty of a muslim man who has something to be given as a bequest not to spend two nights without writing a will about it.&quot; Malik explained, &quot;Had the testator not been able to change his will nor what was mentioned in it about freeing slaves, each testator might withhold making bequests from his property, whether in freeing slaves or other than it. A man gives a bequest in his health and in his travelling.&quot; (i.e. he does not wait till his death bed ) . Malik summed up, &quot;The way of doing things in our community about which there is no dispute is that he can change whatever he likes of that except for the mudabbar</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Malik related to me from Ibn Shihab from Amir ibn Sad ibn Abi Waqqas that his father said, &quot;The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, came to me to treat me for a pain which became hard to bear in the year of the farewell hajj. I said, &#x27;Messenger of Allah, you can see how far the pain has reached me. I have property and only my daughter inherits from me. Shall I give two thirds of my property as sadaqa?&#x27; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &#x27;No.&#x27; I said, &#x27;Half?&#x27; He said, &#x27;No.&#x27; Then the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &#x27;A third, and a third is a lot. Leaving your heirs rich is better than leaving them poor to beg from people. You never spend anything on maintenance desiring the Face of Allah by it, but that you are rewarded for it, even what you appoint for your wife.&#x27; Sad said, &#x27;Messenger of Allah, will I be left here in Makka after my companions have departed for Madina?&#x27; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &#x27;If you are left behind, and do sound deeds you will increase your degree and elevation by them. Perhaps you will be left behind so that some people may benefit by you and others may be harmed by you. O Allah! complete their hijra for my companions, and do not turn them back on their heels. The unfortunate one is Said ibn Khawla.&#x27; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was distressed on his account for he had died at Makka.&quot; Yahya said that he heard Malik speak about a man who willed a third of his property to a man and said as well, &quot;My slave will serve so-and-so (another man) for as long as he lives, then he is free,&quot; then that was looked into, and the slave was found to be a third of the property of the deceased. Malik said, &quot;The service of the slave is evaluated. Then the two of them divide it between them. The one who was willed a third takes his third, as a share, and the one who was willed the service of the slave takes what was evaluated for him of the slave&#x27;s service. Each of them takes, from the service of the slave or from his wage if he has a wage, according to his share. If the one who was given the service of the slave for as long as he lived dies, then the slave is freed.&quot; Yahya said that he heard Malik speak about someone who willed his third and said &quot;So-and-so has such- and-such, and so-and-so has such-and-such,&quot; naming some of his property, and his heirs protested that it was more than a third.&quot; Malik said, &quot;The heirs then have an option between giving the beneficiaries their full bequests and taking the rest of the property of the deceased, or between dividing among the beneficiaries the third of the property of the deceased and surrendering to them their third. If they wish, their rights in it reach as far as they reach</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya said that he heard Malik say, &quot;The best of what I have heard about the testament of a pregnant woman and about what settlements she is permitted in her property is that the pregnant woman is like the sick person. When the illness is light, and one does not fear for the sick person, he does with his property what he likes. If the illness is such that his life is feared for, he can only dispose of a third of his estate.&quot; He said, &quot;It is the same with a woman who is pregnant. The beginning of pregnancy is good news and joy. It is not illness and no fear because Allah the Blessed, the Exalted, said in His Book, &#x27;We gave her good news of Ishaq and after Ishaq, Yaqub.&#x27; (Sura ll ayat 71). And He said, &#x27;She bore a light burden and passed by with it, but when she became heavy, they called upon Allah, their Lord, &quot;If you give us a good-doing son, we will be among the thankful.&quot; &#x27;(Sura 7 ayat 189). &quot;When a pregnant woman becomes heavy, she is only permitted to dispose of a third of her estate. The beginning of this restriction is after six months. Allah, the Blessed, the Exalted, said in His Book, &#x27;Mothers suckle their children for two complete years.&#x27; And He said, &#x27;his bearing and weaning are thirty months.&#x27; (Sura 2 ayat 233). &quot;When six months have passed for the pregnant woman from the day she conceived, she is only permitted to dispose of a third of her property.&quot; Yahya said that he heard Malik say, &quot;A man who is advancing in the row for battle, can only dispose of a third of his property. He is in the same position as a pregnant woman or an ill person who is feared for, as long as he is in that situation.&quot; Yahya said that he heard Malik say, &quot;This ayat is abrogated. It is the word of Allah, the Blessed, the Exalted, &#x27;If he leaves goods, the testament is for parents and kinsmen.&#x27; What came down about the division of the fixed shares of inheritance in the Book of Allah, the Mighty, the Exalted, abrogated it.&quot; Yahya said that he heard Malik say, &quot;The established sunna with us, in which there is no dispute, is that it is not permitted for a testator to make a bequest (in addition to the fixed share) in favour of an heir, unless the other heirs permit him. If some of them permit him and others refuse, he is allowed to diminish the share of those who have given their permission. Those who refuse take their full share from the inheritance. Yahya said that he heard Malik speak about an invalid who made a bequest and asked his heirs to give him permission to make a bequest when he was so ill that he only had command of a third of his property, and they gave him permission to leave some of his heirs more than his third. Malik said, &quot;They cannot revoke that. Had they been permitted to do so, every heir would have done that, and then, when the testator died, they would take that for themselves and prevent him from bequeathing his third and what was permitted to him with respect to his property.&quot; Malik said, &quot;If he asks permission of his heirs to grant a bequest to an heir while he is well and they give him permission, that is not binding on them. The heirs can rescind that if they wish. That is because when a man is well, he is entitled to all his property and can do what he wishes with it. If he wishes, he can spend all of it. He can spend it and give sadaqa with it or give it to whomever he likes. His asking permission of his heirs is permitted for the heirs, when they give him permission when authority over all his property is closed off from him and nothing outside of the third is permitted to him, and when they are more entitled to the two-thirds of his property than he is himself. That is when their permission becomes relevant. If he asks one of the heirs to give his inheritance to him when he is dying, and the heir agrees and then the dying man does not dispose of it at all, it is returned to the one who gave it unless the deceased said to him, &#x27;So-and-so - (one of his heirs) - is weak, and I would like you to give him your inheritance.&#x27; So he gives it to him. That is permitted when the deceased specified it for him.&quot; Malik said, &quot;When a man gives the dying man free use of his share of the inheritance, and the dying man distributes some of it and some remains, it is returned to the giver, after the man has died.&quot; Yahya said that he heard Malik speak about someone who made a bequest and mentioned that he had given one of his heirs something which he had not taken possession of, so the heirs refused to permit that. Malik said, &quot;That gift returns to the heirs as inheritance according to the Book of Allah because the deceased did not mean that to be taken out of the third and the heirs do not have a portion in the third (which the dying man is allowed to bequeath).&quot; Malik said from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father that an effeminate man was with Umm Salama, the wife of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. He said to Abdullah ibn Abi Umayya while the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was listening. &quot;Abdullah! If Allah grants you victory over Ta&#x27;if tomorrow, I will lead you to the daughter of Ghailan. She has four folds on her front and eight folds on her back.&quot; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;This sort of man should not enter freely with you.&quot; (It was customary to allow men with no sexual inclination to enter freely where there were women)</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Malik said in the case of an umm walad who injured someone, &quot;The blood-money of that injury is the responsibility of her master from his property, unless the blood-money of the injury is greater than the value of the umm walad. Her master does not have to pay more than her value. That is because when the master of a slave or slave-girl surrenders his slave or slave-girl for an injury which one of them has done, he does not owe any more than that, even if the blood-money is greater. As the master of the umm walad cannot surrender her because of the precedent of the sunna, when he pays her price, it is as if he had surrendered her. He does not have to pay more than that. This is the best of what I have heard about the matter. The master is not obliged to assume responsibility for more than an umm walad&#x27;s value because of her criminal action.&quot; Malik related to me from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar said, &quot;The Jews came to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and mentioned to him that a man and woman from among them had committed adultery. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, asked them, &#x27;What do you find in the Torah about stoning?&#x27; They said, &#x27;We make their wrong action known and flog them.&#x27; Abdullah ibn Salam said, &#x27;You have lied! <p dir="ltr">Malik related to me from Yahya ibn Said from Said ibn al-Musayyab that a man from the Aslam tribe came to Abu Bakr as-Siddiq and said to him, &quot;I have committed adultery.&quot; Abu Bakr said to him, &quot;Have you mentioned this to anyone else?&quot; He said, &quot;No.&quot; Abu Bakr said to him, &quot;Then cover it up with the veil of Allah. He told him the same as he had said to Abu Bakr, and Umar told him the same as Abu Bakr had said to him. His self was still not settled so he went to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and said to him, &quot;I have committed adultery,&quot; insistently. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, turned away from him three times. Each time the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, turned away from him until it became too much. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, questioned his family, &quot;Does he have an illness which affects his mind, or is he mad?&quot; They said, &quot;Messenger of Allah, by Allah, he is well.&quot; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;Unmarried or married?&quot; They said, &quot;Married, Messenger of Allah.&quot; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, gave the order and he was stoned</p>

<p dir="ltr">Malik related to me from Ibn Shihab from Ubaydullah ibn Abdullah ibn Utba ibn Masud that Abu Hurayra and Zayd ibn Khalid al-Juhani informed him that two men brought a dispute to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. One of them said, &quot;Messenger of Allah! Judge between us by the Book of Allah!&quot; The other said, and he was the wiser of the two, &quot;Yes, Messenger of Allah. Judge between us by the Book of Allah and give me permission to speak.&quot; He said, &quot;Speak.&quot; He said, &quot;My son was hired by this person and he committed fornication with his wife. He told me that my son deserved stoning, and I ransomed him for one hundred sheep and a slave-girl. Then I asked the people of knowledge and they told me that my son deserved to be flogged with one hundred lashes and exiled for a year, and they informed me that the woman deserved to be stoned.&quot; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;By him in whose Hand myself is, I will judge between you by the Book of Allah. As for your sheep and slave girl, they should be returned to you. Your son should have one hundred lashes and be exiled for a year.&quot; He ordered Unays al-Aslami to go to the wife of the other man and to stone her if she confessed .
