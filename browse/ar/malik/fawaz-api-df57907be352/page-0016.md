<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Yahya ibn Said from Abu Salih as-Samman from Abu Hurayra that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;Had I not been concerned for my community, I would have liked never to stay behind a raiding party going out in the way of Allah. However, I do not find the means to carry them to it, nor do they find anything on which to ride out and it is grievous for them that they should stay behind me. I would like to fight in the way of Allah and be killed, then brought to life so I could be killed and then brought to life so I could be killed</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Muwatta Malik · 997]</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Suhayl ibn Abi Salih from his father from Abu Hurayra that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;Whoever makes an oath and then sees that something else would be better than it, should do kaffara for his oath and do what is better.&quot; Yahya said that he heard Malik say, &quot;Anyone who says that he has a vow but does not mention the name of Allah, is still obliged to make the kaffara for an oath (if he breaks it)&quot;. If he breaks any of that oath, divorce is necessary, and there is no breaking of oath after that in whatever he does. There is only one oath to be broken in that.&quot; Malik said, &quot;What we do about a woman who makes a vow without her husband&#x27;s permission is that she is allowed to do so and she must fulfill it, if it only concerns her own person and will not harm her husband.
