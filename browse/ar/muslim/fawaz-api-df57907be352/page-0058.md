<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">A&#x27;isha, the wife of the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ), said that between the time when the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) finished the &#x27;Isha&#x27; prayer which is called &#x27;Atama by the people, he used to pray eleven rak&#x27;ahs, uttering the salutation at the end of every two rak&#x27;ahs, and observing the Witr with a single one. And when the Mu&#x27;adhdhin had finished the call (for the) dawn prayer and he saw the dawn clearly and the Mu&#x27;adhdhin had come to him, he stood up and prayed two short rak&#x27;ahs. Then he lay down on his right side till the Mu&#x27;adhdhin came to him for lqama. (This hadith has been narrated with the same chain of transmitters by Ibn Shihab, but in it no mention has been made of Iqama)</p>
