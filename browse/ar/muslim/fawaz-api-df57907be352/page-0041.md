<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abdullah b. Buhaina reported:The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) led us two rak&#x27;ahs of prayer in one of the (obligatory) prayers and then got up and did not sit. and the people stood up along with him. When he finished the prayer and we expected him to pronounce salutation. he said:&quot; Allah is Most Great&quot; while sitting and made two prostrations before salutation and then pronounced (the, final) salutation</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abdullah b. Buhaina al-Asadi, the ally of Abual-Muttalib, reported:The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) stood up in the noon prayer (though) he hadith sit (after the two rak&#x27;ahs). That was a compensation for he had forgotten to observe jalsa (after two rak&#x27;ahs)</p>
