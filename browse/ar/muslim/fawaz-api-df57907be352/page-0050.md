<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abdullah (b. Mas&#x27;ud) reported:I was in Hims when some of the people asked me to recite the Qur&#x27;an to them. So I recited Surah Yusuf to them. One of the persons among the people said: By Allah, this is not how it has been sent down. I said: Woe upon you! By Allah, I recited it to the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) and he said to me: You have (recited) it well. I was talking with him (the man who objected to my recitation) that I sensed the smell of wine from him. So I said to him. Do you drink wine and belie the Book (of Allah)? You would not depart till I would whip you. So I lashed him according to the prescribed punishment (for the offence of drinking wine)</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Muslim · 1870]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Uqba b. &#x27;Amir reported:When we were in Suffa, the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) came out and said: Which of you would like to go out every morning to Buthan or al-&#x27;Aqiq and bring two large she-camels without being guilty of sin or without severing the ties of kinship? We said: Messenger of Allah, we would like to do it. Upon this he said: Does not one of you go out in the morning to the mosque and teach or recite two verses from the Book of Allah. the Majestic and Glorious? <p dir="ltr">Ubayy b. Ka&#x27;b reported:I was in the mosque when a man entered and prayed and recited (the Qur&#x27;in) in a style to which I objected.
