<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yazid b. Ruman told on the authority of Salih b. Khawwat on the authority of one who prayed in time of danger with Allah&#x27;s Messenger (may peace he upon him) at the Battle of Dhat ar-Riqa&#x27; that a group formed a row and prayed along with him, and a group faced the enemy. He led the group which was along with him in a rak&#x27;ah, then remained standing while they finished the prayer by themselves. Then they departed and formed a row facing the enemy. Then the second group came and he led them in the remaining rak&#x27;ah, after which he remained seated while they finished the prayer themselves. He then led them in salutation</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Jabir reported:We went forward with the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) and when we reached Dhat ar-Riqa&#x27;, we came to a shady tree which -we left for him One of the polytheists came there and, seeing the sword of the Messenger (ﷺ) hanging by a free. took it up. drew it from the scabbard and said to the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ): Are you afraid of Me? He (the Holy Prophet) said: No. He again said: Who would protect you from me? He said: Allah will protect me from you. The Companions of the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) threatened him. He sheathed the sword and hung it up. Then call to prayer was made and he (the Holy Prophet) led a group in two rak&#x27;ah. Then (the members of this group) withdrew and he led the second group in two rak&#x27;ah. So the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) observed four rak&#x27;ah and people observed two rak&#x27;ah</p>
