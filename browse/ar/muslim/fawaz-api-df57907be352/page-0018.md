<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Anas b. Malik reported:While we were in the mosque with Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ), a desert Arab came and stood up and began to urinate in the mosque. The Companions of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said: Stop, stop, but the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: Don&#x27;t interrupt him; leave him alone.
