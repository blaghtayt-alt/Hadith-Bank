<p dir="ltr">Abdullah b. Ja&#x27;far reported that when Allah&#x27;s Messenger (may peace be, upon him) came back from a journey, the children of his family used to accord him welcome. <p dir="ltr">A&#x27;isha, the wife of Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ), reported that he used to say:Never a prophet dies in a state that he is not made to see his abode in Paradise, and then given a choice. &#x27;A&#x27;isha said that when Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) was about to leave the world, his head was over her thigh and he had fallen into swoon three times. (On hearing these words), I then said (to myself) He is not going to opt us and I remembered a hadith which he had narrated to us as he was healthy and in which he said: No prophet dies until he sees his abode in Paradise, he is then given a choice. &#x27;A&#x27;isha said: These were the last words which Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) spoke (the words are): O Allah, with companions on High</p>
