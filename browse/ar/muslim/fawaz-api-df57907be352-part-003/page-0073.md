<p dir="ltr">Abu Usaid Ansar reported:I bear witness to the fact that Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said: The best settlements of the Ansar are of those of Banu Najjar, then of Banu &#x27;Abu al-Aslihal and then of Banu Harith b. Khazraj, then of Banu Sa&#x27;ida and there is in every settlement of the Ansar good. Abu Salama reported that Abu Usaid said: Can I tell a Iie about Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)? And if I were a liar, I would have started with my tribe Banu Sa&#x27;ida. This was conveyed to Sa&#x27;d b. &#x27;Ubida and he found (rankling) in his mind and said: We have been left behind (in the sense) that we have been (mentioned) last of the four. He (Sa&#x27;d) sid: Saddle my pony so that I should go to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ). His nephew saw him and said: Are you going to contradict (the order of) precedence set by Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ), whereas Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) has the best knowledge of it? Is it not sufficient for you that you are the fourth amongst the four (best tribes of the Ansar)?
