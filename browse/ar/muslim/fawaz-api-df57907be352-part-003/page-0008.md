<p dir="ltr">It has been narrated on the authority of &#x27;Abd al-Rahman b. Shamasa al- Mahri who said:I was in the company of Maslama b. They will be worse than the people of pre-Islamic days. They will get whatever they ask of Allah. While we were yet sitting when &#x27;Uqba b. &#x27;Amir came, and Maslama said to him: &#x27;Uqba, listen to what &#x27;Abdullah says. &#x27;Uqba said: He knows better; so far as I am concerned, I heard the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) say: A group of people from my Umma will continue to fight in obedience to the Command of Allah, remaining dominant over their enemies. Those who will oppose them shall not do them any harm. They will remain ill this condition until the Hour overtakes them. (At this) &#x27;Abdullah said: Yes. Then Allah will raise a wild which will be fragrant like musk and whose touch will be like the touch of silk; (but) it will cause the death of all (faithful) persons, not leaving behind a single person with an iota of faith in his heart. Then only the worst of men will remain to be overwhelmed by the Hour</p>
