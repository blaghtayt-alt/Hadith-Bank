<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Khalid b. Walid reported that he visited Maimuna daughter of al-Harith with the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ), and she was the sister of his mother. She presented to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) the flesh of a lizard which Umm Hufaid daughter of al-Harith had brought from Najd, and she had been married to a person belonging to Banu Ja&#x27;far. It was the habit of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) not to eat anything until he knew what that was. The rest of the hadith is the same but with this (addition):&quot; Ibn al-Asamm narrated it from Maimuna and he was under her care</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yazid b. al-Asamm reported:A newly wedded person of Medina invited us to a wedding feast, and he served us thirteen lizards. There were those who ate it and those who abandoned it. I met Ibn &#x27;Abbas the next day, and informed him (about this) in the presence of many persons. Some of them said that the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) had observed: I neither eat it nor forbid (anyone) from eating it, nor declare it to be unlawful. Thereupon Ibn &#x27;Abbas said: Sad it is what you say! Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) has not been sent, but (to declare in clear words) the lawful and the unlawful (things). We were once with Allah&#x27;s Messenger (may peace be. upon him) as he was with Maimuna, and there were with him al-Fadl b. &#x27;Abbas, Khalid b. Walid and some women (also) when a tray of food containing flesh was presented to him. As Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) was about to eat that, Maimuna said: It is the flesh of the lizard. He withdrew his hand saying: That is the flesh which I never eat; but he said to them (those who were present there): You may eat. Al-Fadl ate out of that, so did Khalid b Walid, and the women. Maimuna (however) said: I do not eat anything but that which Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) eats</p>
