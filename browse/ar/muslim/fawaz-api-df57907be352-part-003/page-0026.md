<p dir="ltr">Abu Talha Ansari reported Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) as saying:Angels do not enter the house in which there is a picture or portraits. I came to &#x27;A&#x27;isha and said to her: This is a news that I have received that Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) had said: Angels do not enter the house in which there is a picture or a dog, (and further added) whether she had heard Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) making a mention of it. I took a carpet and screened the door with it. When he (the Holy Prophet) came back he saw that carpet and I perceived signs of disapproval on his face. He pulled it until it was torn or it was cut (into pieces) and he said: God has not commanded us to clothe stones and clay. We cut it (the curtain) and prepared two pillowa out of it by stuffing them with the fibre of date-palms and he (the Holy Prophet) did not find fault with it</p>

<p dir="ltr">Muslim b. Subaih reported:I was with Masriuq in the house which had the portrayals of Mary (hadrat Maryan). Thereupon Masriuq said: These are portraits of Kisra.
