<p dir="ltr">Urwa b. Zubair reported that &#x27;A&#x27;isha said to him:This news has reached me that &#x27;Abdullah b. &#x27;Amr al-&#x27;As would pass by us during the Hajj season, so you meet him and ask him (about religious matters) as he has acquired great knowledge from Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ). And amongst these the one he mentioned was that Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) said: Verily, Allah does not take away knowledge from people directly but he takes away the scholars and consequently takes away (knowledge) along with them and leaves amongst persons the ignorant as their leaders who deliver religious verdicts without (adequate) knowledge and themselves go astray and lead others astray. &#x27;Urwa said: When I narrated this to &#x27;A&#x27;isha, she deemed it too much (to believe) and thus showed reluctance to accept that (as perfectly true) and said to, &#x27;Urwa: Did he (&#x27;Abdullah b. &#x27;Amr) say to you that he had heard Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) as saying: (&#x27;Urwa had forgotten to ask this from &#x27;Abdullah b. &#x27;Amr). <section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Jarir b. Abdullah reported that some desert Arabs clad in woollen clothes came to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ). He saw them in sad plight as they had been hard pressed by need. He (the Holy Prophet) exhorted people to give charity, but they showed some reluctance until (signs) of anger could be seen on his face. Then a person from the Ansar came with a purse containing silver. Then came another person and then other persons followed them in succession until signs of happiness could be seen on his (sacred) face. Thereupon Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said:He who introduced some good practice in Islam which was followed after him (by people) he would be assured of reward like one who followed it, without their rewards being diminished in any respect. And he who introduced some evil practice in Islam which had been followed subsequently (by others), he would be required to bear the burden like that of one who followed this (evil practice) without their&#x27;s being diminished in any respect</p>
