<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Sa&#x27;id Al-Khudri reported Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) as saying:Avoid sitting on the paths. Thereupon Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said: If you have to sit at all, then fulfil the rights, of the path. They said: What are their rights? Thereupon he said: Keeping the eye downward (so that you may not stare at the women), refraining from doing some harm to the other and exchanging mutual greetings (saying as-Salamu &#x27;Alaikum to one another) and commanding the good and forbidding the evil</p>
