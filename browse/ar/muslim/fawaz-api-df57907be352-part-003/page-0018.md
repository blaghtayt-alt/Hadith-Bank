<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Salama b. Akwa&#x27; reported on the authority of his father that a person ate in the presence of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) with his left hand, whereupon he said:Eat with your right hand. He said: I cannot do that, whereupon he (the Holy Prophet) said: May you not be able to do that.
