<p dir="ltr">Abu Huraira reported that Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said:Verily Allah created the universe and when He had finished that, ties of relationship came forward and said This is the place for him who seeks refuge from severing (of blood-relationship). Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) then said: Recite if you like:&quot; But if you turn away you are sure to make mischief in the land and cut off the ties of kinship.
