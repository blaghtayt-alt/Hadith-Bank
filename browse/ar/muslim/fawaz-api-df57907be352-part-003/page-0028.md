<p dir="ltr">Abu Tufail &#x27;Amir b. Withila reported:I was in the company of &#x27;Ali b. Abi Talib, when a person came to him, and said: What was it that Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) told you in secret? Thereupon he (liadrat &#x27;All) was enraged and said: Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) did not tell me anything in secret that he hid from people, except that he told me four things. He said: Com- mader of Faithful, what are these?
