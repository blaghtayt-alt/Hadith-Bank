<p dir="ltr">Abu Sa&#x27;id Khudri reported Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) as saying:There would come to the people a time when a detachment would be sent for fighting in the cause of Allah and they would say: See, if you can find amongst them someone from amongst the Companions of Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ). Then a second detachment would be sent to them and they would say: Do you find amongst them one who had had the privilege of seeing the Companions of the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ)? -and the victory would be granted to them because of him. Then the third detachment would be sent and it would be said to them: See, if you find amongst them (who had had the honour of seeing one) who saw those who saw the Companions of Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ). Then the fourth detachment would be sent and it would be said to them: See it you find amongst them one who had the privilege (of seeing) one who saw those who saw those who saw the Companions of Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ), and a person would be found and they would be granted victory because of him</p>

Umar reported that Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) led us &#x27;Isha&#x27; prayer at the latter part of the night and when he had concluded it by salutations he stood up and said:Have you seen this night of yours?
