<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Huraira (Allah be pleased with him) reported that Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) deputed a person to collect revenue from Khaibar. He brought fine quality of dates, whereupon Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said:Are all the dates of Khaibar like this)? He said: No. We got one sa&#x27; (of fine dates) for two sa&#x27;s (of inferior dates), and (similarly) two sa&#x27;s for three sa&#x27;s. Thereupon Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said: Don&#x27;t do that rather sell the inferior quality of dates for dirhams (money), and then buy the superior quality with the help of dirhams</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Nadra reported:I asked Ibn Umar and Ibn Abbas (Allah be pleased with them) about the conversion of gold with gold but they did not find any harm in that. I was sitting in the company of Abd Sa&#x27;id al-Khudri (Allah be pleased with him) and asked him about this exchange, and he said: Whatever is addition is an&#x27; interest. I refused to accept it on account of their statement (statement of Ibn &#x27;Abbas and Ibn &#x27;Umar). He said: I am not narrating to you except what I heard from Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ). There came to him the owner of a date-palm with one sa&#x27; of fine dates, and the dates of Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) were of that colour. Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) said to him: Where did you get these dates? I went with two sa&#x27;s of (inferior dates) and bought one sa&#x27; of (these fine dates), for that is the prevailing price (of inferior dates) in the market and that is the price (of the fine quality of dates in the market), whereupon Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said: Woe be upon you! You have dealt in interest, when you decide to do it (i. e. exchange superior quality of dates for inferior quality) ; so you should sell your dates for another commodity (or currency) and then with the help of that commodity buy the dates you like. Abu Sa&#x27;ad said: When dates are exchanged for dates (with different qualities) there is the possibility (of the element of) interest (creeping into that) or when gold is exchanged for gold having different qualities. I subsequently came to Ibn &#x27;Umar and he forbade me (to do it), but I did not come to Ibn &#x27;Abbas; (Allah be pleased with them). He (the narrator) said: Abu as-Sahba&#x27; narrated to me: He asked Ibn Abbas (Allah be pleased with them) in Mecca, and he too disapproved of it</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Jabir b. &#x27;Abdullah (Allah be pleased with them) reported:I went on an expedition with Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ). He overtook me and I was on a water-carrying camel who had grown tired and did not walk (trot). He (the Holy Prophet) said to me: What is the matter with your camel? I said: It is sick. He (the Holy Prophet) stepped behind and drove it and prayed for it, and then it always moved ahead of other camels. He (then) said: How do you find your camel? I said: It is, by the grace of your prayer, all right. He said: Would you sell this (camel) to me? I felt shy (to say him,&quot; No&quot; ) as we had no other camel for carrying water, but (later on) I said: Yes, and to I sold it to him on the condition that (I would be permitted) to ride it until I reached Madina. I said to him: Allah&#x27;s Messenger, I am newly married, so I asked his permission (to go ahead of the caravan). He permitted me, and I reached Medina well in advance of other people, until I reached my destination. There my maternal uncle met me and asked me about the camel, and I told him what I had done with regard to it. He reproved me in this connection. He (Jabir) said: When I asked his permission (to go ahead of the caravan) Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) inquired of me whether I had married a virgin or a non-virgin. I said to him: I have married a non-virgin. He said: Why did you not marry a virgin who would have played with you and you would have played with her? I said to him: Allah&#x27;s Messenger, my father died (or he fell as a martyr), and I have small sisters to (look after), so I did not like the idea that I should marry a woman who is like them and thus be not able to teach them manners and look after them properly. So I have married a non-virgin so that she should be able to look after them and teach them manners, When Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) came to Medina, I went to him in the morning with the camel. He paid me its price and returned that (the camel) to me</p>
