<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Hisham b. Urwa reported on the authority of his father (Allah be pleased with him) that Arwa bint Uwais disputed with Sa&#x27;id b. Zaid that he had seized some of the land belonging to her. She brought this dispute before Marwan b. al-Hakam. Sa&#x27;id said:How could I take a part of her land, after what I heard from Allah&#x27;s Messenger (may peace be upon&#x27;him)? He (Marwan) said: What did you hear from Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)? He said: I heard Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) say: He who wrongly took a span of land would be made to wear around his neck seven earths. Marwan said: I do not ask any evidence from you after this. He (Sa&#x27;id) said: O Allah, make her blind if she has told a lie and kill her in her own land. He (the narrator) said: She did not die until she had lost her eyesight, and (one day) as she was walking in her land, she fell down into a pit and died</p>
