<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It has been reported on the authority of Nafi, that &#x27;Abdullah b. Umar paid a visit to Abdullah b. Muti&#x27; in the days (when atrocities were perpetrated on the People Of Medina) at Harra in the time of Yazid b. Mu&#x27;awiya. Ibn Muti&#x27; said:Place a pillow for Abu &#x27;Abd al-Rahman (family name of &#x27;Abdullah b. &#x27;Umar). But the latter said: I have not come to sit with you. I have come to you to tell you a tradition I heard from the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ). I heard him say: One who withdraws his band from obedience (to the Amir) will find no argument (in his defence) when he stands before Allah on the Day of Judgment, and one who dies without having bound himself by an oath of allegiance (to an Amir) will die the death of one belonging to the days of Jahillyya</p>

<p dir="ltr">It has been narrated on the authority of Auf b. Malik al-Ashja&#x27;i who said that he heard the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) say:The best of your rulers are those whom you love and who love you, upon whom you invoke God&#x27;s blessings and who invoke His blessing upon you. <p dir="ltr">It has been narrated on the authority of &#x27;A&#x27;isha, the wife of the Prophet (ﷺ). She said:When the believing women migrated (to Medina) and came to the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ), they would be tested in accordance with the following words of Allah. the Almighty and Exalted:&quot; O Prophet, when believing women come to thee to take the oath of fealty to thee that they will not associate in worship anything with God, that they will not steal. that, they will not commit adultery...&quot; to the end of the verse (lx. 62). Whoso from the believing women accepted these conditions and agreed to abide by them were considered to have offered themselves for swearing fealty. When they had (formally) declared their resolve to do so, the Messenger of Allah (may peace he upon him) would say to them: You may go. I have confirmed your fealty. By God, the hand of the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) never touched the hand of a woman. He would take the oath of fealty from them by oral declaration. By God, the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) never took any vow from women except that which God had ordered him to take, and his palm never touched the palm of a woman.
