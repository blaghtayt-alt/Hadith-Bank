<summary>English · notes and commentary</summary>

<p dir="ltr">Jabir (Allah be pleased with him) reported that a woman gave her garden as a life grant to her son. He died and later on she also died and left a son behind and brothers also, The sons of the woman making life grant said (to those who had been conferred upon this &#x27;Umra):This garden has returned to us. The sons of the one who had been given life grant said: This belonged to our father, during his lifetime and in case of his death. They took their dispute to Tariq, the freed slave of &#x27;Uthman. </p>

<p><a href="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/fawazahmed0/hadith-api@df57907be35291c91ad6a6691180e22ca9920784/editions/eng-muslim/4198.json" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Commentary source</a></p>

<p dir="ltr"> Tariq gave this decision and then wrote to Abd al-Malik and informed him, Jabir bearing witness to it. Abd al-Malik said: Jabir has told the truth. Then Tariq gave a decree and, as a result thereof, it is to this day that the garden belongs to descendants of one who was conferred upon the life grant</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Amir b. Sa&#x27;d reported on the authority of his father (Sa&#x27;d b. Abi Waqqas):Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) visited me in my illness which brought me near death in the year of Hajjat-ul-Wada&#x27; (Farewell Pilgrimage). I said: Allah&#x27;s Messenger, you can well see the pain with which I am afflicted and I am a man possessing wealth, and there is none to inherit me except only one daughter. Should I give two-thirds of my property as Sadaqa? He said: No. I said: Should I give half (of my property) as Sadaqa? He said: No. He (further) said: Give one-third (in charity) and that is quite enough. To leave your heirs rich is better than to leave them poor, begging from people; that you would never incur an expense seeking therewith the pleasure of Allah, but you would be rewarded therefor, even for a morsel of food that you put in the mouth of your wife. I said: Allah&#x27;s Messenger. would I survive my companions? He (the Holy Prophet) said: If you survive them, then do such a deed by means of which you seek the pleasure of Allah, but you would increase in your status (in religion) and prestige; you may survive so that people would benefit from you, and others would be harmed by you. (The Holy Prophet) further said: Allah, complete for my Companions their migration, and not cause them to turn back upon their heels. Sa&#x27;d b. Khaula is, however, unfortunate. Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) felt grief for him as he had died in Mecca</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Humaid b. &#x27;Abd al-Rahman al-Himyari reported from three of the sons of Sa&#x27;d all of whom reported from their father that Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) visited Sa&#x27;d as he was ill in Mecca. He (Sa&#x27;d) wept. He (the Holy Prophet) said:What makes you weep? He said: I am afraid I may die in the land from where I migrated as Sa&#x27;d b. Khaula had died. Thereupon Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) said: O Allah, grant health to Sa&#x27;d. O Allah, grant health to Sad. He repeated it three times. He (Sa&#x27;d) said: Allah&#x27;s Messenger, I own a large property and I have only one daughter as my inheritor. Should I not will away the whole of my property? He (the Holy Prophet) said: No. He said: (Should I not will away, ) two-thirds of the property? he (the Holy Prophet) said: No. He (Sa&#x27;d) (again) said: (Should I not will away) half (of my property)? He said: No. He (Sa&#x27;d) said: Then one-third? Thereupon he (the Holy Prophet) said: (Yes), one-third, and one-third is quite substanial. And what you spend as charity from your property is Sadaqa and flour spending on your family is also Sadaqa, and what your wife eats from your property is also Sadaqa, and that you leave your heirs well off (or he said: prospreous) is better than to leave them (poor and) begging from people. He (the Holy Prophet) pointed this with his hands</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Talha b. Musarrif reported:I asked &#x27;Abdullah b. Abu Aufa whether Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) had made any will (in regard to his property). He said: NO. I said: Then why has making of will been made necessary for the Muslims, or why were they commanded to make will? Thereupon he said: He made the will according to the Book of Allah, the Exalted and Majestic</p>
