<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا يَزِيدُ بْنُ هَارُونَ، أَخْبَرَنَا ابْنُ عَوْنٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ، سِيرِينَ عَنْ أَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ، قَالَ كَانَ ابْنٌ لأَبِي طَلْحَةَ يَشْتَكِي فَخَرَجَ أَبُو طَلْحَةَ فَقُبِضَ الصَّبِيُّ فَلَمَّا رَجَعَ أَبُو طَلْحَةَ قَالَ مَا فَعَلَ ابْنِي قَالَتْ أُمُّ سُلَيْمٍ هُوَ أَسْكَنُ مِمَّا كَانَ ‏.‏ فَقَرَّبَتْ إِلَيْهِ الْعَشَاءَ فَتَعَشَّى ثُمَّ أَصَابَ مِنْهَا فَلَمَّا فَرَغَ قَالَتْ وَارُوا الصَّبِيَّ ‏.‏ فَلَمَّا أَصْبَحَ أَبُو طَلْحَةَ أَتَى رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَأَخْبَرَهُ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ أَعْرَسْتُمُ اللَّيْلَةَ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ قَالَ نَعَمْ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ لَهُمَا ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَوَلَدَتْ غُلاَمًا فَقَالَ لِي أَبُو طَلْحَةَ احْمِلْهُ حَتَّى تَأْتِيَ بِهِ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏.‏ فَأَتَى بِهِ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَبَعَثَتْ مَعَهُ بِتَمَرَاتٍ فَأَخَذَهُ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ أَمَعَهُ شَىْءٌ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ قَالُوا نَعَمْ تَمَرَاتٌ ‏.‏ فَأَخَذَهَا النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَمَضَغَهَا ثُمَّ أَخَذَهَا مِنْ فِيهِ فَجَعَلَهَا فِي فِي الصَّبِيِّ ثُمَّ حَنَّكَهُ وَسَمَّاهُ عَبْدَ اللَّهِ ‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Anas b. Malik reported that the son of Abu Talha had been ailing. Abu Talha set out (on a journey) and his son breathed his last (in his absence). When Abu Talha came back, he said (to his wife):What about my child? Umm Sulaim (the wife of Abu Talha) said: He is now in a more comfortable state than before. She served him the evening meal and he took it. He then came to her (and had sexual intercourse with her) and when it was all over she said: Make arrangements for the burial of the child. When it was morning. Abu Talha came to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) and informed him, whereupon he said: Did you spend the night with her. He said: Yes. He (the Holy Prophet) then said: O Allah, bless both of them (and as a result of blessing) she gave birth to a child. Abu Talha said to me (Anas b. Malik) to take the child, (so I took him) and came to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ). She (Umm Sulaim) also had sent some dates (along with the child). Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) took him (the child) (in his lap) and said: Is there anything with you (for Tahnik). They (the Companions) said: Yes. Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) took hold of them (dates and chewed them). He then put them (the chewed dates) in the mouth of the child and then rubbed his palate and gave him the name of &#x27;Abdullah</p>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Musa Ash&#x27;ari reported that he went to &#x27;Umar b. Khattab and greeted him by saying:As-Salamu-&#x27;Alaikum, here is &#x27;Abdullah b. Qais, but he did not permit him (to get in). He (Abu Musa Ash&#x27;ari) again greeted him with as-Salamu-&#x27;Alaikum and said: Here is Abu Musa, but he (Hadrat &#x27;Umar) did not permit him (to get in). He again said: As-Salam-u-&#x27;Alaikum, (and said) here is Ash&#x27;ari, (then receiving no response he came back). He (Hadrat &#x27;Umar) said: Bring him back to me, bring him back to me So he went there (in the presence of Hadrat &#x27;Umar) and he said to him: Abu Musa, what made you go back, while we were busy in some work? He said: I heard Allah&#x27;s Messenger (may. peace be upon him) as saying: Permission should be sought thrice. And if you are permitted, (then get in), otherwise go back. He said: Bring witness to this fact, otherwise I shall do this and that, i. e. I shall punish you. Abu Musa went away and &#x27;Umar said to him (on his departure): It he (Abu Musa) finds a witness he should meet him by the side of the pulpit in the evening and it he does not find a witness you would not find him there. When it was evening he (Hadrat &#x27;Umar) found him (Abu Musa) there. He (Hadrat &#x27;Umar) said: Abu Musa, have you been able to find a witness to what you have said? He said: Yes. Here is Ubayy bin Ka&#x27;b, whereupon he (Hadrat &#x27;Umar) said: Yes, he is an authentic (witness). He (Hadrat &#x27;Umar) said: Abu Tufail (the kunya of Ubayy b. Ka&#x27;b), what does he (Abu Musa say? Thereupon he said: Ibn Khattab, I heard Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) as saying so. Do not prove to be a hard (task-master) for the Companions of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ), whereupon he Hadrat &#x27;Umar said: Hallowed be Allah. I had heard something (in this connection), but I wished it to be established (as an undeniable fact)</p>
