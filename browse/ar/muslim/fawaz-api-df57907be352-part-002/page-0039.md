<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">A&#x27;isha (Allah be pleased with her) reported that Aflah, the brother of Abu&#x27;l-Qu&#x27;ais, who was her uncle by reason of fosterage, came, and asked her permission (to enter the house) after seclusion (Hijab) was instituted. <h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">A&#x27;isha (Allah be pleased with her) reported that there came Aflah the brother, of Abu&#x27;l-Qu&#x27;ais, who sought her permission (to enter) after seclusion was instituted, and AbuQu&#x27;ais was the father of &#x27;A&#x27;isha by reason of fosterage. &#x27;A&#x27;isha said:By Allah, I would not permit Aflah unless I have solicited the opinion of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) for Abu Qulais has not suckled me, but his wife has given me suck. &#x27;A&#x27;isha&#x27; (Allah be pleased with her) said: When Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) entered, I said: Allah&#x27;s Messenger, Aflah is the brother of Abu&#x27;l-Qulais; he came to me to seek my permission for entering (the houst). I did not like the idea of granting him permission until I had solicited your opinion. Thereupon Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) said: Grant him permission. &#x27;Urwa said it was on account of this that &#x27;A&#x27;isha used to say. What is unlawful by reason of consanguinity is unlawful by reason of fosterage</p>
