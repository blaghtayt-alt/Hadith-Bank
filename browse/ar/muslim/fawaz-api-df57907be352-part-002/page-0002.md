<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It is narrated on the authority of Ibn Abu Laila that while Qais b. Sa&#x27;d and Sahl b. Hunaif were both in Qadislyya a bier passed by them and they both stood up. They were told that it was the bier of one of the people of the land (non-Muslim). They said that a bier passed before the Prophet (ﷺ) and he stood up. He was told that he (the dead man) was a Jew. Upon this he remarked:Was he not a human being or did he not have a soul? And in the hadith narrated by &#x27;Amr b. Murra with the same chain of transmitters, (the words) are:&quot; There passed a bier before us</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Muslim · 2225]</p>
