<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Bakra reported that (in the Farewell Address) Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) said:Time has completed a cycle and come to the state of the day when Allah created the heavens and the earth. The year is constituted of twelve months, of which four are sacred; three of them consecutive, viz. Dhu&#x27;l-Qa&#x27;da, Dhu&#x27;l- Hijja and Muharram, and also Rajab the month of Mudar which comes between Jumada and Sha&#x27;ban. He (the Holy Prophet) then said: which month is this? We said Allah and His Messenger know best. He (the narrator) said: He (the Holy Prophet) remained silent for some time until we thought that he would give it a name other than that (by which it was known). He said: Is it not Dha&#x27;l-Hijja? He (the Holy Prophet) said: Is it not the Balda (the city of Mecca)? We said: Yes. He said: What day is this? We said: Allah and His Messenger know best. He (the Holy Prophet) remained silent until we thought that he would give it another name. He said: Is it not the Day of Sacrifice? We said: Allah&#x27;s Messenger. yes. Thereupon he said: Your blood, your property (Muhammad, one of the narrators, said: I think, he also said this) and your honour are sacred to you like the sacredness of this day of yours, in this city of yours, and in this month of yours. Behold I let him who is present convey to him who is absent, for many a one whom a message is conveyed has a more retentive memory than one who hears. <p dir="ltr">Alqama b. Wa&#x27;il reported on the authority of his-father:While I was sitting in the company of Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ), a person came there dragging another one with the help of a strap and said: Allah&#x27;s Messenger, this man has killed my brother. Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said to him: Did you kill him? And the other man said: (In case he did not make a confession of this, I shall brine, a witness against him). He (the murderer) said: Yes, I have killed him. He (the Holy Prophet) said: Why did you kill him? He said: I and he won striking down the leaves of a tree and he abused me and enraged me, and to I struck his head with an axe and killed him, whereupon Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said: Have you anything with you to pay blood-wit on your behalf? He said: I do not possess any property but this robe of mine and this axe of mine. He (the Holy, Prophet) said: Do you think your people will pay ransom for you? He said: I am more insignificant among my people than this (that I would not be able to get this benefit from my tribe). He (the Holy Prophet) threw the strap towards him (the claimant of the blood-wit) saying: Take away your man. The man took him away, and as he returned, Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said: If he kills him, he will be like him. <h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">A&#x27;isha, the wife of Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ), reported that the Quraish were concerned about the woman who had committed theft during the lifetime of Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ), in the expedition of Victory (of Mecca). They said:Who would speak to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) about her? They (again) said: Who can dare do this but Usama b. Zaid, the loved one of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ)? She was brought to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) and Usama b. Zaid spoke about her to him (interceded on her behalf). The color of the face of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) changed, and he said: Do you intercede in one of the prescribed punishments of Allah? He (Usama) said: &#x27;Messenger of Allah, seek forgiveness for me.&#x27; When it was dusk. Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) stood up and gave an address. He (first) glorified Allah as He deserves, and then said: Now to our topic.
