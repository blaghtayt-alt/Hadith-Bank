<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Hammam b. Munabbih reported:This is what Abu Huraira (Allah be pleased with him) narrated to us from Allah&#x27;s Messenger (may peace he upon him). And he narrated many ahadith, and one was this: Allah&#x27;s Messenger (may peace be upon him said: I am, according to the Book of Allah, the Exalted and Majestic, nearest to the believers of all the human beings.
