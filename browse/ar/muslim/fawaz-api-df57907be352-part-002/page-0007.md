<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Adi b. Hatim (Allah be pleased with him) reported that when (this verse) was revealed:&quot; Until the white streak of the dawn becomes distinct from the dark streak&quot; (ii. 187) Adi b. Hatim said: Messenger of Allah, verily I keep underneath my pillow two strings, one white and the other black, by which I distinguish night from dawn.
