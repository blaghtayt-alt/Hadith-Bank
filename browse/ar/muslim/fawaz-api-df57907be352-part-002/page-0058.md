<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Nu&#x27;man b. Bashir reported that his mother bint Rawaha asked his (Nu&#x27;man&#x27;s) father about donating some gifts from his property to his son. He deferred the matter by one year, and then set forth to do that. She (Nu&#x27;man&#x27;s mother) said:I shall not be pleased unless you call Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) as witness to what you confer as a gift on your son. (Nu&#x27;man said): So father took hold of my hand and I was at that time a boy, and came to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ). and said: Allah&#x27;s Messenger, the mother of this son (of mine), daughter of Rawaha wishes that I should call you witness to what I confer as gift to her son. Allah&#x27;s Messenger (may pease be upon him) said: Bashir, have you any other son besides this (son of yours)? He said: Yes. He (the Holy Prophet) said: Have you given gifts to all of them like this? He said: No. Thereupon he (the Holy Prophet) said: Then call me not as witness, for I cannot be witness to an injustice</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Amir b. Sa&#x27;d reported on the authority of his father (Sa&#x27;d b. Abi Waqqas):Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) visited me in my illness which brought me near death in the year of Hajjat-ul-Wada&#x27; (Farewell Pilgrimage). I said: Allah&#x27;s Messenger, you can well see the pain with which I am afflicted and I am a man possessing wealth, and there is none to inherit me except only one daughter. Should I give two-thirds of my property as Sadaqa? He said: No. I said: Should I give half (of my property) as Sadaqa? He said: No. He (further) said: Give one-third (in charity) and that is quite enough. To leave your heirs rich is better than to leave them poor, begging from people; that you would never incur an expense seeking therewith the pleasure of Allah, but you would be rewarded therefor, even for a morsel of food that you put in the mouth of your wife. I said: Allah&#x27;s Messenger. would I survive my companions? He (the Holy Prophet) said: If you survive them, then do such a deed by means of which you seek the pleasure of Allah, but you would increase in your status (in religion) and prestige; you may survive so that people would benefit from you, and others would be harmed by you. (The Holy Prophet) further said: Allah, complete for my Companions their migration, and not cause them to turn back upon their heels. Sa&#x27;d b. Khaula is, however, unfortunate. Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) felt grief for him as he had died in Mecca</p>
