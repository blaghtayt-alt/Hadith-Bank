<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Urwa b. Zabair reported:I said to &#x27;A&#x27;isha, the wife of Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ): I do not see any (fault) in one who does not circumambl&quot; te between al-Safa&#x27; and al-Marwa, and I do not mind if I do not circumambulate between them, whereupon she said: O, the son of my sister, what you say is wrong. Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) observed Sa&#x27;i and so did the Muslims. So it is a Sunnah (of the Prophet). And it was a common practice (with the pagan Arabs) that those who pronounced Talbiya for the wretched al-Manat, situated at Mushalla, did not observe Sa&#x27;i between al-Safa&#x27; and al-Marwa. With the advent of Islam, we asked Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) about this practice, and (it was on this occasion) that Allah, the Exalted and Majestic, revealed this verse:&quot; Verily al-Safa&#x27; and al-Marwa are among the Signs of Allah&quot; ; so he who performed Hajj or &#x27;Umra it is no sin on him if he circumambulates them. And if it were as you state, (then the wording would have been):&quot; There is no harm for him, that he should not circumambulate round them.&quot; Zuhri said: I made a mention of that to Abu Bakr b. &#x27;Abd al- Rahman b. al-Harith b. Hisham; he was impressed by that and said: This is what is called knowledge. And I have heard many a scholar saying: Many of the Arabs who did not circumambulate between al-Safa&#x27; and al-Marwa caid: Our circumambulation between these two hills is an act of ignorance; whereas others among the Ansar said: We have been commanded to circumambulate the House, and not Commanded to run between al-Safa&#x27; and al-Marwa. So Allah, the Exalted and Majestic, revealed thia verse:&quot; Verily al-Safa&#x27; and al-Marwa are among the Signs of Allah.&quot; Abu Bakr b. &#x27;Abd al-Rahman said: I think that this (verse) has been revealed for such and such (persons)</p>
