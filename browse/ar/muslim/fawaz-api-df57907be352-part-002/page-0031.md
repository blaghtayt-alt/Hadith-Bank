<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abdullah, the freed slave of Asma&#x27; bint Abu Bakr (Allah be pleased with them), narrated that he used to hear Asma, &#x27; whenever she passed by Hajun, saying (these words):&quot; May there be peace and blessing of Allah upon His Messenger.&quot; We used to stay here along with him with light burdens. Few were our rides, and small were our provisions. I performed &#x27;Umra and so did my sister &#x27;A&#x27;isha, and Zubair and so and so. And as we touched the House (performed circumambulation and Sa&#x27;i) we put off Ihram, and then again put on Ihram in the afternoon for Hajj.</p>

When Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) and his Companions came in the state of Ihram for performing Hajj on the fourth (of Dhu&#x27;l-Hijja) he (Allah&#x27;s Apostle) commanded them to change their state of Ihram (from Hajj) to that of &#x27;Umra.
