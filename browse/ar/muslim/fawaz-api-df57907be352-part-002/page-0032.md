<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Jabir reported that a person came from Jaishan, a town of Yemen, and he asked Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) about the wine which was drunk in their land and which was prepared from millet and was called Mizr. Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) asked whether that was intoxicating. He said:Yes. Thereupon Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said: Every intoxicant is forbidden. Verily Allah the Exalted and Majestic, made a covenant to those who drank intoxicants to make their drink Tinat al-Khabal. They said: Allah&#x27;s Messenger, what is Tinat a]-Khabal? <h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Sahl b. Sa&#x27;d reported:An Arab woman was mentioned before Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ). He commanded Abu Usaid to send a message to her and he (accordingly) sent a message to her. She came and stayed in the fortresses of Banu Sa&#x27;idah. Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) went out until he came to her while she was (at that time) sitting with her head downcast. When Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) talked to her, she said: I seek refuge with Allah from you. Thereupon he said: I (have decided to) keep you away from me. They (the people near her) said: Do you know who he is? She said: No. They said: He is the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ). He came to you in order to give you the proposal of marriage. She said: Then I am the most unfortunate woman because of this (i. e. my defiance). Sahl said: Allah&#x27;s. Messenger (ﷺ) then set forth on that day until he sat in the Saqifa of Banu Sa&#x27;idah along with his Companions. He then said to Sahl: Serve us drink. He (Sahl) said: I brought out for them this bowl (containing drink) and served them this. Abu Hazim said: Sahl brought out this cup for us and we also drank from that. Then &#x27;Umar b. &#x27;Abd al-&#x27;Aziz asked him to give that (cup) as a gift to him and he gave (it to) him as a gift. In the narration of Abu Bakr b.
