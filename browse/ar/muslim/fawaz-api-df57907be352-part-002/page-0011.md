<p dir="rtl">[Sahih Muslim · 4328]</p>

<p dir="ltr">Sahl b. Abu Hathma and Rafi&#x27; b. Khadij reported that &#x27;Abdullah b. Sahl b. Zaid and Muhayyisa b. Mas&#x27;ud b. Zaid went out and as they reached Khaibar they were separated. Then Muhayyisa found &#x27;Abdullah b. Sahl having been killed. He buried him, and then came to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ). They were Huwayyisa b. Mas&#x27;ud and &#x27;Abd al-Rahman b. Sahl, and he (the latter one) was the youngest of the people (those three who had come to seek an interview with the Holy Prophet) began to talk before his Companions (had spoken). Thereupon Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said:The eldest one (eldest in regard to age should speak). So he kept quiet, and his companions (Muhayyisa and Huwayyisa) began to speak, and he (&#x27;Abd al Rahman) spoke along with them and they narrated to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) the murder of &#x27;Abdullah b. Sahl. Thereupon he said to them: Are you prepared to take fifty oaths so that you may be entitled (to blood-wit) of your companion (or your man who has murdered)? They said: How can we take an oath on a matter which we have not witnessed? He (the Holy Prophet) said: Then the Jews will exonerate themselves by fifty oaths. They said: How can we accept the oaths of people who are unbelievers? When Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) saw that, he himself paid his blood-wit</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Laila &#x27;Abdullah b. &#x27;Abd al-Rahman b. Sahl reported that the elderly persons of (the tribe) had informed Sahl b. Abu Hathma that &#x27;Abdullah b. Sahl had been killed, and (his dead body) had been thrown in a well or in a ditch. He came to the Jews and said:By Allah, it is you who have killed him. They said: By Allah, we have not killed him. He then came to his people, and made mention of that to them. Then came he and his brother Huwayyisa, and he was older than he, and &#x27;Abd al-Rahman b. Sahl. Then Muhayyisa went to speak, and it was he who had accompanied (&#x27;Abdullah) to Khaibar, whereupon Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said to Muhayyisa: Observe greatness of the great (he meant the seniority of age). Then Huwayyisa spoke and then Muhayyisa also spoke. Thereupon Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said: They should either pay blood-wit for your companion, or be prepared for war. Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) wrote about it to them (to the Jews). They wrote: Verily, by Allah, we have not killed him. Thereupon Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said to Huwayyisa and Muhayyisa and Abd al-Rahman: Are you prepared to take oath in order to entitle yourselves for the blood-wit of your companion? They said: No. He (the Holy Prophet) said: Then the Jews will take oath (of their innocence). They said: They are not Muslims.
