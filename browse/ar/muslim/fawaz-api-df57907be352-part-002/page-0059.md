<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Mus&#x27;ab b. Sa&#x27;d reported on the authority of his father. I was ailing. I sent message to Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) saying:Permit me to give away my property as I like. He refused. I (again) said: (Permit me) to give away half. He (again refused). I (again said): Then one-third. He (the Holy Prophet) observed silence after (I had asked permission to give away) one-third. He (the narrater) said: It was then that endowment of one-third became permissible</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Sa&#x27;id b. Jubair reported that Ibn &#x27;Abbas said:Thursday, (and then said): What is this Thursday? He then wept so much that his tears moistened the pebbles. I said: Ibn &#x27;Abbas, what is (significant) about Thursday? He (Ibn &#x27;Abbas) said: The illness of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) took a serious turn (on this day), and he said: Come to me, so that I should write for you a document that you may not go astray after me. They (the Companions around him) disputed, and it is not meet to dispute in the presence of the Apostle. They said: How is lie (Allah&#x27;s Apostle)? Has he lost his consciousness? Try to learn from him (this point). He (the Holy Prophet) said: Leave me. I am better in the state (than the one in which you are engaged). I make a will about three things: Turn out the polytheists from the territory of Arabia; show hospitality to the (foreign) delegations as I used to show them hospitality. He (the narrator) said: He (Ibn Abbas) kept silent on the third point, or he (the narrator) said: But I forgot that. This hadith was mentioned through another chain</p>

<p dir="ltr">Ibn Abbas reported:When Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) was about to leave this world, there were persons (around him) in his house, &#x27;Umar b. al-Kbattab being one of them. Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) said: Come, I may write for you a document; you would not go astray after that. Thereupon Umar said: Verily Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) is deeply afflicted with pain. You have the Qur&#x27;an with you. The Book of Allah is sufficient for us. Those who were present in the house differed. Some of them said: Bring him (the writing material) so that Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) may write a document for you and you would never go astray after him And some among them said what &#x27;Umar had (already) said. When they indulged in nonsense and began to dispute in the presence of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ), he said: Get up (and go away) &#x27;Ubaidullah said: Ibn Abbas used to say: There was a heavy loss, indeed a heavy loss, that, due to their dispute and noise.
