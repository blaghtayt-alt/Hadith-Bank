<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Tamim b. Tarafa reported:A beggar came to &#x27;Adi b. Hatim and he begged him to give him the price of a slave, or some portion of the price of the slave. He (&#x27;Adi) said: I have nothing to give you except my coat-of-mail and helmet. I will, however, write to my family to give that to you, but he did not agree to that. Thereupon &#x27;Adi was enraged, and said: By Allah, I will not give you anything. The person (then) agreed to accept that, whereupon he said: By Allah, had I not heard Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) saying:&quot; He who took an oath, but then found something more pious in the sight of Allah, he should (break the oath) and do that which is more pious,&quot; I would not have broken the oath (and thus paid you anything)</p>
