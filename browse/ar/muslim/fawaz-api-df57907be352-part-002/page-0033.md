<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Jabir reported Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) as saying:Cover vessels, waterskins, close the doors and extinguish the lamps, for the Satan does not loosen the waterskin, does not open the door and does not uncover the vessels. Qutaiba did not mention the closing of the doors in the hadith transmitted by him</p>
