<p dir="ltr">Shaqiq reported:We were sitting at the door of Abdullah (b. Mas&#x27;ud) waiting for him (to come out and deliver a sermon to us). It was at this time that there happened to pass by us Yazid b. Mu&#x27;awiya an-Nakha&#x27;i. We said: Inform him (&#x27;Abdullah b. Mas&#x27;ud) of our presence here. He went in and Abdullah b.
