<p dir="ltr">Mu&#x27;sab b. Sa&#x27;d reported on the authority of his father that a desert Arab came to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) and said to him:Teach me the words which I should (often) utter. Hallowed be Allah, the Lord of the worlds, there is no Might and Power but that of Allah, the All-Powerful and the Wise.&quot; He (that desert Arab) said: These all (glorify) my Lord. But what about me? Thereupon he (the Holy Prophet) said: You should say:&quot; O Allah, grant me pardon, have mercy upon me, direct me to righteousness and provide me sustenance.&quot; Musa (one of the narrators) said: I think he also said:&quot; Grant me safety.&quot; But I cannot say for certain whether he said this or not. <h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Huraira reported Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) as saying:He who alleviates the suffering of a brother out of the sufferings of the world, Allah would alleviate his suffering from the sufferings of the Day of Resurrection, and he who finds relief for one who is hard-pressed, Allah would make things easy for him in the Hereafter, and he who conceals (the faults) of a Muslim, Allah would conceal his faults in the world and in the Hereafter. Allah is at the back of a servant so long as the servant is at the back of his brother, and he who treads the path in search of knowledge, Allah would make that path easy, leading to Paradise for him and those persons who assemble in the house among the houses of Allah (mosques) and recite the Book of Allah and they learn and teach the Qur&#x27;an (among themselves) there would descend upon them tranquility and mercy would cover them and the angels would surround them and Allah mentions them in the presence of those near Him, and he who is slow-paced in doing good deeds, his (high) lineage does not make him go ahead</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Sa&#x27;id Khudri reported that Mu&#x27;awiya went to a circle in the mosque and said:What makes you sit here? They said: We are sitting here in order to remember Allah. He said: I adjure you by Allah (to tell me whether you are sitting here for this very purpose)? They said: By Allah, we are sitting here for this very purpose. Thereupon, he said: I have not demanded you to take an oath, because of any allegation against you and none of my rank in the eye of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) is the narrator of so few ahadith as I am. The fact is that Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) went out to the circle of his Companions and said: What makes you sit? They said: We are sitting here in order to remember Allah and to praise Him for He guided us to the path of Islam and He conferred favours upon us. Thereupon he adjured by Allah and asked if that only was the purpose of their sitting there. <p dir="ltr">Abu Musa reported:We were along with Allah&#x27;s Apostle (ﷺ) on a journey when the people began to pronounce &quot;Allahu Akbar&quot; in a loud voice. Thereupon Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) said: O people, show mercy to yourselves for you are not calling One who is deaf or absent. Verily, you are calling One who is All-Hearing (and) Near to you and is with you. Abu Musa said that he had been behind him (the Prophet) and reciting: &quot;There is neither might nor power but that of Allah.&quot; He (the Prophet), while addressing &#x27;Abdullah b.
