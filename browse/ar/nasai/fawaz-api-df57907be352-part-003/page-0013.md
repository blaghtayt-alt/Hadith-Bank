<p dir="ltr">Amr bin Shu&#x27;aib bin Muhammad bin &#x27;Abdullah bin &#x27;Amr (narrated) that his father and Zaid bin Aslam said:&quot;O Messenger of Allah! (What about) the Fara&#x27;?&quot; He said: &quot;It is a duty, but if you leave it (the animal) until it becomes half-grown and you load upon it (in Jihad) in the cause of Allah or give it to a widow, that is better than if you slaughter it (when it is just born) and its flesh is difficult to separate from its skin, then you turn your vessel upside down (because you will no longer be able to get milk from the mother) and you cause your she-camel to grieve (at the loss of its young).&quot; They said: &quot;O Messenger of Allah, (what about) the &#x27;Atirah?&quot; He said: &quot;The &#x27;Atirah is a duty.&quot; </p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Yahya bin Zurarah bin Karim bin Al-Harith bin &#x27;Amr Al-Bahili said:&quot;I heard my father say, that he heard his grandfather Al-Harith bin &#x27;Amr, that he met the Messenger of Allah during the Farewell Pilgrimage, when he was atop his slit-eared camel. (He said): &#x27;I said: O Messenger of Allah, May my father and mother be ransomed for you; pray for forgiveness for me. He said: May Allah forgive you (plural). Then I came to him from the other side, hoping that he would supplicate just for me alone, and not them. I said: O Messenger of Allah, pray for forgiveness for me. He said: May Allah forgive you (plural). Then a man among the people said: O Messenger of Allah, (what about) the &#x27;Atirah and Fara&#x27;? He said: Whoever wishes to offer and &#x27;Atirah may do so, and whoever does not wish to, may not. Whoever wishes to offer a Fara&#x27; may do so, and whoever does not wish to, may not. And with regard to sheep, a sacrifice should be offered. And he clasped between his fingers except for one</p>
