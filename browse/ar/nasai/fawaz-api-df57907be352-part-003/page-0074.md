<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from Anas that :The Prophet [SAW] used to say these supplications: &quot;Allahumma inni a&#x27;udhu bika min &#x27;ilmin la yanfa&#x27;u, wa qalbin la yakhsha&#x27;u, wa du&#x27;a&#x27;in la yasma&#x27;u, wa nafsin la tashba&#x27;u. (O Allah, I seek refuge with You from knowledge that is of no benefit, a heart that is not humble, a supplication that is not heard and a soul that is not satisfied.)&quot; Then he would say: &#x27;Allahumma inni a&#x27;udhu bika min ha&#x27;ula&#x27;il-arba&#x27; (O Allah, I seek refuge with You from these four)</p>
