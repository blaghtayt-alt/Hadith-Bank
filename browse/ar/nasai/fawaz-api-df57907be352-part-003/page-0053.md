<p dir="auto">মুহাম্মদ ইবন জাবালা (রহঃ) ... ইসহাক ইবন রাশিদ যুহরী থেকে, তিনি উরওয়া থেকে এবং তিনি আয়েশা (রাঃ) থেকে। মাখযুম গোত্রীয়া যে নারী চুরি করেছিল, তার ব্যাপারটা কুরায়শকে চিন্তিত করে তুলল (কেননা সে তাদের বংশের ছিল)। তারা বললোঃ এই মামলায় নবী সাল্লাল্লাহু আলাইহি ওয়াসাল্লাম এর নিকট কে কথা বলবে? লোক বললোঃ এই দুঃসাহস কে করতে পারে, উসামা ব্যতীত, যিনি তাঁর প্রিয় পাত্র। উসামা তাঁর নিকট কথা বললে তিনি বললেনঃ তোমাদের পূর্বের লোকেরা এজন্যই ধ্বংস হয়েছে যে, যখন তাদের কোন মর্যাদাবান লোক চুরি করতো, তারা তাকে ছেড়ে দিত। আর যখন কোন দুর্বল লোক চুরি করতো, তখন তারা তার উপর হদ জারী করতো। আল্লাহর কসম! ফাতিমা বিনতে মুহাম্মদও যদি চুরি করতো, তা হলে আমি তার হাতও কাটার নির্দেশ দিতাম।</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from &#x27;Aishah that:a woman stole at the time of the Messenger of Allah, during the Conquest, and she was brought to the Messenger of Allah. Usamah bin Zaid spoke to him concerning her. But when he spoke to him, the face of the Messenger of Allah changed color, and the Messenger of Allah said: &quot;Are you interceding concerning one of the Hadd punishment decreed by Allah?&quot; Isa,aj said to him: &quot;O Messenger of Allah ask Allah to forgive me!&quot; When evening came, the Messenger of Allah stood up and praised and glorified Allah, the mighty and sublime, as He deserves, then he said: &quot;The people who came before you were destroyed because whenever a noble person among them stole, they would let him go. But if one who was weak stole, they would carry out the Hadd punishment on him.&quot; Then he said: &quot;By the One in whose hand is my soul, if Fatimah bint Muhammad were to steal, I would cut off her hand</p>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Az-Zuhri said:&quot;Urwah bin Az-Zubair told me that a woman stole at the time of the Messenger of Allah, during the Conquest. Her people went to Uswamah bin Zaid, to ask him to intercede.&quot; &#x27;Urwah said: &quot;When Usamah spoke to him concerning her, the face of the Messenger of Allah changed color and he said: &#x27;Are you speaking to me concerning one of the Hadd punishments of Allah?&quot; Usamah said: &#x27;Pray to Allah for forgiveness for me, O Messenger of Allah.&#x27; When evening came, the Messenger of Allah stood up to deliver a speech. He praised Allah as He deserves, then he said: &#x27;The people who came before you were destroyed because, whenever a noble person among them stole, they would carry out the Hadd punishment on him. By the One in whose hand is my soul, if Fatimah bint Muhammad were to steal, I would cut off her hand.&#x27; Then the Messenger of Allah ordered that the hand of that woman be cut off. After that she repented sincerely, and &#x27;Aishah said: &#x27;She used to come to me after that, and I would convey her needs to the Messenger of Allah</p>
