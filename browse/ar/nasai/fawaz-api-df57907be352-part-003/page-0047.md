Then (it remained) on the hand of &#x27;Uthman for the first six years of his duties, but when he had to write many letters, he gave it to a man from among Ansar who used to seal letters with it. Then the Ansari went out to a well belonging to &#x27;Uthman and the ring fell. They looked for it but could not find it.
