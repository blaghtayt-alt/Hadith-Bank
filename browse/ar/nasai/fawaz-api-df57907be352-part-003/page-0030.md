<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from &#x27;Arm bin Shu&#x27;aib, from his father, from his grandfather, that:a man came to the Prophet and said: &quot;O Messenger of Allah, I have trained dogs; advise me concerning them.&quot; He said: &quot;Whatever your dogs catch for you, eat,&quot; I side: &quot;Even if they kill it?&quot; He said: &quot;Even if they kill it. &quot; He said: &quot;Advise me about my bow. &quot;He said: &quot;Whatever your arrow returns to you, eat.&quot; He said: &quot;Even if it gets away from you, so long as you do not find the mark of an arrow other than yours on it, or you find that it has gone rotten.&quot;(Another chain)</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Rafi bin Khadij said:&quot;While we were with the Messenger of Allah at Dhul-Hulaifah in Tihamanb, they acquired some camels and sheep (as spoils of war). The Messenger of Allah was among the last of the people, and the first of them hastened to slaughter (the animals) and set up pots (For cooking the meat). The Messenger of Allah came and ordered that the pots be came and ordered that the pots be overturned, then he divided it making ten sheep equivalent to one camel. While they were like that, a camel ran away. The people had only a few horses, so they went after fit and it and it got away from them. A man shot an arrow at it and stopped it. The Messenger of Allah said: &#x27;Some of these animals arte untamed like wild animals, so if one of them goes out of your control, do the same</p>
