<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Suhail narrated from his father,:That he heard Talhah bin &#x27;Ubaidullah say: &quot;A man from Najd with unkempt hair came to the Messenger of Allah [SAW]; he was speaking loudly but his speech could not be understood until he came close. He was asking about Islam. The Messenger of Allah [SAW] said: &#x27;Five prayers every day and night.&#x27; He said: &#x27;Do I have to do any more than that? He said: &#x27;No, not unless you do it voluntarily.&#x27; The Messenger of Allah [SAW] said: &#x27;Fasting the month of Ramadan.&#x27; He said: &#x27;Do I have to do any more than that?&#x27; He said: &#x27;No, not unless you do it voluntarily.&#x27; Then the Messenger of Allah [SAW] told him about Zakah. He said: &#x27;Do I have to do any more than that?&#x27; He said: &#x27;No, not unless you do it voluntarily.&#x27; The man left saying, &#x27;I will not do any more than that or any less.&#x27; The Messenger of Allah [SAW] said: &#x27;He will succeed, if he is telling the truth</p>
