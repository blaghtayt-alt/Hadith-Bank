<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from Abu Asma&#x27; Ar-Rahabi that Thawban, the freed slave of the Messenger of Allah [SAW], told him:&quot;Fatimah bint Hubairah came to the Messenger of Allah [SAW] with a big ring on her hand.&quot; He (the narrator) said: &quot;This is what I found in the book of my father, a huge ring.&quot;- &quot;The Messenger of Allah [SAW] started hitting her hand, so she entered upon Fatimah, the daughter of the Messenger of Allah [SAW], and complained to her about what the Messenger of Allah [SAW] had done. Fatimah took off a gold chain from her neck and said: &#x27;This was given to me by Abu Hasan.&#x27; The Messenger of Allah [SAW] came in and (ﷺ) the chain in her hand. He said: &#x27;O Fatimah, would you like the people to say that the daughter of the Messenger of Allah [SAW] has a chain of fire in her hand?&#x27; Then he went out, without sitting down. Fatimah sent the chain to the market and sold it, and she bought a slave with the money, and set him free. He was told of that and he said: &#x27;Praise be to Allah, Who has saved Fatimah from the Fire</p>
