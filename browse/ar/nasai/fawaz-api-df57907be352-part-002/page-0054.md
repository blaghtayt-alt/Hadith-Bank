<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from &#x27;Amr bin Shuaib, from his father, that his grandfather &#x27;Abdullah bin &#x27;Amr, that the Messenger of Allah wsa asked about fruit on the tree. He said:&quot;Whatever a needy person takes without putting any in his pocket (and taking it away), there is no penalty on him. But whoever takes anything away, he must pay a penalty of twice its value, and be punished. Whoever steals something after it has been stored properly, and its value is equal to that of a shield, his hand must be cut off. Whoever steals something worth less than that, he must pay a penalty of twice its value and be punished</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from &#x27;Amr bin Shuaib, from his father, that his grandfather &#x27;Abdullah bin &#x27;Amr, that a man from Muzainah came to the Messenger of Allah and said:&#x27;O Messenger of Allah, what do you think about a sheep stolen from the pasture?&quot; He said: &quot;(The thief must pay) double and be punished. There is no cutting off of the hand for (stealing) livestock, except what which has been put in the pen, if its value is equal to that of a shield, in which case the (thief&#x27;s) hand is to be cut off. If its value is not equal to that of a shield, then he should pay a penalty of twice its value and be flogged as a punishment.&quot; He said: &quot;O Messenger of Allah! What do you think about fruit on the tree?&quot; He said: &quot;(The thief must pay) double and be punished. There is no cutting off of the hand for (stealing) fruit on the tree, except for that which has been stored properly if its value is equal to that of a shield, in which case the (thief&#x27;s) hand is not equal to that of a shield, then he should pay a penalty of twice its value and be flogged as a punishment</p>
