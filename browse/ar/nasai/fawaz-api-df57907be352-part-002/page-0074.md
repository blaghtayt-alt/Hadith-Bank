<p dir="rtl">أَخْبَرَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ حُجْرٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا عُثْمَانُ بْنُ حِصْنٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنَا زَيْدُ بْنُ وَاقِدٍ، عَنْ خَالِدِ بْنِ حُسَيْنٍ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ، يَقُولُ عَلِمْتُ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم كَانَ يَصُومُ فِي بَعْضِ الأَيَّامِ الَّتِي كَانَ يَصُومُهَا فَتَحَيَّنْتُ فِطْرَهُ بِنَبِيذٍ صَنَعْتُهُ فِي دُبَّاءٍ فَلَمَّا كَانَ الْمَسَاءُ جِئْتُهُ أَحْمِلُهَا إِلَيْهِ فَقُلْتُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ إِنِّي قَدْ عَلِمْتُ أَنَّكَ تَصُومُ فِي هَذَا الْيَوْمِ فَتَحَيَّنْتُ فِطْرَكَ بِهَذَا النَّبِيذِ ‏.‏ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ أَدْنِهِ مِنِّي يَا أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَرَفَعْتُهُ إِلَيْهِ فَإِذَا هُوَ يَنِشُّ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ خُذْ هَذِهِ فَاضْرِبْ بِهَا الْحَائِطَ فَإِنَّ هَذَا شَرَابُ مَنْ لاَ يُؤْمِنُ بِاللَّهِ وَلاَ بِالْيَوْمِ الآخِرِ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ وَمِمَّا احْتَجُّوا بِهِ فِعْلُ عُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ رضى الله عنه ‏.‏</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Hurairah said:&quot;I knew that the Messenger of Allah [SAW] was fasting on certain days, so I prepared some Nabidh for him to break his fast, and made it in a gourd. When evening came I brought it to him, and said: &#x27;O Messenger of Allah, I knew that you were fasting today, so I prepared this Nabidh for you to break your fast.&#x27; He said: &#x27;Bring it to me, O Abu Hurairah.&#x27; I brought it to him, and it turned out to be something bubbling. He said: &#x27;Take this and throw it against the wall (throw it away), for this is the drink of one who does not believe in Allah or the Last Day</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from Jabir that:A man from (the tribe of) Jaishan, who are from Yemen, came and asked the Messenger of Allah [SAW] about a drink that they drank in his homeland that was made of corn and called Al-Mizr (beer). The Prophet [SAW] said to him: &quot;Is it an intoxicant?&quot; He said: &quot;Yes.&quot; The Messenger of Allah [SAW] said: &quot;Every intoxicant is unlawful. Allah, the Mighty and Sublime, has promised the one who drinks intoxicants that He will give him to drink from the mud of Khibal.&quot; They said: &quot;O Messenger of Allah, what is the mud of Khibal?&quot; He said: &quot;The sweat of the people of Hell,&quot; or he said: &quot;The juice of the people of Hell</p>
