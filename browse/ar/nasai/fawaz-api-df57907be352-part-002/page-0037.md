<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Rafi bin Khadij said:&quot;I said: &#x27;O Messenger of Allah, we are going to meet the enemy tomorrow, and we do not have any knives.&#x27; He said: If the blood is shed and the name of Allah is mentioned, then eat, unlike (it is slaughtered) with teeth or nails and I will tell you about that. As for teeth, they are bones, and as for nails, they are the knives of the Ethiopians,; We acquired some spoils of war including sheep or camels, and a camel ran away, so a man shot an arrow at it an stopped it. The Messenger of Allah said: &#x27;some of these animals&#x27; or &#x27;these camels&#x27;- &#x27;are untamed like wild animals, so if one of them goes out of your control, do the same.&quot; (Sahih)</p>
