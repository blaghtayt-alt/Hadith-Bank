<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Hurairah narrated that:The Messenger of Allah [SAW] said: &quot;There were two woman who had two children, and the wolf came and took away the son of one of them. She said to her companion: &#x27;It took away your son.&#x27; The other one said: &#x27;No, it took away your son.&#x27; They referred the matter to Dawud, peace be upon him, for judgment (about the remaining child) and he ruled in favor of the older one. Then they went out to Sulaiman bin Dawud and told him (about that). He said: &#x27;Give me a knife and I will cut him in half (to be shared) between you.&#x27; The younger one said: &#x27;Do not do that, may Allah have mercy on you; he is her son.&#x27; So he ruled that (the child) belonged to the younger woman.&quot; Abu Hurairah said: &quot;By Allah! I never heard &#x27;Sikkin&#x27; used until that day. We would only say: &#x27;Mudyah</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Abu Hurairah, Zaid bin Khalid and Shibl said:&quot;We were with the Prophet [SAW] when a man stood up and said: &#x27;I adjure you, by Allah, pass judgment between us according to the Book of Allah.&#x27; His opponent, who was wiser than him, stood up and said: &#x27;He is right, pass judgment between us according to the Book of Allah.&#x27; He said: &#x27;Speak.&#x27; He said: &#x27;My son was a laborer serving this man, and he committed Zina with his wife. I ransomed him with one hundred sheep and a servant.&#x27; It is as if he was told that his son was to be stoned to death but he ransomed him from that. &#x27;Then I asked some knowledgeable men and they told me that my son was to be given one hundred lashes and exiled for a year.&#x27; The Messenger of Allah [SAW] said to him: &#x27;By the One in Whose hand is my soul, I will pass judgment between you according to the Book of Allah, the Mighty and Sublime. As for the one hundred sheep and the servant, take them back, and your son is to be given one hundred lashes and exiled for a year. O Unais, go tomorrow to the wife of this man and if she confesses, then stone her to death.&#x27; She did confess, so he stoned her to death</p>
