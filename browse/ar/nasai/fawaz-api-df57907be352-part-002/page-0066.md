<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Ka&#x27;b bin Malik narrated the Hadith about when he stayed behind, and did not join the Messenger of Allah on the expedition to Tabuk. He told the story, and said:&quot;The envoy of the Messenger of Allah came to me and said: &#x27;The Messenger of Allah commands you to stay away from your wife.&#x27; I said: &#x27;Shall I divorce her or what?&#x27; He said: &#x27;No, just keep away from her and do not approach her.&#x27; I said to my wife: &#x27;Go to your family and stay with them until Allah, the Mighty and Sublime, decides concerning this matter</p>
