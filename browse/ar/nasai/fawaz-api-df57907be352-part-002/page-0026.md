<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from Umm Salamah that the wives of the Prophet asked her to speak to the Prophet and tell him, that the people were trying to bring their gifts to him when it was &#x27;Aishah&#x27;s day, and to say to him:&quot;We love good things as much as &#x27;Aishah does.&quot; So she spoke to him, but he did not reply her. When her turn came again, she spoke to him again, but he did not reply her. They said to her: &quot;How did he respond?&quot; She said: &quot;He did not answer me.&quot; They said: &quot;Do not leave him alone until he answers you or you comprehend what he says.&quot; When her turn came again, she spoke to him and he said: &#x27;Do not bother me about &#x27;Aishah, for the Revelation has never come to me under the blanket of any of you apart from the blanket of &#x27;Aishah</p>

Then he left and I left, he hurried and I hurried, he ran and I ran, and I got there before him and entered (the house). <h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Aishah said:&quot;Shall I not tell you about the Prophet and I?&quot; We said: &quot;Yes.&quot; She said: &quot;When it was my night when he&quot; -meaning the Prophet- &quot;was with me, he came in, placed his shoes by his feet, lay down his Rida&#x27; (upper garment), and spread the edge of his Izar (lower garment) on his bed. As soon as he thought that I had gone to sleep, he put his shoes on slowly, and picked up his Rida&#x27; slowly. Then he opened the door slowly, went out and shut it slowly. I put my garment over my head, covered myself and put on my Izar (lower garment), and I set out after him until he came to Al-Baqi&#x27;, raised his hands three times and stood there for a long time. Then he left and I left, he hurried and I hurried, he ran and I ran, and I got there before him and entered (the house). I had only just laid down when he came in and said: &#x27;O &#x27;Aishah, why are you out of breath?&#x27; She said: &#x27;No.&#x27; He said: &#x27;Either you tell me or Allah, the All-Aware, All-Knowing, will tell me.&#x27; I said: &#x27;O Messenger of Allah, may my father and mother be sacrificed for you;&#x27; and I told him the story. He said: &#x27;You were the black shape I saw in front of me?&#x27; I said: &#x27;Yes.&#x27;&quot; She said: &quot;He gave me a shove in the chest that hurt me and said: &#x27;You thought that Allah and His Messenger would be unfair to you.&#x27; She said: &#x27;Whatever people conceal, Allah knows it.&#x27; He said: &#x27;Yes.&#x27; He said: &#x27;Jibril came to me when you saw (me leave) but he did not enter upon you because you have taken off your garments.
