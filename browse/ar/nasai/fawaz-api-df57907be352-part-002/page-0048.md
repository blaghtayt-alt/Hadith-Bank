<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from &#x27;Amr bin Shu&#x27;aib, from his father, from his grandfather, that:the younger son of Muhayysah was found slain one morning at the gate of one morning at the gates of Khaibar. The Messenger of Allah said: &quot;Bring two witnesses to (say) who killed him, and he will hand him over to you.&quot; He said: &quot;O Messenger of Allah, where shall I get two witnesses? He was found slain in the morning at their gates.&quot; He said: &quot;Will you swear fifty oaths?&quot; He said: &quot;O Messenger of Allah, how can I swear concerning something I do not know?&quot; The Messenger of Allah said: &quot;Then will you accept fifty oaths from them?&quot; He said: &quot;O Messenger of Allah, how can we accept their oaths when they are Jews?&quot; So the Messenger of Allah told them (the Jews) to pay the Diyah and he would help them with half</p>
