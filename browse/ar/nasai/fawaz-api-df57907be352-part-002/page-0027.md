<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Anas said:&quot;Some people from &#x27;Uraynah came to the Messenger of Allah [SAW] and the Messenger of Allah [SAW] said to them: &#x27;Why don&#x27;t you go out to our camels and stay with them and drink their milk and urine?&#x27; So they did that, and when they recovered, they went to the herdsman of the Messenger of Allah [SAW] and killed him, reverted to being disbelievers, and drove off the camels of the Prophet [SAW]. He sent (men) after them, and they were brought to him. He had their hands and feet cut off, and their eyes gouged out</p>
