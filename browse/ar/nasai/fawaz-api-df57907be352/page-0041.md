<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from &#x27;Abdullah bin &#x27;Umar that :&#x27;Umar bin al-Khattab saw a Hullah and said: &quot;O Messenger of Allah (ﷺ), why don&#x27;t you buy this and wear it on Fridays and when meeting the delegations when they come to you?&quot; The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: &quot;This is worn by one who has no share in the Hereafter.&quot; Then something similar was brought to the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) and he gave a Hullah to &#x27;Umar from it. &#x27;Umar said: &quot;O Messenger of Allah (ﷺ), have you given me this when you said what you said about the Hullah of &#x27;Utarid?&quot; The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: &quot;I have not given it to you to wear it.&quot; So &#x27;Umar gave it to an idolator brother of his in Makkah</p>
