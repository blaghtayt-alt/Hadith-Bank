<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Ibn &#x27;Abbas said:&quot;I was very keen to ask &#x27;Umar bin Al-Khattab about the two wives of the Messenger of Allah to whom Allah said: If you two turn in repentance to Allah, (it will be better for you), your hearts are indeed so inclined.&quot; And he quoted the Hadith. He said concerning it:&#x27; &quot;The from his wives for twenty-nine days because of that, when Hafsah had made her disclosure to Aishah. He had said: &#x27;I will not enter upon them for a month,&#x27; because he was so upset with them when Allah, the Mighty and Sublime, informed him of what they had said. When twenty-nine days had passed, he entered upon &#x27;Aishah, so he started with her. Aishah, said to him: &#x27;O Messenger of Allah, you swore not to enter upon us for a month, and now twenty-nine days have passed; we have been counting them.&#x27; The Messenger of Allah said; &#x27;The month is twenty-nine days</p>
