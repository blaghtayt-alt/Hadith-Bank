<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from Umm Salamah that a woman said:&quot;O Messenger of Allah, Allah is not shy to tell the truth. Does a women have to perform Ghusl if she has a wet dream?&quot; He said: &quot;Yes, if she sees water.&quot;[1] Umm Salamah laughed and said: &quot;Do women really have wet dreams?&quot; The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: &quot;How else would her child resemble her?&quot; [1] That is, if she has an emission of the fluid of an orgasm</p>
