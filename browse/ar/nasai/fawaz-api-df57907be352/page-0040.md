<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Abdullah in &#x27;Umar said:&quot;The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: &#x27;There are two qualities which no Muslim person attains but he will enter Paradise, and they are easy, but those who do them are few.&#x27; The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: &#x27;The five daily prayers: After each prayer one of you glorifies Allah (SWT) ten times and praises Him ten times and magnifies him ten times, which makes one hundred and fifty on the tongue and one thousand five hundred in the balance.&#x27; And I saw the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) counting them on his hands. &#x27;And when one of you retires to his bed he says the tasbih thirty-three times and the tahmid thirty-three times and the takbir thirty-four times, that is one hundred on the tongue and one thousand in the balance.&#x27; The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: &quot;So which of you does two thousand five hundred bad deeds in a day and a night?&quot; It was said: &quot;O Messenger of Allah (ﷺ), how can a person not persist in doing that?&quot; He said: &quot;The Shaitan comes to one of you when he is praying and says: &#x27;Remember such and such, remember such and such,&quot; or he comes to him when he is in bed and makes him fall asleep</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Abu Sa&#x27;eed Al-Khudri said:&quot;The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) used to observe I&#x27;tikaf during the middle ten days of the month, and after the twentieth (day of the month), he would come out on the twenty-first and go back to his home, and those who were observing I&#x27;tikaf with him would go back like him. Then he stayed one month on the night when he used to go back home, and he addressed the people and enjoined upon them whatever Allah (SWT) willed. Then he said: &#x27;I used to observe I&#x27;tikaf during these ten days, then I decided to spend the last ten days in I&#x27;tikaf. So whoever was observing I&#x27;tikaf with me, let him stay in his place of I&#x27;tikaf, for I was shown this night (Lailatul Qadr), then I was caused to forget it, so seek it during the last ten nights on the odd-numbered nights. And I saw myself prostrating in water and mud.&#x27;&quot; Abu Sa&#x27;eed said: &quot;It rained on the night of the twenty-first, and the roof of the Masjid leaked over the place where the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) used to pray. I looked at him when he had finished praying subh and his face was wet with water and mud</p>
