<p dir="auto"><strong>हदीस-ए-नबवी विश्वकोश से चयनित हदीसों का संग्रह · 1</strong></p>

<p><strong>Hadith</strong> · A narration preserved from the source.</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic · full narration</h3>

<p dir="auto">Full narration; the selected source preserves an excerpt or reported meaning.</p>

<p dir="rtl">عن عمر بن الخطاب رضي الله عنه قال: قال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: «إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّةِ، وَإِنَّمَا لِامْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى، فَمَنْ كَانَتْ هِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى اللهِ وَرَسُولِهِ، فَهِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى اللهِ وَرَسُولِهِ، وَمَنْ كَانَتْ هِجْرَتُهُ لِدُنْيَا يُصِيبُهَا أَوِ امْرَأَةٍ يَتَزَوَّجُهَا، فَهِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى مَا هَاجَرَ إِلَيْهِ».<br>
وفي لفظ للبخاري: «إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ، وَإِنَّمَا لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى».</p>

<p dir="rtl">[صحيح البخاري، رقم 6953; صحيح البخاري، رقم 1; صحيح مسلم، رقم 1907]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English · full narration</h3>

<p dir="auto">Full narration; the selected source preserves an excerpt or reported meaning.</p>

<p dir="auto">‘Umar ibn al-Khattāb (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: The Messenger of Allah (may Allah’s peace and blessings be upon him) said: &quot;Verily, the reward of deeds depends on the intention, and a person will be rewarded according to what he intended. So, he whose migration is for the sake of Allah and His Messenger, then his migration is for the sake of Allah and His Messenger, and he whose migration is to achieve some worldly gain or take some woman in marriage, then his migration is for what he migrated for.&quot; In a version by Al-Bukhāri: &quot;Verily, the reward of deeds depends on the intentions, and each person will be rewarded according to what he intended.&quot;</p>

<p dir="auto">[Sahih al-Bukhari no. 6953; Sahih al-Bukhari no. 1; Sahih Muslim no. 1907]</p>

<p dir="rtl">عَنْ مُعَاذٍ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُ، قَالَ: كُنْتُ رِدْفَ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ عَلَى حِمَارٍ يُقَالُ لَهُ عُفَيْرٌ، فَقَالَ: «يَا مُعَاذُ، هَلْ تَدْرِي حَقَّ اللَّهِ عَلَى عِبَادِهِ، وَمَا حَقُّ العِبَادِ عَلَى اللَّهِ؟»، قُلْتُ: اللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُ أَعْلَمُ، قَالَ: «فَإِنَّ حَقَّ اللَّهِ عَلَى العِبَادِ أَنْ يَعْبُدُوهُ وَلا يُشْرِكُوا بِهِ شَيْئًا، وَحَقَّ العِبَادِ عَلَى اللَّهِ أَنْ لا يُعَذِّبَ مَنْ لا يُشْرِكُ بِهِ شَيْئًا»، فَقُلْتُ: يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ أَفَلاَ أُبَشِّرُ بِهِ النَّاسَ؟ قَالَ: «لا تُبَشِّرْهُمْ، فَيَتَّكِلُوا».</p>

<p dir="auto">Mu‘ādh (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: I was riding behind the Prophet (may Allah’s peace and blessings be upon him) on a donkey called ‘Ufayr. The Prophet (may Allah’s peace and blessings be upon him) asked: &quot;O Mu‘ādh, do you know what Allah&#x27;s right upon His servants is, and what the right of His servants upon Him is?&quot; I replied: &quot;Allah and His Messenger know best.&quot; He said: &quot;Allah&#x27;s right upon His servants is that they should worship Him and associate no partners with Him. And the right of the servants upon Allah is that He should not punish whoever associates no partners with Him.&quot; I said: &quot;O Messenger of Allah, should I not inform the people of this good news?&quot; He said: &quot;Do not inform them of it, lest they depend on it solely.&quot;</p>
