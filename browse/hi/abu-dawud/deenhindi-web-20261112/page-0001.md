<p dir="ltr">Abu al-Tayyah reported on the authority of a shaykh (an old man):When Abdullah ibn Abbas came to Basrah, people narrated to him traditions from AbuMusa. <p dir="auto">[Sunan Abi Dawud · 3]</p>
