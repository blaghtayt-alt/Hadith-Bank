<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Al-Miqdad ibn al-Aswad: Ali ibn AbuTalib commanded him to ask the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) what a man should do when he wants to have intercourse with his wife and the prostatic fluid comes out (at this moment). <h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Urwah se riwayat hai ke Ali bin Abi Talib (Radi Allahu Anhu) ne Miqdad (Radi Allahu Anhu) se kaha, phir rawi ne aage isi tarah ki riwayat zikr ki, ismein hai ke Miqdad ne Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se daryaft kiya to Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya: Chahiye ke woh sharmgah (uzw-e-makhsoos) aur foton ko dho dale. Abu Dawood kehte hain: Ise Thawri aur ek jamaat ne Hisham se, Hisham ne apne walid Urwah se, Urwah ne Miqdad (Radi Allahu Anhu) se, Miqdad (Radi Allahu Anhu) ne Ali (Radi Allahu Anhu) se, aur Ali (Radi Allahu Anhu) ne Nabi Akram (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se marfoo&#x27;an riwayat kiya hai.</p>
