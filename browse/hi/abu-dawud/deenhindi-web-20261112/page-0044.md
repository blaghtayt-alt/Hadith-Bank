<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abdullah ibn Abbas: Sa&#x27;id ibn Jubayr said: I said to Abdullah ibn Abbas: AbulAbbas, I am surprised to see the difference of opinion amongst the companions of the Apostle (ﷺ) about the wearing of ihram by the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) when he made it obligatory. He replied: I am aware of it more than the people. The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) performed only one hajj. Hence the people differed among themselves. The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) came out (from Medina) with the intention of performing hajj. When he offered two rak&#x27;ahs of prayer in the mosque at Dhul-Hulayfah, he made it obligatory by wearing it. At the same meeting, he raised his voice in the talbiyah for hajj, when he finished his two rak&#x27;ahs. Some people heard it and I retained it from him. He then rode (on the she-camel), and when it (the she-camel) stood up, with him on its back, he raised his voice in the talbiyah and some people heard it at that moment. This is because the people were coming in groups, so they heard him raising his voice calling the talbiyah when his she-camel stood up with him on its back, and they thought that the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) had raised his voice in the talbiyah when his she-camel stood up with him on its back. The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) proceeded further; when he ascended the height of al-Bayda&#x27; he raised his voice in the talbiyah. Some people heard it at that moment. They thought that he had raised his voice in the talbiyah when he ascended the height of al-Bayda&#x27;. I swear by Allah, he raised his voice in the talbiyah at the place where he prayed, and he raised his voice in the talbiyah when his she-camel stood up with him on its back, and he raised his voice in the talbiyah when he ascended the height of al-Bayda&#x27;. Sa&#x27;id (ibn Jubayr) said; He who follows the view of Ibn Abbas raises his voice in talbiyah (and ihram) at the place of is prayer after he finishes two rak&#x27;ahs of his prayer</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Abi Dawud · 1770]</p>

<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">सईद बिन जुबैर (रदी अल्लाहु अन्हु) कहते हैं के मैं ने अब्दुल्ला बिन अब्बास (रदी अल्लाहु अन्हुमा) से कहा: अबुल-अब्बास! मुझे ताअजुब है के सहाबा किराम (रदी अल्लाहु अन्हुम) रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) के एहराम बांधने के सिलसिले में इख़्तिलाफ़ रखते हैं के आप ने एहराम कब बांधा? तोह उन्होंने कहा: इस बात को लोगों में सब से ज़्यादा मैं जानता हूँ, चूंकि रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने एक ही हज्ज किया था इसी वजह से लोगों ने इख़्तिलाफ़ किया है, रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) हज्ज की नियत कर के मदीना से निकले, जब ज़िल-हुलैफाह की अपनी मस्जिद में आप ने अपनी दो रकअतें अदा कीं तोह इसी मजलिस में आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने एहराम बांधा और दो रकअतों से फ़ारिग होने के बाद हज्ज का तलबीया पढ़ा, लोगों ने इस को सुना और मैं ने इस को याद रखा, फिर आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) सवार हुये जब आप की ऊंटनी आप को ले कर सीधी खड़ी हो गई तोह आप ने तलबीया पढ़ा, बाज़ लोगों ने उस वक़्त आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) को तलबीया पढ़ते हुये पाया, और यह इस वजह से के लोग अलग अलग टुकड़ियों में आप के पास आते थे, तोह जब आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) की ऊंटनी आप को ले कर उठी तोह उन्होंने आप को तलबीया पुकारते हुये सुना तोह कहा: आप ने तलबीया इस वक़्त कहा है जब आप की ऊंटनी आप को ले कर खड़ी हुई फिर रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) चले, जब मक़ाम बैदा की ऊंचाई पर चढ़े तोह तलबीया कहा तोह बाज़ लोगों ने उस वक़्त इसे सुना तोह उन्होंने कहा के आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने इसी वक़्त तलबीया कहा है जब आप बैदा की ऊंचाई पर चढ़े हलांकि अल्लाह की क़सम आप ने वहीं तलबीया कहा था जहां आप ने नमाज़ पढ़ी थी, फिर आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने उस वक़्त तलबीया कहा जब ऊंटनी आप को ले कर सीधी हुई और फिर आप ने बैदा की ऊंचाई चढ़ते वक़्त तलबीया कहा।</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ur-Latn">
<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Saeed bin Jubair (Radi Allahu Anhu) kehte hain ke main ne Abdullah bin Abbas (Radi Allahu Anhuma) se kaha: Abul-Abbas! Mujhe ta&#x27;ajub hai ke sahaba kiram (Radi Allahu Anhum) Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ke ehram bandhne ke silsile mein ikhtilaf rakhte hain ke aap ne ehram kab bandha? Toh unhone kaha: Is baat ko logon mein sab se zyada main jaanta hoon, chunke Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne ek hi hajj kiya tha isi wajah se logon ne ikhtilaf kiya hai, Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) hajj ki niyat kar ke Madina se nikle, jab Zil-Hulaifah ki apni masjid mein aap ne apni do rakatein ada ki toh isi majlis mein aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne ehram bandha aur do rakaton se farigh hone ke baad hajj ka talbiya padha, logon ne is ko suna aur main ne is ko yaad rakha, phir aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) sawar huye jab aap ki oontni aap ko le kar seedhi khadi ho gayi toh aap ne talbiya padha, baaz logon ne us waqt aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ko talbiya padhte huye paya, aur yeh is wajah se ke log alag alag tukdiyon mein aap ke paas aate the, toh jab aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki oontni aap ko le kar uthi toh unhone aap ko talbiya pukarte huye suna toh kaha: Aap ne talbiya is waqt kaha hai jab aap ki oontni aap ko le kar khadi hui phir Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) chale, jab maqam Baida ki unchai par chadhe toh talbiya kaha toh baaz logon ne us waqt ise suna toh unhone kaha ke aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne isi waqt talbiya kaha hai jab aap Baida ki unchai par chadhe halanki Allah ki qasam aap ne wahin talbiya kaha tha jahan aap ne namaz padhi thi, phir aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne us waqt talbiya kaha jab oontni aap ko le kar seedhi hui aur phir aap ne Baida ki unchai chadhte waqt talbiya kaha.</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Abi Dawud · 1770]</p>

<p dir="ltr">Ubayd ibn Jurayj said to Abdullah ibn Umar:AbuAbdurRahman, I saw you doing things which I did not see being done by your companions. He asked: What are they, Ibn Jurayj? He replied: I saw you touching only the two Yamani corners; and I saw you wearing shoes having no hair; I saw you dyeing in yellow colour; and I saw you wearing ihram on the eighth of Dhul-Hijjah, whereas the people had worn ihram when they sighted the moon.
