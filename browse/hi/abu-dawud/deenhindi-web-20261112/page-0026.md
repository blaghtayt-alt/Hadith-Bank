<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا الْقَعْنَبِيُّ، عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ، أَنَّ عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ، رَأَى حُلَّةً سِيَرَاءَ - يَعْنِي تُبَاعُ عِنْدَ بَابِ الْمَسْجِدِ - فَقَالَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ لَوِ اشْتَرَيْتَ هَذِهِ فَلَبِسْتَهَا يَوْمَ الْجُمُعَةِ وَلِلْوَفْدِ إِذَا قَدِمُوا عَلَيْكَ ‏.‏ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏&quot;‏ إِنَّمَا يَلْبَسُ هَذِهِ مَنْ لاَ خَلاَقَ لَهُ فِي الآخِرَةِ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ ثُمَّ جَاءَتْ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِنْهَا حُلَلٌ فَأَعْطَى عُمَرَ بْنَ الْخَطَّابِ مِنْهَا حُلَّةً فَقَالَ عُمَرُ كَسَوْتَنِيهَا يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ وَقَدْ قُلْتَ فِي حُلَّةِ عُطَارِدَ مَا قُلْتَ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏&quot;‏ إِنِّي لَمْ أَكْسُكَهَا لِتَلْبَسَهَا ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَكَسَاهَا عُمَرُ أَخًا لَهُ مُشْرِكًا بِمَكَّةَ ‏.‏</p>

<p dir="rtl">[Sunan Abi Dawud · 1076]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">‘Abd Allah b. ‘Umar said:‘Umar b. al-Khattab saw a silken suit sold at the gate of the mosque. He said: Messenger of Allah, would that you purchase this suit and wear it on Friday and on the occasion when a delegation (from the outside) comes to you. The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: One who has no share in the afterlife will put on this (suit). Afterwards suits of similar nature were brought to the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ). He gave ‘Umar b. al-Khattab one of these suits. ‘Umar said: Messenger of Allah, you are giving it to me for use while you had told me such-and-such about the suit of ‘Utarid (I.e. sold by ‘Utarid). The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: I did not give it to you that you should wear it. Hence ‘Umar gave it to his brother who was a disbeliever at Mecca for wearing</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ur-Latn">
<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abdullah bin Umar (Radi Allahu Anhu) kehte hain ke Umar bin Khattab (Radi Allahu Anhu) ne masjid ke darwazay ke paas ek reshmi jora biktay huay dekha toh unhon ne aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se arz kiya: Allah ke Rasool! Kash! Aap isko khareed lein aur Juma ke din aur jis din aap ke paas bahar ke wufood aayein, isay pehna karein, Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya: Isay toh sirf wahi pehne ga jis ka aakhirat mein koi hissa nahi hai, phir Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ke paas isi qisam ke kuch joray aaye toh aap ne in mein se ek jora Umar (Radi Allahu Anhu) ko diya toh Umar ne arz kiya: Allah ke Rasool! Aap ne yeh kapra mujhe pehne ko diya hai? Halankeh Utarid ke joray ke silsilay mein aap aisa aisa keh chukay hain? Toh aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya: Maine tumhein yeh jora is liye nahi diya hai ke isay tum pehno, chunancha Umar ne isay apne ek mushrik bhai ko jo Makkah mein rehta tha pehne ke liye de diya.</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Abi Dawud · 1076]</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Hazim b. Dinar said:People came to Sahl b. Sa&#x27;d al-Sa&#x27;idi, when they were doubtful about the kind of wood of the pulpit (in the mosque of the Prophet). The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) sent for a woman whom Sahl named and asked her: Order your boy, the carpenter, to construct for me a wooden pulpit so that I sit on it when I deliver a speech to the people. So she ordered him and he made a pulpit of a wood called tarfa taken from al-Ghabah (a place at a distance of nine miles from Medina). He brought it to her. She sent it to the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ). He ordered and that was placed here. I saw the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) praying on it: he said: &quot;Allah is most great&quot;; he then bowed while he was on it; then he returned and prostrated in the root of the pulpit; he then returned (to the pulpit).
