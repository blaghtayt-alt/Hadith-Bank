<p dir="ltr">Abdur Rahman bin Ghanam kehte hain ki Abu Malik Ash&#x27;ari (Radi Allahu Anhu) ne kaha: Kya main tum ko Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki namaz na bataun? Abdur Aala kehte hain ki main yahi samajhta hoon ki aapne meri ummat ki namaz farmaya.</p>
