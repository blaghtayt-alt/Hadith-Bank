<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated &#x27;Abd Allah b. &#x27;Umar: I heard the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) say: If any of you can become like the man who had a faraq of rice, he should become like him. They (the people) asked: Who is the man who had a faraq of rice with him, Messenger of Allah ? Thereupon he narrated the story of the cave when a hillock fell on them (three persons), each of them said: Mention any best work of yours. The narrator said: The third of them said: O Allah, you know that I took a hireling for a faraq of rice. When the evening came, I presented to him his due (i.e. his wages). But he refused to take it and went away. I then cultivated it until I amassed cows and their herdsmen for him. He then met me and said: Give me my dues. I said (to him): Go to those cows and their herdsmen and take them all. He went and drove them away</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: companion report. Attribution: Rafi&#x27; b. Khadij (companion). This numbered source report retains the named Companion&#x27;s words or reported conduct under that attribution; it is not relabelled as the Prophet&#x27;s speech.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Hanzlah b. Qais al-Ansari: I asked Rafi&#x27; b. Khadij about the lease of land for gold and silver (i.e. for dinars and dirhams). There is no harm in it, for the people used to let out land in the time of the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) for what grew by the current of water and at the banks of streamlets and at the places of cultivation. So sometimes this (portion) perished and that (portion) was saved, and sometimes this remained intact and that perished. There was no (form of) lease among the people except this. Therefore, he forbade it. But if there is something which is secure and known, then there is no harm in it.</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Abi Dawud · 3392]</p>
