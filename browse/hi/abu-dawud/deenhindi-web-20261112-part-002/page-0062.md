<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا سَعِيدُ بْنُ شَبِيبٍ، وَحَيْوَةُ بْنُ شُرَيْحٍ الْحِمْصِيُّ، قَالَ حَيْوَةُ حَدَّثَنَا بَقِيَّةُ، عَنْ ثَوْرِ بْنِ يَزِيدَ، عَنْ صَالِحِ بْنِ يَحْيَى بْنِ الْمِقْدَامِ بْنِ مَعْدِيكَرِبَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ جَدِّهِ، عَنْ خَالِدِ بْنِ الْوَلِيدِ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم نَهَى عَنْ أَكْلِ لُحُومِ الْخَيْلِ وَالْبِغَالِ وَالْحَمِيرِ - زَادَ حَيْوَةُ - وَكُلِّ ذِي نَابٍ مِنَ السِّبَاعِ ‏.‏ قَالَ أَبُو دَاوُدَ وَهُوَ قَوْلُ مَالِكٍ ‏.‏ قَالَ أَبُو دَاوُدَ لاَ بَأْسَ بِلُحُومِ الْخَيْلِ وَلَيْسَ الْعَمَلُ عَلَيْهِ ‏.‏ قَالَ أَبُو دَاوُدَ وَهَذَا مَنْسُوخٌ قَدْ أَكَلَ لُحُومَ الْخَيْلِ جَمَاعَةٌ مِنْ أَصْحَابِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِنْهُمُ ابْنُ الزُّبَيْرِ وَفَضَالَةُ بْنُ عُبَيْدٍ وَأَنَسُ بْنُ مَالِكٍ وَأَسْمَاءُ ابْنَةُ أَبِي بَكْرٍ وَسُوَيْدُ بْنُ غَفَلَةَ وَعَلْقَمَةُ وَكَانَتْ قُرَيْشٌ فِي عَهْدِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم تَذْبَحُهَا ‏.‏</p>

<p dir="rtl">[Sunan Abi Dawud · 3790]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Khalid ibn al-Walid: The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) forbade us to eat horse-flesh, the flesh of mules and of asses. The narrator Haywah added: Every beast of prey with a fang. Abu Dawud said: This view is held by Malik. Abu Dawud said: There is no harm in (eating) horse-flesh and this tradition is not practised. Abu Dawud said: This tradition has been abrogated. A body of Companions of the Prophet (ﷺ) had eaten horse-flesh. OF them are: Ibn al-Zubair, Fudalah bin &#x27;Ubaid, Anas bin Malik, Asma&#x27; daughter of Abu Bakr, Suwaid bin Ghaflah, &#x27;Alqamah; the Quraish used to slaughter them (horses) during the time of the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ)</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Abi Dawud · 3790]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="hi">
<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">Khalid bin Waleed (Radi Allahu Anhu) kehte hain Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne ghore, khachar aur gadho ke gosht khane se mana kiya hai. Haywah ne har daant se phaar kar khane wale darinde ka izafa kiya hai. Abu Dawood kehte hain: Yeh Malik ka qaul hai. Abu Dawood kehte hain: Ghore ke gosht mein koi muzaaiqa nahi aur is hadith par amal nahi hai. Abu Dawood kehte hain: Yeh hadith mansookh hai, khud sahaba ki ek jamat ne ghore ka gosht khaya jis mein Abdullah bin Zubair, Fazala bin Ubaid, Anas bin Malik, Asma bint Abu Bakr, Suwaid bin Ghafala, Alqama shamil hain aur Quraish ahd-e-nabwi mein ghore zibah karte thay.</p>

<p dir="auto">[Sunan Abi Dawud · 3790]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: companion report. Attribution: Abdullah ibn Abbas (companion). This numbered source report retains the named Companion&#x27;s words or reported conduct under that attribution; it is not relabelled as the Prophet&#x27;s speech.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abdullah ibn Abbas: The people of pre-Islamic times used to eat some things and leave others alone, considering them unclean. Then Allah sent His Prophet (ﷺ) and sent down His Book, marking some things lawful and others unlawful; so what He made lawful is lawful, what he made unlawful is unlawful, and what he said nothing about is allowable. And he recited: &quot;Say: I find not in the message received by me by inspiration any (meat) forbidden to be eaten by one who wishes to eat it....&quot; up to the end of the verse</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Abi Dawud · 3800]</p>
