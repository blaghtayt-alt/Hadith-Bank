<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Talhat al-Ansari:I heard the Prophet (ﷺ) say: The angels do not enter a house which contains a dog or a picture. Zaid b. Khalid al-Juhani said to Sa&#x27;id b. Yasar al-Ansari, the transmitter of this tradition: Go with me to &#x27;Aishah, Mother of Faithful, so that we ask about it. So we went and said to her: Mother of Faithful, Abu Talhah has transmitted to us a tradition so-and-so. Have you heard the Prophet (ﷺ) mentioning that ? She replied: No but I tell what I saw him doing. The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) went on an expedition and I was waiting for his return. I got a carpet which I hung as a screen on a stick over the door. When he came I received him and said: Peace be upon you, Messenger of Allah, His mercy and His blessings. Praise to be Allah Who gave you dominance and respect. Then he looked at the house and saw the carpet; and he did not respond to me at all. I found (signs of) disapproval in his face. He then came to the carpet and tore it down. He then said: Allah has not commanded us to clothe stones and clay out of the sustenance He has given us. She said: I then cut it to pieces and made two pillows out of it and stuffed them with palm fibre, and he did not disapprove of it to me</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Abi Dawud · 4153]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="hi">
<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">Abu Talha Ansari (Radi Allahu Anhu) kehte hain ke maine Nabi Akram (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ko farmate suna: Farishtay aise ghar mein dakhil nahi hote jis mein kutta ya mujasma ho. Zaid bin Khalid ne jo is hadith ke raavi hain Saeed bin Yasar se kaha: Mere saath Ummul Momineen Aisha (Radi Allahu Anha) ke paas chalo hum unse is baare mein poochenge, chunancha hum gaye aur ja kar poocha: Ummul Momineen! Abu Talha ne hum se hadith bayan ki hai ke Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne hum se aisa aisa farmaya hai, toh kya aap ne bhi Nabi Akram (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ko is ka zikr karte hue suna hai? Aap ne kaha: Nahi, lekin maine jo kuch aap ko karte dekha hai woh main tum se bayan karti hoon, Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) kisi ghazwa mein nikle, main aap ki wapsi ki muntazir thi, maine ek parda liya jo mere paas tha aur use darwaze ki padi lakdi par latka diya, phir jab aaye toh maine aap ka istiqbaal kiya aur kaha: Salamti ho aap par ae Allah ke Rasool! Allah ki rahmat aur us ki barkatein hon, shukr hai us Allah ka jis ne aap ko izzat bakshi aur apne fazl-o-karam se nawaza, aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne ghar par nazar daali, toh aap ki nigah parde par gayi toh aap ne mere salam ka koi jawab nahi diya, maine aap ke chehra par nagawari dekhi, phir aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) parde ke paas aaye aur use utaar diya aur farmaya: Allah ne hamein yeh hukum nahi diya hai ke hum us ki ata ki hui cheezon mein se patthar aur eent ko kapde pehnayein. Phir maine kaat kar us ke do takiye bana liye, aur un mein khajoor ki chhaal ka bharao kiya, toh aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne mere is kaam par koi nakeer nahi farmayi.</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated AbuHurayrah: The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: Gabriel (ﷺ) came to me and said: I came to you last night and was prevented from entering simply because there were images at the door, for there was a decorated curtain with images on it in the house, and there was a dog in the house. So order the head of the image which is in the house to be cut off so that it resembles the form of a tree; order the curtain to be cut up and made into two cushions spread out on which people may tread; and order the dog to be turned out. The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) then did so. The dog belonged to al-Hasan or al-Husayn and was under their couch. So he ordered it to be turned out. Abu Dawud said: Al-Nadd means a thing on which clothes are placed like a couch</p>
