<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Rafi&#x27; b. Khadij:I came to the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) and said: Messenger of Allah, we shall meet the enemy tomorrow and we have no knives with us. May we kill with a sharp-edged white stone (flint) and with splinter of a staff ? The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: Hasten in slaughtering it. When Allah&#x27;s name is mentioned you may eat what is killed by anything which causes the blood to flow except tooth and claw. I shall tell you about it. The tooth is a bone, and the claw is the knife of Abyssinians. Some people hastened and went forward, they made haste and got booty, while the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) was in the rear and they setup cooking pots. The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) passed by over the cooking pots. He ordered to turn them over. He then divided (the spoils of war) between them, and gave them a camel for ten goats in equation. One of the camels of the people ran away, and they had no horses with them at that time. A man shot an arrow at it, and Allah prevented it from escaping. The Prophet (ﷺ) said: Among animals (i.e. camels) there are some which bolt like wild animals ; so when any of them does so, do with it like this</p>

<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">Rafi bin Khadij (Radi Allahu Anhu) kehte hain ki main Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ke paas aaya aur arz kiya: Allah ke Rasool! Toh Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya: Jaldi kar lo, jo cheez khoon baha de aur Allah ka naam us par liya jaye toh use khao, haan woh daant aur nakhun se zibah na ho. Kuch jald-baaz log aage badh gaye, unhone jaldi ki aur kuch maal-e-ghaneemat hasil kar liya. Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) sab se peeche chal rahe the, toh un logon ne degen (degh) chadha dein. Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) un deghon ke paas se guzre toh aap ne unhe palat dene ka hukum diya, chunancha woh palat di gayin. Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne maal-e-ghaneemat taqseem kiya toh ek oonth ko das bakriyon ke barabar qarar diya. Ek oonth bhag nikla, us waqt logon ke paas ghode na the, chunancha ek shakhs ne use teer mara toh Allah ne use rok diya. Is par Nabi-e-Akram (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya: In chaupayon mein bhi badakne wale janwar hote hain jaise wahshi janwar badakte hain, toh jo koi in janwaron mein se aisa kare toh tum bhi uske saath aisa hi karo.</p>
