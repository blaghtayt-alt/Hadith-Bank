<section class="language-reading" lang="ur-Latn">
<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Khalda kehte hain ke maine Abul Aaliyah se poocha: Kya Anas (Radi Allahu Anhu) ne Nabi Akram (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se suna hai? To unhone kaha: Anas (Radi Allahu Anhu) ne Nabi Akram (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki das saal khidmat ki hai aur Aap ne unke liye dua farmayi hai, aur Anas (Radi Allahu Anhu) ka ek baagh tha jo saal mein do baar phalta tha, aur is mein ek khushbudar pauda tha jis se mushk ki boo aati thi.
