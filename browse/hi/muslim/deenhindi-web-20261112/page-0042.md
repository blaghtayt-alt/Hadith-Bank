<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">وَحَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ بْنُ يُونُسَ، حَدَّثَنَا زُهَيْرٌ، حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو إِسْحَاقَ، ح وَحَدَّثَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ يَحْيَى، أَخْبَرَنَا أَبُو خَيْثَمَةَ، عَنْ أَبِي إِسْحَاقَ، قَالَ سَأَلْتُ الأَسْوَدَ بْنَ يَزِيدَ عَمَّا حَدَّثَتْهُ عَائِشَةُ، عَنْ صَلاَةِ، رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَتْ كَانَ يَنَامُ أَوَّلَ اللَّيْلِ وَيُحْيِي آخِرَهُ ثُمَّ إِنْ كَانَتْ لَهُ حَاجَةٌ إِلَى أَهْلِهِ قَضَى حَاجَتَهُ ثُمَّ يَنَامُ فَإِذَا كَانَ عِنْدَ النِّدَاءِ الأَوَّلِ - قَالَتْ - وَثَبَ - وَلاَ وَاللَّهِ مَا قَالَتْ قَامَ - فَأَفَاضَ عَلَيْهِ الْمَاءَ - وَلاَ وَاللَّهِ مَا قَالَتِ اغْتَسَلَ ‏.‏ وَأَنَا أَعْلَمُ مَا تُرِيدُ - وَإِنْ لَمْ يَكُنْ جُنُبًا تَوَضَّأَ وُضُوءَ الرَّجُلِ لِلصَّلاَةِ ثُمَّ صَلَّى الرَّكْعَتَيْنِ ‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">A&#x27;isha thus reported about the (night prayer) of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him): He used to sleep in the early part of the night, and woke up in the latter part. If he then wished intercourse with his wife, he satisfied his desire, and then went to sleep; and when the first call to prayer was made he jumped up (by Allah, she, i. e. &#x27;A&#x27;isha, did not say&quot; he stood up&quot; ), and poured water over him (by Allah she, i. e. &#x27;A&#x27;isha, did not say that he took a bath but I know what she meant) and if he did not have an intercourse, he performed ablution, just as a man performs ablution for prayer and then observed two rak&#x27;ahs</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Muslim · 1728]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="hi">
<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">अबू खुसैमा (ज़ुहैर बिन मुआविया) ने अबू इसहाक़ से ख़बर दी, कहा: मैं ने अस्वद बिन यज़ीद से उस हदीस के बारे में पूछा जो उन से हज़रत आइशा (रज़ियल्लाहु अन्हा) ने रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) की नमाज़ के बारे में बयान की थी। उन्होंने (आइशा रज़ियल्लाहु अन्हा) ने कहा: रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) रात के पहले हिस्से में सो जाते और आख़री हिस्से को ज़िंदा करते (अल्लाह के सामने क़याम फ़रमाते हुए जागते) फिर अगर अपने घर वालों से कोई ज़रूरत होती तो अपनी ज़रूरत पूरी करते और सो जाते, फिर जब पहली अज़ान का वक़्त होता तो आइशा (रज़ियल्लाहु अन्हा) ने कहा: आप उछल कर खड़े होजाते (रावी ने कहा:) अल्लाह की कसम! आइशा (रज़ियल्लाहु अन्हा) ने वत्सब कहा, कामा (खड़े होते) नहीं कहा। फिर अपने ऊपर पानी बहाते, अल्लाह की कसम! उन्होंने इगतसाला (नहाते) नहीं कहा: &quot;अपने ऊपर पानी बहाते&quot; मैं जानता हूँ उन की मुराद क्या थी। (यानी ज़्यादा मिक़दार में पानी बहाते) और अगर आप जुनूबी न होते तो जिस तरह आदमी नमाज़ के लिए वज़ू करता है, उसी तरह वज़ू फ़रमाते, फिर दो रकअतें (सुन्नत फ़ज्र) अदा फ़रमाते।</p>

<p dir="auto">[Sahih Muslim · 1728]</p>

<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Khusaima (Zuhair bin Muawiyah) ne Abu Ishaq se khabar di, kaha: Main ne Aswad bin Yazid se us hadith ke bare mein pucha jo un se Hazrat Aisha (Radi Allahu Anha) ne Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki namaz ke bare mein bayan ki thi. Unhone (Aisha Radi Allahu Anha) ne kaha: Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) raat ke pehle hisse mein so jate aur aakhri hisse ko zinda karte (Allah ke samne qayam farmate hue jaagte) phir agar apne ghar walon se koi zaroorat hoti to apni zaroorat puri karte aur so jate, phir jab pehli azan ka waqt hota to Aisha (Radi Allahu Anha) ne kaha: Aap uchhal kar khade hojate (rawi ne kaha:) Allah ki qasam! Aisha (Radi Allahu Anha) ne wathab kaha, qama (khade hote) nahi kaha. Phir apne upar paani bahate, Allah ki qasam! Unhone ightasala (nahate) nahi kaha: &quot;Apne upar paani bahate&quot; main janta hoon un ki murad kya thi. (Yaani zyada miqdar mein paani bahate) aur agar aap junubi na hote to jis tarah aadmi namaz ke liye wuzu karta hai, usi tarah wuzu farmate, phir do rakaatein (sunnat Fajr) ada farmate.</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْمُثَنَّى الْعَنَزِيُّ، حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ أَبِي عَدِيٍّ، عَنْ سَعِيدٍ، عَنْ قَتَادَةَ، عَنْ زُرَارَةَ، أَنَّ سَعْدَ بْنَ هِشَامِ بْنِ عَامِرٍ، أَرَادَ أَنْ يَغْزُوَ، فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ فَقَدِمَ الْمَدِينَةَ فَأَرَادَ أَنْ يَبِيعَ عَقَارًا لَهُ بِهَا فَيَجْعَلَهُ فِي السِّلاَحِ وَالْكُرَاعِ وَيُجَاهِدَ الرُّومَ حَتَّى يَمُوتَ فَلَمَّا قَدِمَ الْمَدِينَةَ لَقِيَ أُنَاسًا مِنْ أَهْلِ الْمَدِينَةِ فَنَهَوْهُ عَنْ ذَلِكَ وَأَخْبَرُوهُ أَنَّ رَهْطًا سِتَّةً أَرَادُوا ذَلِكَ فِي حَيَاةِ نَبِيِّ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَنَهَاهُمْ نَبِيُّ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ أَلَيْسَ لَكُمْ فِيَّ أُسْوَةٌ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَلَمَّا حَدَّثُوهُ بِذَلِكَ رَاجَعَ امْرَأَتَهُ وَقَدْ كَانَ طَلَّقَهَا وَأَشْهَدَ عَلَى رَجْعَتِهَا فَأَتَى ابْنَ عَبَّاسٍ فَسَأَلَهُ عَنْ وِتْرِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ ابْنُ عَبَّاسٍ أَلاَ أَدُلُّكَ عَلَى أَعْلَمِ أَهْلِ الأَرْضِ بِوِتْرِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ مَنْ قَالَ عَائِشَةُ ‏.‏ فَأْتِهَا فَاسْأَلْهَا ثُمَّ ائْتِنِي فَأَخْبِرْنِي بِرَدِّهَا عَلَيْكَ فَانْطَلَقْتُ إِلَيْهَا فَأَتَيْتُ عَلَى حَكِيمِ بْنِ أَفْلَحَ فَاسْتَلْحَقْتُهُ إِلَيْهَا فَقَالَ مَا أَنَا بِقَارِبِهَا لأَنِّي نَهَيْتُهَا أَنْ تَقُولَ فِي هَاتَيْنِ الشِّيعَتَيْنِ شَيْئًا فَأَبَتْ فِيهِمَا إِلاَّ مُضِيًّا ‏.‏ - قَالَ - فَأَقْسَمْتُ عَلَيْهِ فَجَاءَ فَانْطَلَقْنَا إِلَى عَائِشَةَ فَاسْتَأْذَنَّا عَلَيْهَا فَأَذِنَتْ لَنَا فَدَخَلْنَا عَلَيْهَا ‏.‏ فَقَالَتْ أَحَكِيمٌ فَعَرَفَتْهُ ‏.‏ فَقَالَ نَعَمْ ‏.‏ فَقَالَتْ مَنْ مَعَكَ قَالَ سَعْدُ بْنُ هِشَامٍ ‏.‏ قَالَتْ مَنْ هِشَامٌ قَالَ ابْنُ عَامِرٍ فَتَرَحَّمَتْ عَلَيْهِ وَقَالَتْ خَيْرًا - قَالَ قَتَادَةُ وَكَانَ أُصِيبَ يَوْمَ أُحُدٍ ‏.‏ فَقُلْتُ يَا أُمَّ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ أَنْبِئِينِي عَنْ خُلُقِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏.‏ قَالَتْ أَلَسْتَ تَقْرَأُ الْقُرْآنَ قُلْتُ بَلَى ‏.‏ قَالَتْ فَإِنَّ خُلُقَ نَبِيِّ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم كَانَ الْقُرْآنَ ‏.‏ - قَالَ - فَهَمَمْتُ أَنْ أَقُومَ وَلاَ أَسْأَلَ أَحَدًا عَنْ شَىْءٍ حَتَّى أَمُوتَ ثُمَّ بَدَا لِي فَقُلْتُ أَنْبِئِينِي عَنْ قِيَامِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏.‏ فَقَالَتْ أَلَسْتَ تَقْرَأُ ‏{‏ يَا أَيُّهَا الْمُزَّمِّلُ‏}‏ قُلْتُ بَلَى ‏.‏ قَالَتْ فَإِنَّ اللَّهَ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ افْتَرَضَ قِيَامَ اللَّيْلِ فِي أَوَّلِ هَذِهِ السُّورَةِ فَقَامَ نَبِيُّ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَصْحَابُهُ حَوْلاً وَأَمْسَكَ اللَّهُ خَاتِمَتَهَا اثْنَىْ عَشَرَ شَهْرًا فِي السَّمَاءِ حَتَّى أَنْزَلَ اللَّهُ فِي آخِرِ هَذِهِ السُّورَةِ التَّخْفِيفَ فَصَارَ قِيَامُ اللَّيْلِ تَطَوُّعًا بَعْدَ فَرِيضَةٍ ‏.‏ - قَالَ - قُلْتُ يَا أُمَّ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ أَنْبِئِينِي عَنْ وِتْرِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏.‏ فَقَالَتْ كُنَّا نُعِدُّ لَهُ سِوَاكَهُ وَطَهُورَهُ فَيَبْعَثُهُ اللَّهُ مَا شَاءَ أَنْ يَبْعَثَهُ مِنَ اللَّيْلِ فَيَتَسَوَّكُ وَيَتَوَضَّأُ وَيُصَلِّي تِسْعَ رَكَعَاتٍ لاَ يَجْلِسُ فِيهَا إِلاَّ فِي الثَّامِنَةِ فَيَذْكُرُ اللَّهَ وَيَحْمَدُهُ وَيَدْعُوهُ ثُمَّ يَنْهَضُ وَلاَ يُسَلِّمُ ثُمَّ يَقُومُ فَيُصَلِّي التَّاسِعَةَ ثُمَّ يَقْعُدُ فَيَذْكُرُ اللَّهَ وَيَحْمَدُهُ وَيَدْعُوهُ ثُمَّ يُسَلِّمُ تَسْلِيمًا يُسْمِعُنَا ثُمَّ يُصَلِّي رَكْعَتَيْنِ بَعْدَ مَا يُسَلِّمُ وَهُوَ قَاعِدٌ فَتِلْكَ إِحْدَى عَشْرَةَ رَكْعَةً يَا بُنَىَّ فَلَمَّا أَسَنَّ نَبِيُّ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَخَذَ اللَّحْمَ أَوْتَرَ بِسَبْعٍ وَصَنَعَ فِي الرَّكْعَتَيْنِ مِثْلَ صَنِيعِهِ الأَوَّلِ فَتِلْكَ تِسْعٌ يَا بُنَىَّ وَكَانَ نَبِيُّ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِذَا صَلَّى صَلاَةً أَحَبَّ أَنْ يُدَاوِمَ عَلَيْهَا وَكَانَ إِذَا غَلَبَهُ نَوْمٌ أَوْ وَجَعٌ عَنْ قِيَامِ اللَّيْلِ صَلَّى مِنَ النَّهَارِ ثِنْتَىْ عَشْرَةَ رَكْعَةً وَلاَ أَعْلَمُ نَبِيَّ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَرَأَ الْقُرْآنَ كُلَّهُ فِي لَيْلَةٍ وَلاَ صَلَّى لَيْلَةً إِلَى الصُّبْحِ وَلاَ صَامَ شَهْرًا كَامِلاً غَيْرَ رَمَضَانَ ‏.‏ - <section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Sa&#x27;d b. Hisham b. &#x27;Amir decided to participate in the expedition for the sake of Allah, so he came to Medina and he decided to dispose of his property there and buy arms and horses instead and fight against the Romans to the end of his life. When he came to Medina, he met the people of Medina. They dissuaded him to do such a thing, and informed him that a group of six men had decided to do so during the lifetime of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) and the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) forbade them to do it, and said: Is there not for you a model pattern in me? And when they narrated this to him (Sa&#x27;d b. Hisham), he returned to his wife, though he had divorced her and made (people) witness to his reconciliation. He then came to Ibn &#x27;Abbas and asked him about the Witr of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him). Ibn &#x27;Abbas said: Should I not lead you to one who knows best amongst the people of the world about the Witr of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him)? He said: Who is it? He (Ibn &#x27;Abbas) said: It is &#x27;A&#x27;isha. So go to her and ask her (about Witr) and then come to me and inform me about her answer that she would give you. So I came to Hakim bin Aflah and requested him to take me to her. He said: I would not go to her, for I forbade her to speak anything (about the conflict) between the two groupS, but she refused (to accept my advice) and went (to participate in that corflict). I (requested) him (Hakim) with an oath to lead me to her. So we went to &#x27;A&#x27;isha and we begged permission to meet her. She granted us permission and we went in. She said: Are you Hakim? (She recognised him.) He replied: Yes. She said: Who is there with you? He said: He is Sa&#x27;d b. Hisham. She said: Which Hisham? He said: He is Hisham b. &#x27;Amir. She blessed him (&#x27;Amir) with mercy from Allah and spoke good of him (Qatada said that he died as a martyr in Uhud). I said: Mother of the Faithful, tell me about the character of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him). She said: Don&#x27;t you read the Qur&#x27;an? I said: Yes. Upon this she said: The character of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) was the Qur&#x27;an. He said: I felt inclined to get up and not ask anything (further) till death. But then I changed my mind and said: Inform me about the observance (of the night prayer) of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him). She said: Did you not recite: &quot; O thou wrapped up&quot;? He said: Yes. She said: Allah, the Exalted and the Glorious, made the observance of the night prayer at the beginning of this Surah obligatory. So the Messenger of Allah (may peace be upon him and his Companions around him observed this (night prayer) for one year. Allah held back the concluding portion of this Surah for twelve months in the Heaven till (at the end of this period) Allah revealed the concluding verses of this Surah which lightened (the burden of this prayer), and the night prayer became a supererogatory prayer after being an obligatory one. I said: Mother of the Faithful, inform me about the Witr of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him). She said: I used to prepare tooth stick for him and water for his ablution, and Allah would rouse him to the extent He wished during the night. He would use the tooth stick, and perform ablution, and would offer nine rak&#x27;ahs, and would not sit but in the eighth one and would remember Allah, and praise Him and supplicate Him, then he would get up without uttering the salutation and pray the ninth rak&#x27;ah. He would then sit, remember, praise Him and supplicate Him and then utter a salutation loud enough for us to hear. He would then pray two rak&#x27;ahs sitting after uttering the salutation, and that made eleven rak&#x27;ahs. O my son, but when the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) grew old and put on flesh, he observed Witr of seven, doing in the two rak&#x27;ahs as he had done formerly, and that made nine. <h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Ibn Abi Adi ne Saeed (ibn Abi Urooba) se, unhone Qatada se aur unhone Zurara se riwayat ki ke Saad bin Hisham bin Aamir ne irada kiya ke Allah ki rah mein jihad karein, woh Madina Munawara aa gaye aur apni ek jaidad bechni chahi taake hathiyar aur ghode khareed saken aur maut tak Rumiyon ke khilaf jihad karein. Jab Madina aaye to kuch logon ne unhe is irade se roka aur bataya ke Nabi Akram (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ke zamane mein bhi kuch logon ne aisa irada kiya tha magar Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne mana farmaya tha: Kya mere tarz-e-amal mein tumhare liye namoona nahi? Is par unhone apni biwi se ruju kar liya (jisay talaq di thi). Phir Hazrat Ibn Abbas (Radi Allahu Anhu) ke paas gaye aur Witr aur Qayam-ul-Lail ke bare mein pucha. Unhone Hazrat Aisha (Radi Allahu Anha) ke paas bheja. Jab woh wahan pahuche aur wazahat chahi to Hazrat Aisha (Radi Allahu Anha) ne farmaya ke Nabi (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ka akhlaq Quran tha. Raat ke qayam ke bare mein bataya ke Surah Muzammil ke aaghaz mein qayam farz tha jo baad mein nafal ho gaya. Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) pehle 9 rakat parhte the aur baad mein jab jism mubarak bhari hua to 7 rakat parhne lage. Agar kabhi neend ya bimari ki wajah se na parh paate to din mein 12 rakat parh lete the.</p>
