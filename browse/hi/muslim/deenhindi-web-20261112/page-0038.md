<h3>Arabic</h3>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Imran bin Husain reported: We were with the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) in a journey and we travelled throughout the night till at the end, just before dawn, we lay down (for rest), and nothing is sweeter for a traveller than this and none awakened us but the heat of the sun, and the rest of the hadith is the same (as mentioned above) except this additien: &quot; When &#x27;Umar bin Khattab woke up, he saw what had happened to the people. And he was a man having a big belly and strongly built; he recited takbir in a loud voice till the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) woke up by the loudness of his voice in takbir. When the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) got up, the people told him what had happened. Upon this the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said: There is no harm; you better proceed further,&quot; and (the rest of the hadith) was narrated</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Muslim · 1564]</p>

<p dir="rtl">وَحَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، وَأَبُو كُرَيْبٍ وَزُهَيْرُ بْنُ حَرْبٍ وَإِسْحَاقُ بْنُ إِبْرَاهِيمَ قَالَ إِسْحَاقُ أَخْبَرَنَا وَقَالَ الآخَرُونَ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ إِدْرِيسَ، عَنِ ابْنِ جُرَيْجٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ أَبِي عَمَّارٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ بَابَيْهِ، عَنْ يَعْلَى بْنِ أُمَيَّةَ، قَالَ قُلْتُ لِعُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ ‏{‏ لَيْسَ عَلَيْكُمْ جُنَاحٌ أَنْ تَقْصُرُوا، مِنَ الصَّلاَةِ إِنْ خِفْتُمْ أَنْ يَفْتِنَكُمُ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا‏}‏ فَقَدْ أَمِنَ النَّاسُ فَقَالَ عَجِبْتُ مِمَّا عَجِبْتَ مِنْهُ فَسَأَلْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنْ ذَلِكَ ‏.‏ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ صَدَقَةٌ تَصَدَّقَ اللَّهُ بِهَا عَلَيْكُمْ فَاقْبَلُوا صَدَقَتَهُ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya bin Umayya said: I told &#x27;Umar b. al-Khattab that Allah had said: &quot; You may shorten the prayer only if you fear that those who are unbelievers may afflict you&quot; (Qur&#x27;an, iv. 101), whereas the people are now safe. He replied: I wondered about it in the same way as you wonder about it, so I asked the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) about it and he said: It is an act of charity which Allah has done to you, so accept His charity</p>
