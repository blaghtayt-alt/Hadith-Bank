<p dir="ltr">Uthman bin Abu&#x27;l-&#x27;As at-Thaqafi reported: The Apostle of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said to him: Lead your people in prayer. I said: Messenger of Allah. I perceive something (disturbing) in my soul. He then said: Act as an Imam for your people. He who acts as Imam of the people, he must be brief, for among them are the aged, among them are the sick, among them are the weak, and among them are the people who have business to attend.
