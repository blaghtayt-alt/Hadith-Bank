<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنِي مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ الْمُثَنَّى، حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ جَعْفَرٍ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، قَالَ سَمِعْتُ مُهَاجِرًا أَبَا الْحَسَنِ، يُحَدِّثُ أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ زَيْدَ بْنَ وَهْبٍ، يُحَدِّثُ عَنْ أَبِي ذَرٍّ، قَالَ أَذَّنَ مُؤَذِّنُ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِالظُّهْرِ فَقَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏&quot;‏ أَبْرِدْ أَبْرِدْ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ أَوْ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ انْتَظِرِ انْتَظِرْ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ وَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ إِنَّ شِدَّةَ الْحَرِّ مِنْ فَيْحِ جَهَنَّمَ فَإِذَا اشْتَدَّ الْحَرُّ فَأَبْرِدُوا عَنِ الصَّلاَةِ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ قَالَ أَبُو ذَرٍّ حَتَّى رَأَيْنَا فَىْءَ التُّلُولِ ‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Dharr reported: The Mu&#x27;adhdbin (the announcer of the hour of prayer) of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) called for the noon prayer. Upon this the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said: Let it cool down, let it cool down, or he said: Wait, wait for the intensity of heat is from the exhalation of Hell. When the heat is intense, delay the prayer till it becomes cooler. Abu Dharr said: (We waited) till we saw the shadow of the mounds</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Muslim · 1400]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="hi">
<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">Hazrat Abu Zar (Radi Allahu Anhu) se riwayat hai, unhon ne kaha Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ka muazzin Zuhr ki azan dene laga to Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya: (Waqt ko) thanda hone do, thanda hone do. Ya farmaya: Intezar karo, intezar karo. Aur farmaya: Bila shuba garmi ki shiddat Jahannam ki lapton mein se hai, is liye jab garmi shadeed ho jaye to namaz thande waqt tak moakkhar karo. Abu Zar (Radi Allahu Anhu) ka qaul hai: (Namaz mein itni takheer ki gayi) hatta ke hum ne teelon ka saya dekha.</p>

<p dir="rtl">وَحَدَّثَنِي عَمْرُو بْنُ سَوَّادٍ، وَحَرْمَلَةُ بْنُ يَحْيَى، - وَاللَّفْظُ لِحَرْمَلَةَ - أَخْبَرَنَا ابْنُ وَهْبٍ، أَخْبَرَنِي يُونُسُ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، قَالَ حَدَّثَنِي أَبُو سَلَمَةَ بْنُ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ أَبَا هُرَيْرَةَ، يَقُولُ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ &quot;‏ اشْتَكَتِ النَّارُ إِلَى رَبِّهَا فَقَالَتْ يَا رَبِّ أَكَلَ بَعْضِي بَعْضًا ‏.‏ فَأَذِنَ لَهَا بِنَفَسَيْنِ نَفَسٍ فِي الشِّتَاءِ وَنَفَسٍ فِي الصَّيْفِ فَهُوَ أَشَدُّ مَا تَجِدُونَ مِنَ الْحَرِّ وَأَشَدُّ مَا تَجِدُونَ مِنَ الزَّمْهَرِيرِ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Huraira reported: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said: The Fire made a complaint before the Lord saying.&quot; O Lord, some parts of mine have consumed the others.&quot; So it was allowed to take two exhalations, one exhalation in winter and the other exhalation in summer. That is why you find extreme heat (in summer) and extreme cold (in winter)</p>

<p dir="rtl">وَحَدَّثَنَا عَوْنُ بْنُ سَلاَّمٍ الْكُوفِيُّ، أَخْبَرَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ طَلْحَةَ الْيَامِيُّ، عَنْ زُبَيْدٍ، عَنْ مُرَّةَ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ، قَالَ حَبَسَ الْمُشْرِكُونَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَنْ صَلاَةِ الْعَصْرِ حَتَّى احْمَرَّتِ الشَّمْسُ أَوِ اصْفَرَّتْ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏&quot;‏ شَغَلُونَا عَنِ الصَّلاَةِ الْوُسْطَى صَلاَةِ الْعَصْرِ مَلأَ اللَّهُ أَجْوَافَهُمْ وَقُبُورَهُمْ نَارًا ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ أَوْ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ حَشَا اللَّهُ أَجْوَافَهُمْ وَقُبُورَهُمْ نَارًا ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abdullah (b. Mas&#x27;ud) reported that the polytheists detained the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) from observing the afternoon prayer till the sun became red or it became yellow. Upon this the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said: They have diverted us from (offering) the middle prayer. i. e. the &#x27;Asr prayer. May Allah fill their bellies and their graves with fire, or he said: May Allah stuff their bellies and their graves with fire</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Muslim · 1426]</p>
