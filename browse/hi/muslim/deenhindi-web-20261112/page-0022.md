<p dir="ltr">Jabir bin Samura reported: When we said prayer with the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him), we pronounced: Peace be upon you and Mercy of Allah, peace be upon you and Mercy of Allah, and made gesture with the hand on both the sides.
