<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: prophetic report. Attribution: The Messenger of Allah in Anas ibn Maliks travel account. The complete field supplies the Medina-Mecca journey, two-unit prayers and the answer about the ten-day stay.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Anas bin Malik reported: We went out from Medina to Mecca with the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) and he prayed two rak&#x27;ahs at each time of prayer till we returned to Medina. I said: For how long did he stay in Mecca? He said: (For) ten (days)</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">

<p dir="ltr">Ibn &#x27;Umar reported that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) used to say prayer on his camel while coming from Mecca to Medina, in whatever direction his face had turned; and its was (in this context) that this verse was revealed: &quot; So whether you turn thither is Allah&#x27;s face&quot; (ii)</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Muslim · 1612]</p>
