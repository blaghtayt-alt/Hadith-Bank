<p dir="auto">Report type: companion report. Attribution: Umar and Sad in Jabir ibn Samuras report. The complete field gives their dialogue about the complaints and prayer, preserving the two source alternatives for Umars final response.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Jabir bin Samura reported:&#x27;Umar said to Sa&#x27;d: They complain against you in every matter, even in prayer. He (Sa&#x27;d) said: I prolong (standing) in the first two (rak&#x27;ahs) and shorten it in the last two, and I make no negligence in following the prayer of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him). He (&#x27;Umar) remarked: This is what is expected of you, or, that is what I deemed of you</p>

<p dir="ltr">Jabir reported that Mu&#x27;adh b. Jabal used to pray with the Apostle (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him), then came and led his people in prayer. <h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Jabir reported:&#x27;Mu&#x27;adh b. Jabal al-Ansari led his companions in the night prayer and prolonged it for them. A person amongst us said prayer (after having separated himself from the congregation). Mu&#x27;adh was informed of this, and he remarked that he was a hypocrite. When it (the remark) was conveyed to the man, he went to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) and informed him of what Mu&#x27;adh had said. Upon this the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said to him: Mu&#x27;adh, do you want to become a person putting (people) to trial? When you lead people in prayer, recite: &quot; By the Sun and its morning brightness&quot; (Surat ash-Shams),&quot; Glorify the name of thy most high Lord&quot; (Surat al-A`la) and&quot; Read in the name of Lord&quot; (Surat al-`Alaq), and&quot; By the night when it spreads&quot; (Surat al-Lail)</p>
