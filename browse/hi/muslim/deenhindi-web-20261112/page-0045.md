<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: companion report. Attribution: Aisha describing her and the Messengers actions during prayer. The complete field gives her leg position, the pinch and response, and her statement about the absence of lamps. Preserve the providers typos without repair.</p>

<p dir="ltr">A&#x27;isha reported: I was sleeping in front of the Mcsseinger ef Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) with my legs between him and the Qibla. When he prostrated himself he pinched me and I drew up my legs, and when be stood up, I stretched them out. She said: At that time there were no lamps in the houses</p>
