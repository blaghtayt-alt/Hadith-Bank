<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: companion report. Attribution: Ibn Abbas answering the repeated calls to prayer in Abdullah ibn Shaqiqs account. The full field supplies the repeated exchange and Ibn Abbas statement about combining prayers during the Messengers life. Preserve the actual speaking Companion.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Abdullah bin Shaqiq al-&#x27;Uqaili reported: A person said to Ibn &#x27;Abbas (as he delayed the prayer): Prayer. He kept silence. He again said: Prayer. He again kept silence, and he again cried: Prayer. He again kept silence and said: May you be deprived of your mother, do you teach us about prayer? We used to combine two prayers during the life of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him)</p>
