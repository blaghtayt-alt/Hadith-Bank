<p dir="ltr">Zaid bin Thabit reported: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) made an apartment with the help of the leaves of date trees or of mats. The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) went out to pray in it. People followed him and came to pray with him. Then they again came one night and waited (for him), but the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) delayed in coming out to them. And when he did not come out, they cried aloud and threw pebbles at the door. The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) came out in anger and said to them: By what you have been constantly doing, I was inclined to think that it (prayer) might not become obligatory for you. The people crowded round him one night. He (the Holy Prophet) then said: O people, perform such acts as you are capable of doing, for Allah does not grow weary but you will get tired.
