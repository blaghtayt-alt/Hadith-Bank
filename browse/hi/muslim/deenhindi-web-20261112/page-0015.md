<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Huraira reported the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said: My people would come to me on the Cistern and I would drive away persons (from it) just as a person drives away other people&#x27;s camels from his camels. They (the hearers) said: Apostle of Allah, would you recognize us? He replied: Yea, you would have a mark which other people will not have. You would come to me with a white blaze on your foreheads and white marks on your feet because of the traces of ablution. A group among you would be prevented from coming to me, and they would not meet me, and I would say: O my Lord, they are my companions. Upon this an angel would reply to me saying: Do you know what these people did after you</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Muslim · 582]</p>

<p dir="ltr">Hudhaifa reported: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said: My Cistern is bigger than the space between Aila and Aden. <section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Huraira reported: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) came to the graveyard and said: &quot;Peace be upon you! The abode of the believing people and we, if God so wills, are about to join you. I love to see my brothers.&quot; They (the hearers) said: Aren&#x27;t we your brothers, O Messenger of Allah? He said: You are my companions, and our brothers are those who have, so far, not come into the world. They said: Messenger of Allah, how would you recognise those persons of your Ummah who have not yet been born? He said: Supposing a man had horses with white blazes on foreheads and legs among horses which were all black, tell me, would he not recognise his own horses? They said: Certainly, O Messenger of Allah. He said: They would come with white faces and arms and legs owing to ablution, and I would arrive at the Cistern before them. Some people would be driven away from my Cistern as the stray camel is driven away. I would call out: Come, come. Then it would be said (to me): These people changed themselves after you, and I would say: Be off, be off</p>

<p dir="auto">इस्माइल बिन जाफ़र ने अला से, उन्होंने अपने वालिद से और उन्होंने हज़रत अबू हुरैरा (रज़ि अल्लाहु अन्हु) से रिवायत की के रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) क़ब्रिस्तान में आए और फ़रमाया: ऐ ईमान वाली क़ौम के घराने! तुम सब पर सलामती हो और हम भी इंशाअल्लाह तुम्हें साथ मिलने वाले हैं, मेरी ख़्वाहिश है के हम ने अपने भाइयों को भी देखा होता। सहाबा ने अर्ज़ की: ऐ अल्लाह के रसूल! क्या हम आप के भाई नहीं? आप ने जवाब दिया: तुम मेरे साथी हो और हमारे भाई वो लोग हैं जो अभी तक दुनिया में नहीं आए। इस पर उन्होंने अर्ज़ की: ऐ अल्लाह के रसूल! आप अपनी उम्मत के उन लोगों को, जो अभी दुनिया में नहीं आए, कैसे पहचानेंगे? तो आप ने फ़रमाया: बताओ! अगर काले स्याह घोड़ों के दरम्यान किसी के सफ़ेद चेहरे और सफ़ेद पांव वाले घोड़े हों तो क्या वो अपने घोड़ों को नहीं पहचानेगा? उन्होंने कहा: क्यों नहीं, ऐ अल्लाह के रसूल! आप ने फ़रमाया: वो वुज़ू की बिना पर रोशन चेहरों, सफ़ेद हाथ पांव के साथ आएंगे और मैं हौज पर उन का पेश-रो होगा, ख़बरदार! कुछ लोग यक़ीनन मेरे हौज से परे हटाए जाएंगे, जैसे कहीं और का भटका हुआ ऊंट जो गले का हिस्सा नहीं होता परे हटा दिया जाता है, मैं उन्हें आवाज़ दूंगा, देखो! इधर आ जाओ। तो कहा जाएगा, उन्होंने आप के बाद अपने क़ौल-ओ-अमल को बदल लिया था। तो मैं कहूंगा: दूर हो जाओ, दूर हो जाओ।</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ur-Latn">
<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Ismail bin Jafar ne Alaa se, unhone apne walid se aur unhone Hazrat Abu Huraira (Radi Allahu Anhu) se riwayat ki ke Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) qabristan mein aaye aur farmaya: Ae iman wali qoum ke gharane! Tum sab par salamti ho aur hum bhi InshaAllah tumhein sath milne wale hain, meri khwahish hai ke hum ne apne bhaiyon ko bhi dekha hota. Sahaba ne arz ki: Ae Allah ke Rasool! Kya hum aap ke bhai nahi? Aap ne jawab diya: Tum mere sathi ho aur hamare bhai wo log hain jo abhi tak duniya mein nahi aaye. Is par unhone arz ki: Ae Allah ke Rasool! Aap apni ummat ke un logon ko, jo abhi duniya mein nahi aaye, kaise pehchanenge? Toh aap ne farmaya: Batao! Agar kale siyah ghoron ke darmiyan kisi ke safed chehre aur safed paon wale ghore hon toh kya wo apne ghoron ko nahi pehchanega? Unhone kaha: Kyun nahi, ae Allah ke Rasool! Aap ne farmaya: Wo wudu ki bina par roshan chehron, safed hath paon ke sath aayenge aur main hauz par un ka pesh-ro hunga, khabardar! Kuch log yaqeenan mere hauz se pare hataye jayenge, jaise kahin aur ka bhatka hua unt jo ghaley ka hissa nahi hota pare hata diya jata hai, main unhein awaz dunga, dekho! idhar aa jao. Toh kaha jayega, unhone aap ke baad apne qaul-o-amal ko badal liya tha. Toh main kahunga: Door ho jao, door ho jao.</p>
