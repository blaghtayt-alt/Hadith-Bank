<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: companion report. Attribution: Aisha in the actual exchange reported by Abdullah ibn Shihab al-Khaulani. The full field gives the guests action, the message and answer, and Aishas recollection of her own practice with the Messengers garment. Preserve the actual speakers.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Abdullah bin Shihab al-Khaulani reported: I stayed in the house of &#x27;A&#x27;isha and had a wet dream (and perceived its effect on my garment), so (in the morning) I dipped both (the clothes) in water. This (act of mine) was watched by a maid-servant of A&#x27;isha and she informed her. She (Hadrat A&#x27;isha) sent me a message: Whatprompted you to act like this with your clothes? He (the narrator) said: I told that I saw in a dream what a sleeper sees. She said: Did you find (any mark of the fluid) on your clothes? I said: No. She said: Had you found anything you should have washed it. Incase I found that (semen) on the garment of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) dried up, I scraped it off with my nails</p>
