<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">This source entry preserves two separately attributed reports in their original order. They remain one provider entry, with their distinct narrator introductions and source references. Report type: companion report. Attribution: Anas describing the Companions dozing and prayer, as transmitted by Qatada. The source supplies the Companions actions and then an actual hearing-verification question and answer. Keep these distinct from a Prophetic command, which this field does not supply. Report type: historical report. Attribution: The questioner and Qatada in the explicitly reported hearing-verification exchange. The source supplies the Companions actions and then an actual hearing-verification question and answer. Keep these distinct from a Prophetic command, which this field does not supply.</p>

<p class="counterpart-note">Source report 1</p>

<p dir="ltr">Qatida reported: I heard Anas as saying that the Companion of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) dozed off and then offered prayer and did not perform ablution. </p>

<p class="counterpart-note">Source report 2</p>

<p dir="ltr">He (the narrator) said: I asked him if he had actually heard it from Anas. He said: By Allah. yes</p>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنِي إِسْحَاقُ بْنُ مَنْصُورٍ، أَخْبَرَنَا أَبُو جَعْفَرٍ، مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ جَهْضَمٍ الثَّقَفِيُّ حَدَّثَنَا إِسْمَاعِيلُ بْنُ جَعْفَرٍ، عَنْ عُمَارَةَ بْنِ غَزِيَّةَ، عَنْ خُبَيْبِ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ إِسَافٍ، عَنْ حَفْصِ بْنِ عَاصِمِ بْنِ عُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ جَدِّهِ، عُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ &quot;‏ إِذَا قَالَ الْمُؤَذِّنُ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ ‏.‏ فَقَالَ أَحَدُكُمُ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ ‏.‏ ثُمَّ قَالَ أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ ‏.‏ قَالَ أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ ثُمَّ قَالَ أَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ‏.‏ قَالَ أَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ‏.‏ ثُمَّ قَالَ حَىَّ عَلَى الصَّلاَةِ ‏.‏ قَالَ لاَ حَوْلَ وَلاَ قُوَّةَ إِلاَّ بِاللَّهِ ‏.‏ ثُمَّ قَالَ حَىَّ عَلَى الْفَلاَحِ ‏.‏ قَالَ لاَ حَوْلَ وَلاَ قُوَّةَ إِلاَّ بِاللَّهِ ‏.‏ ثُمَّ قَالَ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ ‏.‏ قَالَ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ ‏.‏ ثُمَّ قَالَ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ ‏.‏ قَالَ لاَ إِلَهَ إِلاَّ اللَّهُ ‏.‏ مِنْ قَلْبِهِ دَخَلَ الْجَنَّةَ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Umar b. al-Khattab reported: The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said: When the Mu&#x27;adhdhin says: Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest, and one of you should make this response: Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest; (and when the Mu&#x27;adhdhin) says: I testify that there is no god but Allah, one should respond: I testify that there is no god but Allah, and when he says: I testify that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, one should make a response: I testify that Muhammad is Allah&#x27;s Messenger. When he (the Mu&#x27;adhdhin) says: Come to prayer, one should make a response: There is no might and no power except with Allah. When he (the Mu&#x27;adhdhin) says: Come to salvation, one should respond: There is no might and no power except with Allah, and when he (the Mu&#x27;adhdhin) says: Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest, then make a response: Allah is the Greatest, Allah is the Greatest. When he (the Mu&#x27;adhdhin) says: There is no god but Allah, and he who makes a re- sponse from the heart: There is no god but Allah, he will enter Paradise</p>
