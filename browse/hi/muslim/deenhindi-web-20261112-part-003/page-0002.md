<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It has been reported on the authority of Nafi, that &#x27;Abdullah bin Umar paid a visit to Abdullah bin Muti&#x27; in the days (when atrocities were perpetrated on the People Of Medina) at Harra in the time of Yazid bin Mu&#x27;awiya. Ibn Muti&#x27; said: Place a pillow for Abu &#x27;Abd al-Rahman (family name of &#x27;Abdullah b. &#x27;Umar). But the latter said: I have not come to sit with you. I have come to you to tell you a tradition I heard from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him). I heard him say: One who withdraws his band from obedience (to the Amir) will find no argument (in his defence) when he stands before Allah on the Day of Judgment, and one who dies without having bound himself by an oath of allegiance (to an Amir) will die the death of one belonging to the days of Jahillyya</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Muslim · 4793]</p>

<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">नाफी (रज़ि अल्लाहु अन्हु) से रिवायत है, अब्दुल्लाह बिन उमर (रज़ि अल्लाहु अन्हु), अब्दुल्लाह बिन मुती (रज़ि अल्लाहु अन्हु) के पास आए, जब हर्रा का वकिया हुआ यज़ीद बिन मुआविया के ज़माने में। अब्दुल्लाह बिन मुती ने कहा, अबू अब्दुर रहमान (ये अब्दुल्लाह बिन उमर की कुनियत है) के लिए तकिया बिछाओ। उन्होंने कहा, मैं इसलिए नहीं आया की बैठूं, बल्कि एक हदीस तुम्हें सुनाने के लिए आया हूं जो मैंने रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) से सुनी है, आप फरमाते थे: जो शख्स अपना हाथ निकाल ले इताअत से, वो कयामत के दिन खुदा से मिलेगा और कोई दलील उसके पास न होगी, और जो शख्स मर जाए और किसी से उसने बैअत न की हो, तो उसकी मौत जाहिलियत की सी होगी।</p>

<p dir="auto">[Sahih Muslim · 4793]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It has been narrated on the authority of Auf bin Malik al-Ashja&#x27;i who said that he heard the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) say: The best of your rulers are those whom you love and who love you, upon whom you invoke God&#x27;s blessings and who invoke His blessing upon you. And the worst of your rulers are those whom you hate and who hate you, who curse you and whom you curse. (Those present) said: Shouldn&#x27;t we overthrow them at this? He said: No, as long as they establish prayer among you. No, as long as they establish prayer among you. Mind you! One who has a governor appointed over him and he finds that the governor indulges in an act of disobedience to God, he should condemn the governor&#x27;s act, in disobedience to God, but should not withdraw himself from his obedience. Ibn Jabir said: Ruzaiq narrated to me this hadith. I asked him: Abu Miqdam, have you heard it from Muslim bin Qaraza or did he describe it to you and he heard it from &#x27;Auf (b. Malik) and he transmitted this tradition of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him)? Upon this Ruzaiq sat upon his knees and facing the Qibla said: By Allah, besides Whom there is no other God, I heard it from Muslim bin Qaraza and he said that te had heard it from Auf (b. Malik) and he said that he had heard it from the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him)</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It has been narrated on the authority of &#x27;A&#x27;isha, the wife of the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him). She said: When the believing women migrated (to Medina) and came to the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him), they would be tested in accordance with the following words of Allah. the Almighty and Exalted: &quot; O Prophet, when believing women come to thee to take the oath of fealty to thee that they will not associate in worship anything with God, that they will not steal. that, they will not commit adultery...&quot; to the end of the verse (lx. 62). Whoso from the believing women accepted these conditions and agreed to abide by them were considered to have offered themselves for swearing fealty. When they had (formally) declared their resolve to do so, the Messenger of Allah (may peace he upon him) would say to them: You may go. I have confirmed your fealty. By God, the hand of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) never touched the hand of a woman. He would take the oath of fealty from them by oral declaration. By God, the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) never took any vow from women except that which God had ordered him to take, and his palm never touched the palm of a woman. When he had taken their vow, he would tell them that he had taken the oath from them orally</p>
