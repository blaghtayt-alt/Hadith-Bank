<p dir="auto">‘Abdullāh ibn ‘Umar (may Allah be pleased with him) reported that a man from the Bedouins met him on his way to Makkah. ‘Abdullāh ibn ‘Umar greeted him, made him ride on a donkey that he was riding, and gave him a turban that was wrapped around his head. Ibn Dinār said: &quot;We said to him: &#x27;May Allah grant you righteousness! These people are Bedouins and they are pleased with much less.&#x27;&quot; ‘Abdullāh ibn ‘Umar said: &quot;This man’s father was a dear friend of ‘Umar ibn al-Khattāb (may Allah be pleased with him) and I heard the Messenger of Allah (may Allah’s peace and blessings be upon him) say: &#x27;The finest form of dutifulness is maintaining relations with those whom one’s father loved.&#x27;&quot;<br>
In another narration by Ibn Dinār, he reported that when Ibn ‘Umar set out to Makkah, he kept a donkey with him that he would ride when he became tired of riding his camel, and had a turban that he would tie around his head. One day, while he was riding this donkey, a Bedouin passed by him. Ibn ‘Umar asked him: &quot;Aren’t you so and so the son of so and so?&quot; He said: &#x27;Yes.&#x27; So he gave him his donkey and said: &quot;Ride this,&quot; and he gave him his turban and said: &quot;Tie this around your head.&quot; Some of his companions said to him: &quot;May Allah forgive you! You gave this Bedouin a donkey that you would rest on, and a turban that you would tie your head with?&quot; He said: &quot;I heard the Messenger of Allah (may Allah’s peace and blessings be upon him) say: &quot;The finest form of dutifulness is maintaining relations with those whom one’s father loved after his death”, and his father was a friend of ‘Umar (may Allah be pleased with him).</p>
