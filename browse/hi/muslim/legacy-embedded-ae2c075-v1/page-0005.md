<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English · full narration</h3>

<p dir="auto">‘Ali (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: &quot;We were attending a funeral at Baqī‘ aI-Gharqad (Muslim cemetery in Madīnah). The Messenger of Allah (may Allah&#x27;s peace and blessings be upon him) came to us and sat down, and we sat down around him. He had a small stick in his hand. He lowered his head and started scraping the ground with the stick, then said: &quot;There is none among you, except that his seat in Paradise or in Hell is decreed.&quot; They said: &quot;O Messenger of Allah, should we depend on what has been decreed for us?&quot; The Prophet (may Allah&#x27;s peace and blessings be upon him) said: &quot;Exert the effort, for everyone will find it easy to do such deeds that will lead him to what he was created for...&quot; Then he mentioned the rest of the Hadīth.</p>

<p dir="auto">[Sahih Muslim no. 2647a]</p>
