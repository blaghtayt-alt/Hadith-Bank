<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu&#x27;l-Bakhtari reported: We saw the new moon of Ramadan as we were at Dhit-i-&#x27;Irq. We sent a man to Ibn Abbas (Allah be pleased with both of them) to ask him (whether the sighting of a small moon had something of the nature of defect in it). Upon this Ibn &#x27;Abbas (Allah be pleased with both of them) said that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) had said: Verily Allah deferred its sight, but if (the new moon) is hidden from you, then, complete its number (thirty)</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Muslim · 2530]</p>
