<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">A&#x27;isha (Allah be pleased with her) reported: When we came to Medina, and it was an unhealthy, uncogenial place, Abu Bakr fell sick and Bilal also fell sick; and when Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) saw the illness of his Companions he said: O Allah, make Medina as congenial to us as you made Mecca congenial or more than that; make it conducive to health, and bleesus in its sa&#x27; and in its mudd, and transfer its fever to al-juhfa</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Muslim · 3342]</p>
