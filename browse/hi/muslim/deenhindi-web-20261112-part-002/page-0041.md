<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Huraira (Allah be pleased with him) reported: Prayer in the mosque of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) is more excellent than a thousand prayers in other mosques except the Masjid al-Haram, for Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) is the last of the Apostles, and his mosque is the last of the mosques. Abu Salama and Abu Abdullah (two of the narrators in this chain of narrations said: We had no doubt that what Abu Haraira (Allah be pleased with him) had said was from Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him), and so we did not like to get an attestation from Abu Huraira about this hadith until Abu Huraira (Allah be pleased with him) died. We discussed it (the issue of getting attestation from Abu Huraira) amongst ourselves and blamed one another as to why we did not talk about it to Abu Huraira regarding it so that he could attribute its transmission to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) in case he had heard It from him. While we were discussing it as we sat with &#x27;Abdullah bin Ibrahlm b. Qariz; we made a mention of this hadith, and our omission (in getting its attestation) about its direct transmission by Abu Huraira from him (the Holy Proohet) ; thereupon Abdullah bin Ibrahim said to us: I bear witness to the fact that I heard Abu Huraira (Allah be pleased with him) say that Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said: I am the last of the Apostles and my mosque is the last of the mosques</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ur-Latn">
<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Salama bin Abdur Rahman aur Juhaina walon ke azad karda ghulam Abu Abdullah Aghar se riwayat hai... aur ye Hazrat Abu Huraira (Radi Allahu Anhu) ke sathiyon mein se the... un dono ne Abu Huraira (Radi Allahu Anhu) se suna, wo keh rahe the: Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki masjid mein ek namaz Masjid-e-Haram ko chhod kar dusri masajid mein ek hazar namazon se afzal hai. Bila shuba Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) tamam anbiya (Alaihimussalam) mein se aakhri hain, aur Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki masjid (bhi kisi nabi ki tameer karda) aakhri masjid hai. Abu Salama aur Abu Abdullah ne kaha: Hamein is bare mein shak na tha ki Abu Huraira (Radi Allahu Anhu) ye baat Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki hadith se bayan kar rahe hain, chunanche isi baat ne hamein roke rakha ki hum Abu Huraira (Radi Allahu Anhu) se is hadith ke bare mein (Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se sama&#x27; ka) isbat karayein, hatta ki jab Abu Huraira (Radi Allahu Anhu) faut ho gaye toh humne aapas mein is baat ka tazkira kiya aur ek dusre ko malamat ki ki humne Abu Huraira (Radi Allahu Anhu) se is bare mein guftagu kyun nahi ki, taaki agar unhone ye hadith Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se suni thi toh iski nisbat Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki taraf kardein. Hum isi kaifiyat mein the ki Abdullah bin Ibrahim bin Qariz hamare sath majlis mein aa baithe, toh humne is hadith ka aur jis baat ke bare mein Abu Huraira (Radi Allahu Anhu) se sarahat karane mein humne kotahi ki thi ka tazkira kiya, toh Abdullah bin Ibrahim bin Qariz ne hamein kaha: Main gawahi deta hoon ki main ne Hazrat Abu Huraira (Radi Allahu Anhu) se suna, wo keh rahe the: Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya: Bila shuba main tamam anbiya (Alaihimussalam) mein se aakhri nabi hoon aur meri masjid aakhri masjid hai (jise kisi nabi ne tameer kiya).</p>

<p dir="ltr">Ibn Abbas (Allah be pleased with them) reported that a woman fell ill and she said: In case Allah cures me I will certainly go and observe prayer in Bait al-Maqdis. <p dir="auto">लैस ने नाफ़ेअ से रिवायत की, उन्होंने इब्राहिम बिन अब्दुल्लाह बिन मअबाद (बिन अब्बास) से रिवायत की, (कहा:) हज़रत इब्न-ए-अब्बास (रज़ियल्लाहु अन्हु) से रिवायत है के उन्होंने कहा: एक औरत बीमार हो गई, उसने कहा: अगर अल्लाह ने मुझे शिफ़ा दी तो मैं बैत-उल-मक़दिस में जा कर ज़रूर नमाज़ अदा करूंगी। वो सेहत-याब हो गई, फिर सफ़र के इरादे से तैयारी की (सफ़र से पहले) वो नबी (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) की ज़ौजा हज़रत मैमूना (रज़ियल्लाहु अन्हा) की ख़िदमत में सलाम कहने के लिए हाज़िर हुई और उन्हें ये सब बताया तो हज़रत मैमूना (रज़ियल्लाहु अन्हा) ने उस से कहा: बैठ जाओ और जो (ज़द-ए-राह) तुमने तय्यार किया है वो खा लो और रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) की मस्जिद में नमाज़ पढ़ लो। बिला शुबा! मैंने रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) से सुना, आप फ़रमा रहे थे: इस (मस्जिद) में एक नमाज़ पढ़ना इसके सिवा (बाक़ी) तमाम मसाजिद में एक हज़ार नमाज़ें अदा करने से अफ़ज़ल है सिवाए मस्जिद-ए-कअबा के।</p>
