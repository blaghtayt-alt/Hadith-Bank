<p dir="ltr">Rafi bin Khadij (Allah be pleased with him) reported: We used to give on rent land during the lifetime of Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him). We rented it on the share of one-third or one-fourth of the (produce) along with a definite quantity of corn.
