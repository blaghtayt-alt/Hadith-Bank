<p dir="ltr">Mujahid reported: I and &#x27;Urwah bin Zubair entered the mosque and found &#x27;Abdullah b. &#x27;Umar sitting near the apartment of A&#x27;ishah and the people were observing the forenoon prayer (when the sun had sufficiently risen).
