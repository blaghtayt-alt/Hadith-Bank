<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Zayd ibn Aslam from Ibrahim ibn Abdullah ibn Hunayn from his father Abdullah ibn Hunayn that Abdullah ibn Abbas and al-Miswar ibn Makhrama once had a disagreement at al-Abwa. Abdullah said that some one in ihram could wash his head, and al Miswar ibn Makhrama maintained that some one in ihram could not wash his head. Abdullah ibn Hunayn continued, &quot;Abdullah ibn Abbas sent me to Abu Ayyub al-Ansari, and I found him doing ghusl between the posts of a well, screened by a garment. I greeted him and hesaid, &#x27;Who is that?&#x27; I replied, &#x27;I am &#x27;Abdullah ibn Hunayn. &#x27;Abdullah ibn Abbas sent me to you to ask how the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, used to wash his head when he was in ihram.&#x27; &quot; He continued, &quot;Abu Ayyub put his hand on the garment and pulled it down until I could see his head. He said to the man who was pouring out the water for him, &#x27;Pour,&#x27; and he poured some over his head. Then he passed his hands over his head from the front to the back and then to the front again, and then said, &#x27;I saw the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, doing it like this</p>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Nafi from Abdullah ibn Umar that a man once asked the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, what clothes someone in ihram could wear, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;Do not wear shirts, turbans, trousers, burnouses, or leather socks, except if you cannot find sandals. In that case you can wear leather socks, but cut them off below the ankles. Do not wear any clothes that have been touched by saffron or yellow dye.&quot; Yahya said that Malik was asked about the hadith attributed to the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, &quot;Whoever cannot find a waist wrapper should wear trousers,&quot; and he said, &quot;I have never heard this, and I do not think that some one who is in ihram can wear trousers, because among the things which the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, forbade some one in ihram to wear were trousers, and he did not make any exception for them although he did make an exception for leather socks.&quot; 20.4 Wearing Clothes when in Ihram</p>

<p dir="auto">Report type: historical report. Attribution: Salim ibn Abdullah (successor); Kharija ibn Zayd ibn Thabit (successor). The source describes two named respondents giving differing answers within one shared question-and-answer incident. Their disagreement is preserved; subsequent compiler rules remain notes.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Yahya ibn Said and &#x27;Abdullah ibn Abi Bakr and Rabia ibn Abi Abd ar-Rahman that al-Walid ibn Abd al- Malik asked Salim ibn Abdullah and Kharija ibn Zayd ibn Thabit if he could use perfume after he had stoned the jamra and shaved his head, but before he had left for the tawafal-ifada. Salim forbade him to do so, but Kharija ibn Zayd ibn Thabit said that he could.</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">وَحَدَّثَنِي يَحْيَى، عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ يُهِلُّ أَهْلُ الْمَدِينَةِ مِنْ ذِي الْحُلَيْفَةِ وَيُهِلُّ أَهْلُ الشَّامِ مِنَ الْجُحْفَةِ وَيُهِلُّ أَهْلُ نَجْدٍ مِنْ قَرْنٍ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ قَالَ عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ عُمَرَ وَبَلَغَنِي أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ وَيُهِلُّ أَهْلُ الْيَمَنِ مِنْ يَلَمْلَمَ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏</p>
