<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنِي يَحْيَى، عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ يَحْيَى بْنِ سَعِيدٍ، أَنَّهُ قَالَ دَخَلَ أَعْرَابِيٌّ الْمَسْجِدَ فَكَشَفَ عَنْ فَرْجِهِ لِيَبُولَ فَصَاحَ النَّاسُ بِهِ حَتَّى عَلاَ الصَّوْتُ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ &quot;‏ اتْرُكُوهُ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَتَرَكُوهُ فَبَالَ ثُمَّ أَمَرَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِذَنُوبٍ مِنْ مَاءٍ فَصُبَّ عَلَى ذَلِكَ الْمَكَانِ ‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik that Yahya ibn Said said, &quot;A Bedouin came into the mosque and uncovered his private parts to urinate. The people called out to him and began to raise their voices but the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said ,&#x27;Let him be. &#x27;So they let him be and he urinated. Then the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, ordered a bucketful of water to be brought and it was poured on the place</p>

<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya bin Saeed Ansari se riwayat hai ke ek a&#x27;rabi masjid mein aaya aur apna satr khola peshab ke liye, to logon ne ghul machaya aur bada pukara. Jab wo peshab kar chuka to aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne hukum kiya, ek dol paani ka us jagah daal diya gaya.</p>
