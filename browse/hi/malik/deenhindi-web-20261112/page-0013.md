<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Ibn Shihab from Anas ibn Malik that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, fell off his horse when riding, and his right side was scraped, so he did one of the prayers sitting, and we prayed behind him sitting. When he left, he said, &quot;The imam is appointed to be followed. If he prays standing, then pray standing, and when he goes into ruku, go into ruku, and when he rises, rise, and when he says, &#x27;Allah hears whoever praises him,&#x27; say, &#x27;Our Lord, praise belongs to You,&#x27; and if he prays sitting, then all of you pray sitting</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Muwatta Malik · 303]</p>

<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Anas bin Malik (Radi Allahu Anhu) se riwayat hai ke Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) sawar hue ek ghoday par, pas gir paday us par se toh chhil gaya daahini jaanib aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki. Phir jab farigh hue aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) namaz se toh farmaya ke imam is liye muqarrar kiya gaya hai ke uski pairwi ki jaye aur jab imam kharay ho kar namaz parhay toh tum bhi kharay ho kar padho aur jab imam ruku kare toh tum bhi ruku karo aur jab imam sar uthaye toh tum bhi sar uthao aur jab imam Sami Allahu liman hamida kahe toh tum Rabbana lakal hamd kaho aur jab imam baith kar namaz parhay toh tum bhi baith kar padho.</p>
