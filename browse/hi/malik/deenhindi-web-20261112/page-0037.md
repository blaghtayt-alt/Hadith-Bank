<p dir="auto">Report type: companion report. Attribution: Umar ibn al-Khattab (companion). The source attributes this numbered report to the named speaker or actor; it is not relabelled as the Prophet’s words.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Yahya ibn Said that he had heard that on the day after the day of sacrifice Umar ibn al-Khattab went out a little after the sun had risen and said the takbir, and everyone repeated it after him. Then he went out a second time the same day when the sun was well up and said the takbir, and everyone repeated it after him. Then he went out a third time after mid-day and said the takbir, and everyone repeated it after him until it resounded from group to group until it reached the House and people knew that Umar had left to throw the stones.</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Abd ar-Rahman ibn al-Qasim, from his father that A&#x27;isha, umm al-muminin, said, &quot;We set out with the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, in the year of the farewell hajj and we went into ihram for umra. Afterwards, the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &#x27;Whoever has a sacrificial animal with him should go into ihram for hajj and umra together, and he should not leave ihram without leaving ihram for both of them at the same time.&#x27; &quot; She continued &quot;I was menstruating when I got to Makka, so I did not do tawaf of the House or say between Safa and Marwa. I complained to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and he said, &#x27;Undo your hair and comb it and leave the umra and go back into ihram for the hajj.&#x27; &quot; She said, &quot;I did so, and when we had completed the hajj, the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, sent me with Abd ar-Rahman ibn Abi Bakr as-Siddiq to at-Tanim and I performed an umra and he said, &#x27;This is in place of your umra.&#x27; &quot; &quot;Those who had entered ihram for the umra did tawaf of the House and say between Safa and Marwa, then left ihram. Then they did another tawaf after returning from Mina for their hajj, whereas those who entered ihram for the hajj or combined the hajj and the umra, only did one tawaf.&quot; Yahya related the same as that to me from Malik from Ibn Shihab from Urwa ibn az-Zubayr from A&#x27;isha</p>

<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">हज़रत उम्मुल मोमिनीन आयशा (रदियल्लाहु अन्हा) से रिवायत है के हम निकले रसूलुल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) के साथ हज्जत-उल-विदा के साल में तो एहराम बांधा हमने उमराह का। फिर फरमाया रसूलुल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने जिस शख्स के साथ हदी हो तो वो एहराम हज और उमराह का साथ बांधे पर एहराम न खोले यहां तक के दोनों से फारिग हो। हज़रत आयशा ने कहा के मैं आयी मक्का में हैज़ की हालत में तो मैंने न तवाफ किया न सई की सफा-मरवाह की और शिकायत की मैंने रसूलुल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) से, आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने फरमाया अपना सर खोल डाल और कंघी कर और उमराह छोड़ दे और हज का एहराम बांध ले, मैंने वैसा ही किया। जब हम हज कर चुके तो रसूलुल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने मुझको अब्दुल रहमान बिन अबी बक्र के साथ तनीम को भेजा, मैंने उमराह अदा किया और रसूलुल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने फरमाया उमराह इवज़ है तेरे इस उमराह का। तो जिन लोगों ने उमराह का एहराम बांधा था वो तवाफ और सई करके हलाल हो गये फिर हज के वास्ते दूसरा तवाफ किया जब लौट कर आये मिना से, और जिन लोगों ने हज का एहराम बांधा था या हज और उमराह का एक साथ बांधा था उन्होंने एक ही तवाफ किया। उर्वा बिन जुबैर ने भी हज़रत आयशा (रदियल्लाहु अन्हा) से ऐसा ही रिवायत किया।</p>

<p dir="ltr">Hazrat Ummul Momineen Ayesha (Radi Allahu Anha) se riwayat hai ke hum nikle Rasoolullah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ke saath hajjat-ul-wida ke saal mein toh ehram bandha humne umrah ka. Hazrat Ayesha ne kaha ke main aayi Makkah mein haiz ki halat mein toh maine na tawaf kiya na sa&#x27;ee ki Safa-Marwah ki aur shikayat ki maine Rasoolullah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se, Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya apna sar khol daal aur kanghi kar aur umrah chhod de aur hajj ka ehram bandh le, maine waisa hi kiya. Toh jin logon ne umrah ka ehram bandha tha woh tawaf aur sa&#x27;ee karke halaal ho gaye phir hajj ke waste dusra tawaf kiya jab laut kar aaye Mina se, aur jin logon ne hajj ka ehram bandha tha ya hajj aur umrah ka ek saath bandha tha unhone ek hi tawaf kiya. Urwa bin Zubair ne bhi Hazrat Ayesha (Radi Allahu Anha) se aisa hi riwayat kiya.</p>
