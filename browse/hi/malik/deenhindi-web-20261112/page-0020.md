<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: prophetic report. Attribution: The Prophet in Urwa ibn az-Zubayrs supplied revelation-setting account. The full field supplies the encounter with Ibn Umm Maktum and the leading idol worshipper, dialogue and reported revelation setting. Keep its actual source chain and attribution without creating missing intermediaries.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Hisham ibn Urwa that his father said that Abasa (Sura 80) was sent down about Abdullah ibn Umm Maktum. He came to the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and began to say, &quot;O Muhammad, show me a place near you (where I can sit),&quot; whilst one of the leading men of the idol worshippers was in audience with the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. The Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, began to turn away from him and give his attention to the other man, and he said to him, &quot;Father of so-and-so, do you see any harm in what I am saying?&quot; and he said, &quot;No, by the blood (of our sacrifices) I see no harm in what you are saying.&quot; And Abasa - &quot;He frowned and turned away when the blind man came&quot; - was sent down</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Zayd ibn Aslam from his father that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was on one of his journeys, and one night Umar ibn al-Khattab, who was travelling with him, asked him about something, but he did not answer him. He asked him again, but he did not answer him. Then he asked him again, and again he did not answer him. Umar said, &quot;May your mother be bereaved of you, Umar. Three times you have importuned the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, with a question and he has not answered you at all.&quot; Umar continued, &quot;I got my camel moving until, when I was in front of the people, I feared that a piece of Qur&#x27;an was being sent down about me. It was not long before I heard a crier calling for me, and I said that I feared that a piece of Qur&#x27;an had been sent down about me.&quot; He continued, &quot;I came to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and said, &#x27;Peace be upon you&#x27; to him, and he said, &#x27;A sura has been sent down to me this night that is more beloved to me than anything on which the sun rises.&#x27; Then he recited al-Fath (Sura)</p>
