<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">وَحَدَّثَنِي عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنِ الْعَلاَءِ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ أَبِي هُرَيْرَةَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم خَرَجَ إِلَى الْمَقْبُرَةِ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ السَّلاَمُ عَلَيْكُمْ دَارَ قَوْمٍ مُؤْمِنِينَ وَإِنَّا إِنْ شَاءَ اللَّهُ بِكُمْ لاَحِقُونَ وَدِدْتُ أَنِّي قَدْ رَأَيْتُ إِخْوَانَنَا ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَقَالُوا يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ أَلَسْنَا بِإِخْوَانِكَ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ بَلْ أَنْتُمْ أَصْحَابِي وَإِخْوَانُنَا الَّذِينَ لَمْ يَأْتُوا بَعْدُ وَأَنَا فَرَطُهُمْ عَلَى الْحَوْضِ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَقَالُوا يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ كَيْفَ تَعْرِفُ مَنْ يَأْتِي بَعْدَكَ مِنْ أُمَّتِكَ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ أَرَأَيْتَ لَوْ كَانَ لِرَجُلٍ خَيْلٌ غُرٌّ مُحَجَّلَةٌ فِي خَيْلٍ دُهْمٍ بُهْمٍ أَلاَ يَعْرِفُ خَيْلَهُ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ قَالُوا بَلَى يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ فَإِنَّهُمْ يَأْتُونَ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ غُرًّا مُحَجَّلِينَ مِنَ الْوُضُوءِ وَأَنَا فَرَطُهُمْ عَلَى الْحَوْضِ فَلاَ يُذَادَنَّ رِجَالٌ عَنْ حَوْضِي كَمَا يُذَادُ الْبَعِيرُ الضَّالُّ أُنَادِيهِمْ أَلاَ هَلُمَّ أَلاَ هَلُمَّ أَلاَ هَلُمَّ فَيُقَالُ إِنَّهُمْ قَدْ بَدَّلُوا بَعْدَكَ فَأَقُولُ فَسُحْقًا فَسُحْقًا فَسُحْقًا ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏</p>

<p dir="rtl">[Muwatta Malik · 58]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from al-Ala ibn Abd ar-Rahman from his father from Abu Hurayra that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, went to the burial grounds and said, &quot;Peace be upon you, home of a people who believe! We shall be among you, Allah willing. I wish that I had seen our brothers!&quot; The people with him said, &quot;Messenger of Allah! Are we not your brothers?&quot; &quot;No,&quot; he said, &quot;You are my companions. Our brothers are those who have not yet come. And I will precede them to the Hawd. (The Hawd:the watering place of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, from which he will give to the people of his community on the day of rising.)&quot; They asked him, &quot;Messenger of Allah! How will you recognise those of your community who come after you?&quot; He said, &quot;Doesn&#x27;t a man who has horses with white legs and white blazes on their foreheads among totally black horses recognise which ones are his own?&quot; They said, &quot;Of course, Messenger of Allah.&quot; He went on, &quot;Even so will they come on the day of rising with white marks on their foreheads, hands and feet from wudu, and I will precede them to the Hawd. Some men will be driven away from the Hawd as if they were straying camels and I shall call out to them, &#x27;Will you not come? Will you not come? Will you not come?&#x27; and someone will say, &#x27;They changed things after you,&#x27; so I shall say, &#x27;Then away with them, away with them, away with them</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="hi">
<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">अबू हुरैराह (रदी अल्लाहु अन्हु) से रिवायत है के रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) गए मक़बरा को, सो कहा सलाम है तुम्हारे पर ऐ क़ौम मोमिनों की और हम अगर अल्लाह चाहे तोह तुमसे मिलने वाले हैं। तमन्ना की मैंने के मैं देख लूं अपने भाइयों को, तोह कहा सहाबा ने, या रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) क्या नहीं हैं हम भाई? आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने फ़रमाया, बल्कि तुम भाइयों से बढ़कर असहाब हो मेरे, और भाई मेरे वोह लोग हैं जो अभी नहीं आये दुनिया में और मैं क़यामत के रोज़ उनका पेश-ख़ैमा होऊंगा हौज़-ए-कौसर पर। तब कहा सहाबा ने, या रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) क्योंकर पहचानेंगे उन लोगों को क़यामत के रोज़ जो दुनिया में बाद आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) के पैदा होंगे उम्मत में? आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने फ़रमाया के तुम मुझको बतलाओ के किसी शख्श के सफ़ेद मुंह और सफ़ेद पांव के घोड़े ख़ालिस मिश्की घोड़ों में मिल जाएं, क्या वोह अपने घोड़े न पहचानेगा? कहा सहाबा ने, पहचानेगा। पस फ़रमाया आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने के क़यामत के रोज़ भाई मेरे आएंगे, चमकते होंगे मुंह और पांव उनके वज़ू से और मैं उनका पेश-ख़ैमा होऊंगा हौज़-ए-कौसर पर। तोह ऐसा न हो के कोई शख्श निकाला जाए मेरे हौज़ से जैसे निकाला जाता है वोह ऊंट जो अपने मालिक से छूट गया हो, तोह पुकारूंगा मैं उनको, इधर आओ इधर आओ। कहा जाएगा मुझसे के इन लोगों ने बदल दिया सुन्नत तेरी को बाद तेरे, तब मैं कहने लगूंगा, दूर हो दूर हो।</p>

<p dir="auto">[Muwatta Malik · 58]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ur-Latn">
<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Hurairah (Radi Allahu Anhu) se riwayat hai ke Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) gaye maqbara ko, so kaha salam hai tumhare par aye qaum momino ki aur hum agar Allah chahe toh tumse milne wale hain. Tamanna ki maine ke main dekh loon apne bhaiyon ko, toh kaha sahaba ne, Ya Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) kya nahi hain hum bhai? Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya, balki tum bhaiyon se badhkar ashaab ho mere, aur bhai mere woh log hain jo abhi nahi aaye duniya mein aur main qayamat ke roz unka pesh-khaima hounga hauz-e-kauthar par. Tab kaha sahaba ne, Ya Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) kyonkar pehchanenge un logon ko qayamat ke roz jo duniya mein baad aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ke paida honge ummat mein? Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya ke tum mujhko batlao ke kisi shakhs ke safed munh aur safed paon ke ghode khaalis mishki ghodon mein mil jayen, kya woh apne ghode na pehchanega? Kaha sahaba ne, pehchanega. Pas farmaya aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne ke qayamat ke roz bhai mere aayenge, chamakte honge munh aur paon unke wazu se aur main unka pesh-khaima hounga hauz-e-kauthar par. Toh aisa na ho ke koi shakhs nikala jaye mere hauz se jaise nikala jata hai woh oont jo apne maalik se chhoot gaya ho, toh pukaruunga main unko, idhar aao idhar aao. Kaha jayega mujhse ke in logon ne badal diya sunnat teri ko baad tere, tab main kehne lagunga, door ho door ho.</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Muwatta Malik · 58]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Hisham ibn Urwa from his father from Humran, the mawla of Uthman ibn Affan, that Uthman ibn Affan was once sitting on the Maqaid (the benches surrounding the Madina Mosque, or else a stone near Uthman ibn Affan&#x27;s house where he sat to discuss with people), when the muadhdhin came and told him that it was time for the asr prayer. I heard the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, say, &#x27;If a man does wudu, and makes sure he does it correctly, and then does the prayer, he will be forgiven everything that he does between then and the time when he prays the next prayer.&#x27; &quot; Yahya said that Malik said, &quot;I believe he meant this ayat - &#x27;Establish prayer at the two ends of the day and in some watches of the night. <h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Ibn Shihab from Abbad ibn Ziyad, a descendant of al-Mughira ibn Shuba from his father from al Mughira ibn Shuba that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, went to relieve himself during the expedition of Tabuk. Mughira said, &quot;I went with him, taking water. Then the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, came back and I poured out the water for him. He washed his hands and then went to push his hands out of the sleeves of his garment, but could not do so because of their narrowness. So he brought them out from underneath his garment. Then he washed his arms, wiped his head and wiped over his leather socks. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, returned and Abdar Rahman ibn Awf was leading the people in prayer, and he had already finished one raka with them. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, prayed the remaining raka with them to everyone&#x27;s concern. When the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, finished he said, &#x27;You have acted correctly</p>
