<p dir="rtl">وَحَدَّثَنِي عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ أَسْلَمَ، أَنَّهُ قَالَ عَرَّسَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم لَيْلَةً بِطَرِيقِ مَكَّةَ وَوَكَّلَ بِلاَلاً أَنْ يُوقِظَهُمْ لِلصَّلاَةِ فَرَقَدَ بِلاَلٌ وَرَقَدُوا حَتَّى اسْتَيْقَظُوا وَقَدْ طَلَعَتْ عَلَيْهِمُ الشَّمْسُ فَاسْتَيْقَظَ الْقَوْمُ وَقَدْ فَزِعُوا فَأَمَرَهُمْ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنْ يَرْكَبُوا حَتَّى يَخْرُجُوا مِنْ ذَلِكَ الْوَادِي وَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ إِنَّ هَذَا وَادٍ بِهِ شَيْطَانٌ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَرَكِبُوا حَتَّى خَرَجُوا مِنْ ذَلِكَ الْوَادِي ثُمَّ أَمَرَهُمْ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَنْ يَنْزِلُوا وَأَنْ يَتَوَضَّئُوا وَأَمَرَ بِلاَلاً أَنْ يُنَادِيَ بِالصَّلاَةِ أَوْ يُقِيمَ فَصَلَّى رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِالنَّاسِ ثُمَّ انْصَرَفَ إِلَيْهِمْ وَقَدْ رَأَى مِنْ فَزَعِهِمْ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ إِنَّ اللَّهَ قَبَضَ أَرْوَاحَنَا وَلَوْ شَاءَ لَرَدَّهَا إِلَيْنَا فِي حِينٍ غَيْرِ هَذَا فَإِذَا رَقَدَ أَحَدُكُمْ عَنِ الصَّلاَةِ أَوْ نَسِيَهَا ثُمَّ فَزِعَ إِلَيْهَا فَلْيُصَلِّهَا كَمَا كَانَ يُصَلِّيهَا فِي وَقْتِهَا ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ ثُمَّ الْتَفَتَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم إِلَى أَبِي بَكْرٍ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ إِنَّ الشَّيْطَانَ أَتَى بِلاَلاً وَهُوَ قَائِمٌ يُصَلِّي فَأَضْجَعَهُ فَلَمْ يَزَلْ يُهَدِّئُهُ كَمَا يُهَدَّأُ الصَّبِيُّ حَتَّى نَامَ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ ثُمَّ دَعَا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِلاَلاً فَأَخْبَرَ بِلاَلٌ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مِثْلَ الَّذِي أَخْبَرَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَبَا بَكْرٍ فَقَالَ أَبُو بَكْرٍ أَشْهَدُ أَنَّكَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik that Zayd ibn Aslam said, &quot;The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, stopped for a rest one night on the way to Makka and appointed Bilal to wake them up for the prayer. Bilal slept and everyone else slept and none of them woke up until the sun had risen. When they did wake up they were all alarmed. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, ordered them to ride out of the valley, saying that there was a shaytan in it. So they rode out of the valley and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, ordered them to dismount and do wudu and he told Bilal either to call the prayer or to give the iqama. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, then led them in the prayer. Noticing their uneasiness, he went to them and said, &#x27;O people! Allah seized our spirits (arwah) and if He had wished He would have returned them to us at a time other than this. So if you sleep through the time for a prayer or forget it and then are anxious about it, pray it as if you were praying it in its time.&#x27; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, turned to Abu Bakr and said, &#x27;Shaytan came to Bilal when he was standing in prayer and made him lie down and lulled him to sleep like a small boy.&#x27; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, then called Bilal and told him the same as he had told Abu Bakr. Abu Bakr declared, &#x27;I bear witness that you are the Messenger of Allah</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Muwatta Malik · 26]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="hi">
<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">ज़ैद बिन असलम से रिवायत है के रात को उतरे राह में मक्का के रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) और मुक़र्रर किया बिलाल (रज़ियल्लाहु अन्हु) को इस पर के जगा दें उनको वास्ते नमाज़ के, तो सो गए बिलाल और सो गए लोग फिर जागे और सूरज निकल आया था और घबराए लोग, तो हुक्म किया रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने सवार होने का ताके निकल जाएं इस वादी से और फरमाया के इस वादी में शैतान है, पस सवार हुए और निकल गए इस वादी से तब हुक्म किया उनको रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने उतरने का और वुज़ू करने का और हुक्म किया बिलाल को अज़ान का या तकबीर का, फिर मुतवज्जह हुए आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) लोगों की तरफ और देखा उनकी घबराहट को तो फरमाया आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने लोगों को: बेशक रोक रखा था अल्लाह तआला ने हमारी जानों को और अगर चाहता तो वो फेर देता हमारी जानों को सिवा उस वक़्त के और किसी वक़्त, तो जब सो जाए कोई तुम में से नमाज़ से या भूल जाए उसकी, फिर घबरा के उठे नमाज़ के लिए तो चाहिए के पढ़ ले उसको जैसे पढ़ता है उसको वक़्त पर। फिर शैतान आया बिलाल के पास और वो खड़े हुए नमाज़ पढ़ते थे तो लुटा दिया उनको फिर लगा थपकारने उनको जैसे थपकारते हैं बच्चे को यहां तक के सो रहे वो, फिर हिलाया रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने बिलाल को, पस बयान किया बिलाल ने इसी तरह जैसे फरमाया था आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने हाल उनको, पस बयान किया बिलाल ने इसी तरह जैसे फरमाया था आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने हाल उनका अबू बक्र (रज़ियल्लाहु अन्हु) से, तो कहा अबू बक्र ने मैं गवाही देता हूं इस अम्र की के आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) अल्लाह के रसूल हैं।</p>

<p dir="auto">[Muwatta Malik · 26]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ur-Latn">
<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Zaid bin Aslam se riwayat hai ke raat ko utre rah mein Makkah ke Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) aur muqarrar kiya Bilal (Radi Allahu Anhu) ko is par ke jaga dein unko waste namaz ke, to so gaye Bilal aur so gaye log phir jage aur sooraj nikal aaya tha aur ghabraye log, to hukm kiya Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne sawar hone ka taake nikal jayen is wadi se aur farmaya ke is wadi mein Shaitan hai, pas sawar huye aur nikal gaye is wadi se tab hukm kiya unko Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne utarne ka aur wuzu karne ka aur hukm kiya Bilal ko azan ka ya takbeer ka, phir mutawajeh huye aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) logon ki taraf aur dekha unki ghabrahat ko to farmaya aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne logon ko: Beshak rok rakha tha Allah Ta&#x27;ala ne hamari jaano ko aur agar chahta to woh pher deta hamari jaano ko siwa us waqt ke aur kisi waqt, to jab so jaye koi tum mein se namaz se ya bhool jaye uski, phir ghabra ke uthe namaz ke liye to chahiye ke parh le usko jaise parhta hai usko waqt par. Phir Shaitan aaya Bilal ke paas aur woh khare huye namaz parhte the to luta diya unko phir laga thapkarne unko jaise thapkarte hain bachche ko yahan tak ke so rahe woh, phir hilaya Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne Bilal ko, pas bayan kiya Bilal ne isi tarah jaise farmaya tha aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne haal unko, pas bayan kiya Bilal ne isi tarah jaise farmaya tha aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne haal unka Abu Bakr (Radi Allahu Anhu) se, to kaha Abu Bakr ne main gawahi deta hun is amr ki ke aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) Allah ke Rasool hain.</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: prophetic report. Attribution: The Messenger of Allah in Suwayd ibn an-Numans Khaybar expedition account. The full field supplies the stopping place, prayer, barley meal, mouth rinsing and subsequent prayer without renewed wudu.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Yahya ibn Said from Bushayr ibn Yasar, the mawla of the Bani Haritha, that Suwayd ibn anNuman told him that he went with the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, on the expedition to Khaybar. When they reached as Suhba, which was near Khaybar, the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, stopped and prayed asr. He asked for provisions but only parched barley was brought, so he asked for it to be moistened. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, ate and the people ate with him. Then he got up to do maghrib and rinsed his mouth out and they rinsed out theirs. Then he prayed without doing wudu</p>
