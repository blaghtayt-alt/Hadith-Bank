<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Zayd ibn Aslam from Ata ibn Yasar that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, sent a gift to Umar ibn al-Khattab, and Umar returned it. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;Why did you return it?&quot; He said, &quot;Messenger of Allah, didn&#x27;t you tell us that it is better for us not to take anything from anyone?&quot; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;That is by asking. Provision which Allah gives you is different from asking.&quot; Umar ibn al-Khattab said, &quot;By the One in whose hand my self is, I will not ask anything from anyone, and anything that comes to me without my asking for it, I will accept</p>

<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Attar bin Yasar se riwayat hai ki Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne Hazrat Umar (Radi Allahu Anhu) ke paas kuch maal bheja, Hazrat Umar ne usko pher diya. Poocha tumne kyun pher diya? Hazrat Umar ne kaha: Ya Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) aapne farmaya hai ki behtar woh shakhs hai jo kisi se kuch na le. Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya iska matlab yeh hai ki maang kar kuch na le aur jo bin maange mile woh Allah ka diya hua hai. Hazrat Umar ne kaha qasam us zaat ki jiske haath mein meri jaan hai, main ab kisi se kuch na maangunga aur jo bin maange mere paas aayega usko lunga.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Zayd ibn Aslam from Ata ibn Yasar that a man of the Banu Asad said, &quot;My family and I dismounted to rest at Baqi.
