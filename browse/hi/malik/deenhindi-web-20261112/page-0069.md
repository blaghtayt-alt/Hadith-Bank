<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنِي عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ زَيْدِ بْنِ أَسْلَمَ، أَنَّ رَجُلاً، فِي زَمَانِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أَصَابَهُ جُرْحٌ فَاحْتَقَنَ الْجُرْحُ الدَّمَ وَأَنَّ الرَّجُلَ دَعَا رَجُلَيْنِ مِنْ بَنِي أَنْمَارٍ فَنَظَرَا إِلَيْهِ فَزَعَمَا أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ لَهُمَا ‏&quot;‏ أَيُّكُمَا أَطَبُّ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَقَالاَ أَوَ فِي الطِّبِّ خَيْرٌ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ فَزَعَمَ زَيْدٌ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ أَنْزَلَ الدَّوَاءَ الَّذِي أَنْزَلَ الأَدْوَاءَ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏</p>

<p dir="rtl">[Muwatta Malik · 1722]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Zayd ibn Aslam that a man received a wound in the time of the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. The blood clotted in the wound and the man called two men from the Banu Ammar tribe. They looked at it and claimed that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said to them, &quot;Which of you is the better doctor?&quot; They said, &quot;Is there any good in medicine, Messenger of Allah?&quot; Zayd claimed that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;The one who sent down the disease sent down the remedy</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Muwatta Malik · 1722]</p>

<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Idrees Khawlani se riwayat hai ke main Damishq ki masjid mein gaya wahan maine ek naujawan ko dekha jo safed dandan tha. Uske sath wale log jab kisi baat mein ikhtilaf karte hain to jo woh kehta hai usi ki sanad pakarte hain aur uske qaul par tham jate hain. Maine pucha yeh naujawan kaun hai? Logon ne kaha Muadh bin Jabal hain. Jab doosra roz hua to main bahut sawere gaya dekha to woh mujh se aagay aaye hain aur namaz parh rahe hain. Main thehra raha, jab namaz parh chuke to main unke samne aaya aur salam kiya phir maine kaha main tumko Allah Jalla Jalaluhu ke wastay chahta hoon aur mohabbat karta hoon. Unhon ne kaha Allah ke wastay? Maine kaha haan Allah ke wastay. Unhon ne phir kaha Allah ke wastay? Maine kaha haan Allah ke wastay. Phir unhon ne meri chadar ka kona pakar ke mujhe ghiseeta aur kaha khush hoja, maine Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se suna aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) farmate thay ke Allah Jalla Jalaluhu farmata hai, wajib hui mohabbat meri un logon se jo mere wastay dosti aur mohabbat rakhte hain aur mere wastay mil kar baithte hain aur mere wastay apni jaan aur maal sarf karte hain aur mere wastay ek doosre ki mulaqat ko jate hain. Abdullah bin Abbas (Radi Allahu Anhuma) kehte hain miyana-rawi aur narmi aur achi saj-dhaj ek juz hai nabuwat ke pachees juzon mein se.</p>
