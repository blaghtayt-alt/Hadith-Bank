<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Nafi from Abdullah ibn Umar that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;I dreamt at night that I was at the Kaba, and I saw a dark man like the most handsome of dark men you have ever seen. He had hair reaching to between his ears and his shoulders like the most excellent of such hair that you have seen. He had combed his hair, and water was dripping from it. He was leaning on two men or on the shoulders of two men doing tawaf around Kaba. I asked, &#x27;Who is this?&#x27; It was said, &#x27;al- Masih ibn Maryam.&#x27; Then we were with a man with wiry hair and blind in his right eye, as if it was a floating grape. I asked &#x27;Who is this?&#x27; It was said to me, &#x27;This is al-Masih ad-Dajjal</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Muwatta Malik · 1672]</p>

<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">अब्दुल्लाह बिन उमर (रदी अल्लाहु अन्हु) से रिवायत है के रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने फ़रमाया के मुझे ख़्वाब में एक रात मालूम हुआ के मैं काबा के पास हूँ, तो मैंने एक शख़्स को देखा गंदुमी रंग, जैसे के तुमने बहुत अच्छे गंदुमी रंग के आदमी देखे हों, उसके कंधों तक बाल हैं जैसे तुमने बहुत अच्छे कंधों तक बाल देखे हों। सो उस मर्द ने उन बालों में कंघी की है तो उनसे पानी टपकता है, दो आदमियों पर तकिया लगाए, यूँ फ़रमाया के दो आदमियों के कंधों पर तकिया लगाए, वही शख़्स बैतुल्लाह का तवाफ़ करता है। सो मैंने पूछा ये कौन शख़्स है? तो किसी ने मुझसे कहा ये मसीह है मरयम का बेटा। फिर मैंने यकायक एक और शख़्स देखा निहायत घुंघराले बाल वाला, दाईं आँख का काना, उसकी कानी आँख ऐसी थी जैसे फूला हुआ अंगूर। सो मैंने पूछा ये कौन शख़्स है? किसी ने मुझसे कहा ये मसीह दज्जाल है।</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ur-Latn">
<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abdullah bin Umar (Radi Allahu Anhu) se riwayat hai ke Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya ke mujhe khwab mein ek raat maloom hua ke main Kaaba ke paas hoon, to maine ek shakhs ko dekha gundumi rang, jaise ke tumne bohot achhe gundumi rang ke aadmi dekhe hon, uske kandhon tak baal hain jaise tumne bohot achhe kandhon tak baal dekhe hon. So us mard ne un baalon mein kanghi ki hai to unse pani tapakta hai, do aadmiyon par takiya lagaye, yoon farmaya ke do aadmiyon ke kandhon par takiya lagaye, wahi shakhs Baitullah ka tawaf karta hai. So maine poochha ye kaun shakhs hai? To kisi ne mujhse kaha ye Maseeh hai Maryam ka beta. Phir maine yakayak ek aur shakhs dekha nihayat ghungrale baal wala, daayin aankh ka kana, uski kani aankh aisi thi jaise phoola hua angoor. So maine poochha ye kaun shakhs hai? Kisi ne mujhse kaha ye Maseeh Dajjal hai.</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Muwatta Malik · 1672]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">وَحَدَّثَنِي عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ أَبِي حَازِمِ بْنِ دِينَارٍ، عَنْ سَهْلِ بْنِ سَعْدٍ الأَنْصَارِيِّ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم أُتِيَ بِشَرَابٍ فَشَرِبَ مِنْهُ وَعَنْ يَمِينِهِ غُلاَمٌ وَعَنْ يَسَارِهِ الأَشْيَاخُ فَقَالَ لِلْغُلاَمِ ‏ &quot;‏ أَتَأْذَنُ لِي أَنْ أُعْطِيَ هَؤُلاَءِ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَقَالَ الْغُلاَمُ لاَ وَاللَّهِ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ لاَ أُوثِرُ بِنَصِيبِي مِنْكَ أَحَدًا ‏.‏ قَالَ فَتَلَّهُ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي يَدِهِ ‏.‏</p>

<p dir="rtl">[Muwatta Malik · 1688]</p>

## Narration 1668

[Open this narration online](https://blaghtayt-alt.github.io/Hadith-Bank/?occurrence=deenhindi-malik%3A1689)

<p dir="auto"><strong>Chapter 49</strong></p>

<p><strong>Hadith</strong> · A narration preserved from the source.</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنِي عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ إِسْحَاقَ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي طَلْحَةَ، أَنَّهُ سَمِعَ أَنَسَ بْنَ مَالِكٍ، يَقُولُ قَالَ أَبُو طَلْحَةَ لأُمِّ سُلَيْمٍ لَقَدْ سَمِعْتُ صَوْتَ، رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ضَعِيفًا أَعْرِفُ فِيهِ الْجُوعَ فَهَلْ عِنْدَكِ مِنْ شَىْءٍ فَقَالَتْ نَعَمْ ‏.‏ فَأَخْرَجَتْ أَقْرَاصًا مِنْ شَعِيرٍ ثُمَّ أَخَذَتْ خِمَارًا لَهَا فَلَفَّتِ الْخُبْزَ بِبَعْضِهِ ثُمَّ دَسَّتْهُ تَحْتَ يَدِي وَرَدَّتْنِي بِبَعْضِهِ ثُمَّ أَرْسَلَتْنِي إِلَى رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ فَذَهَبْتُ بِهِ فَوَجَدْتُ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم جَالِسًا فِي الْمَسْجِدِ وَمَعَهُ النَّاسُ فَقُمْتُ عَلَيْهِمْ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏&quot;‏ آرْسَلَكَ أَبُو طَلْحَةَ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ قَالَ فَقُلْتُ نَعَمْ ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ لِلطَّعَامِ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَقُلْتُ نَعَمْ ‏.‏ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم لِمَنْ مَعَهُ ‏&quot;‏ قُومُوا ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ قَالَ فَانْطَلَقَ وَانْطَلَقْتُ بَيْنَ أَيْدِيهِمْ حَتَّى جِئْتُ أَبَا طَلْحَةَ فَأَخْبَرْتُهُ فَقَالَ أَبُو طَلْحَةَ يَا أُمَّ سُلَيْمٍ قَدْ جَاءَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِالنَّاسِ وَلَيْسَ عِنْدَنَا مِنَ الطَّعَامِ مَا نُطْعِمُهُمْ ‏.‏ فَقَالَتِ اللَّهُ وَرَسُولُهُ أَعْلَمُ ‏.‏ قَالَ فَانْطَلَقَ أَبُو طَلْحَةَ حَتَّى لَقِيَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَأَقْبَلَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَأَبُو طَلْحَةَ مَعَهُ حَتَّى دَخَلاَ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏&quot;‏ هَلُمِّي يَا أُمَّ سُلَيْمٍ مَا عِنْدَكِ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَأَتَتْ بِذَلِكَ الْخُبْزِ فَأَمَرَ بِهِ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَفُتَّ وَعَصَرَتْ عَلَيْهِ أُمُّ سُلَيْمٍ عُكَّةً لَهَا فَآدَمَتْهُ ثُمَّ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم مَا شَاءَ اللَّهُ أَنْ يَقُولَ ثُمَّ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ ائْذَنْ لِعَشَرَةٍ بِالدُّخُولِ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَأَذِنَ لَهُمْ فَأَكَلُوا حَتَّى شَبِعُوا ثُمَّ خَرَجُوا ثُمَّ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ ائْذَنْ لِعَشَرَةٍ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَأَذِنَ لَهُمْ فَأَكَلُوا حَتَّى شَبِعُوا ثُمَّ خَرَجُوا ثُمَّ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ ائْذَنْ لِعَشَرَةٍ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَأَذِنَ لَهُمْ فَأَكَلُوا حَتَّى شَبِعُوا ثُمَّ خَرَجُوا ثُمَّ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ ائْذَنْ لِعَشَرَةٍ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَأَذِنَ لَهُمْ فَأَكَلُوا حَتَّى شَبِعُوا ثُمَّ خَرَجُوا ثُمَّ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ ائْذَنْ لِعَشَرَةٍ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ حَتَّى أَكَلَ الْقَوْمُ كُلُّهُمْ وَشَبِعُوا وَالْقَوْمُ سَبْعُونَ رَجُلاً أَوْ ثَمَانُونَ رَجُلاً ‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik that Ishaq ibn Abdullah ibn Abi Talha heard Anas ibn Malik say that Abu Talha had said to Umm Sulaym, &quot;I have just been listening to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and his voice was very weak. I recognised hunger in it, so, do you have anything?&quot; She replied, &quot;Yes,&quot; and brought out some barley loaves. She took her long head scarf and wrapped up the bread with part of it and put it into my (Anas&#x27;s) hand and gave me part of it to wear. Then she sent me to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace.&quot; Anas continued, &quot;I took it, and I found the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, sitting in the mosque with some people. I watched them. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &#x27;Did Abu Talha send you?&#x27; I replied, &#x27;Yes.&#x27; He said, &#x27;For food?&#x27; I said, &#x27;Yes.&#x27; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said to those with him, &#x27;Let us go.&#x27; He set off and I went among them until I came to Abu Talha and told him. Abu Talha said, &#x27;Umm Sulaym! The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, has brought people and we have no food. What shall we give them to eat?&#x27; She said, &#x27;Allah and His Messenger know best.&#x27; &quot; Anas continued, &quot;Abu Talha went out and met the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, approached with Abu Talha until they entered. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &#x27;Come now, Umm Sulaym, what have you got?&#x27; She brought out bread. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, ordered it to be broken into pieces, and Umm Sulaym squeezed out onto it a container of clarified butter which she had seasoned. Then the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said whatever Allah wished him to say, and said, &#x27;Will you give permission for ten of them to come in?&#x27; He gave them permission, and they ate until they were full and then left. He said, &#x27;Give permission to ten more.&#x27; He gave them permission, and they ate until they were full and left. Then he said, &#x27;Give permission to ten more.&#x27; He gave them permission and they ate until they were full and left. Then he said, &#x27;Give permission to ten more.&#x27; He gave permission and they ate until they were full and left. There were seventy or eighty men</p>

<p dir="auto">Report type: companion report. Attribution: Jabir ibn Abdullah describing the delegation commanded by Abu Ubayda. <p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Wahb ibn Kaysan that Jabir ibn Abdullah said, &quot;The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, sent a delegation to the coast. Abu Ubayda ibn al- Jarrah was in command of them. There were 300 people and I was among them. We went out until we had gone part of the way and our provisions were finished. Abu Ubayda ordered that the provisions of the army be gathered up and they amounted to two containers of dates. He used to give us a little provision from it each day until it was finished, and we used to have only a single date each. I said, &#x27;What use is one date?&#x27; He said, &#x27;We will certainly feel its loss when they are finished.&#x27; &quot; Jabir continued, &quot;Then we reached the sea and there was a fish like a small mountain. The army ate from it for eighteen nights. Then Abu Ubayda ordered two ribs from it to be set up. Then he commanded that a camel be ridden underneath them and it did not touch them</p>
