<section class="language-reading" lang="ur-Latn">
<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Hurairah (Radi Allahu Anhu) se riwayat hai ke farmaya Nabi (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne, jis shakhs ne parhi namaz aur na parhi us mein Surah Fatiha toh namaz uski naqis hai, naqis hai, hargiz tamam nahi hai. Abul Saib ne kaha ae Abu Hurairah (Radi Allahu Anhu) kabhi main Imam ke peeche hota hoon, toh daba diya Abu Hurairah (Radi Allahu Anhu) ne mera bazu aur kaha parh le apne dil mein ae Fars ke rehne wale, kyonke main ne suna hai Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se farmate thay, farmaya Allah Ta&#x27;ala ne bat gayi namaz mere aur mere bande ke beech mein aadhon aadh, aadhi meri aur aadhi uski, aur jo banda mera mange usko doonga. Farmaya Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne parho, banda kehta hai ke sab tareef Allah ko hai jo sahib hai saare jahan ka, Parwardigar kehta hai meri tareef ki mere bande ne.
