<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Abdullah ibn Abdullah ibn Jabir ibn Atik that Atik ibn al-Harith, the grandfather of Abdullah ibn Abdullah ibn Jabir on his mother&#x27;s side, told him that Jabir ibn Atik had told him that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, came to visit Abdullah ibn Thabit and found him in his death-throes. He called to him but he did not reply. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;We belong to Allah, and to Him we are returning,&quot; and added, &quot;You are being taken from us, Abur-Rabi`.&quot; The women cried out and wept, and Jabir began to silence them. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;Leave them, and when the necessary time comes, none of the women should cry.&quot; They said, &quot;Messenger of Allah, what is the necessary time?&quot; and he replied, &quot;When he dies.&quot; The dying man&#x27;s daughter said, &quot;By Allah, I hope that you will be a martyr, for you have completed your preparations for battle,&quot; and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;Allah has made his reward fall according to his intention. What do you consider dying a martyr to be?&quot; They said, &quot;Death in the way of Allah.&quot; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;There are seven kinds of martyr other than those killed in the way of Allah. Someone who is killed by the plague is a martyr, someone who drowns is a martyr, someone who dies of pleurisy is a martyr, someone who dies of a disease of the belly is a martyr, someone who dies by fire is a martyr, someone who dies under a falling building is a martyr and a woman who dies in childbirth is a martyr</p>

<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Jabir bin Atik se riwayat hai ke Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) Abdullah bin Thabit ki ayadat ko aaye toh dekha unko bimari ki shiddat mein, so pukara Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne unko, unhone jawab na diya. Pas Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne &#x27;Inna lillahi wa inna ilaihi raji&#x27;un&#x27; kaha aur farmaya hum maghloob hue tumhare par aye Abu Al-Rabi! Phir auraton ne chilla kar rona shuru kiya aur Jabir bin Atik unhe chup karane lage, so farmaya Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne sabhi auraton ko rone do, jab jaan nikal jaye toh us waqt koi na roye. Rone wali sahabiya ne poocha kya matlab hai jaan padne ka, farmaya jab mar jaye. Itne mein Abdullah bin Thabit ki beti ne kaha mujhe umeed thi ke tum shaheed hoge kyunke tum saaman-e-jihad ka tayyar kar chuke the, toh farmaya Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne Allah Jalla Jalaluhu ajar dega mutabiq uski niyat ke. Tum kis cheez ko shahadat samajhti ho? Boli Allah Jalla Jalaluhu ki raah mein mare jaane ko. Farmaya Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne siwa uske saat shaheed hain: ek wo jo taun se mar jaye, dusre wo jo doob kar mar jaye, teesre wo jo zaat-ul-janb se mar jaye, chauthe jo pet ki aarizah se mar jaye, paanchve wo jo aag se jal kar mar jaye, chhathe wo jo dab kar mar jaye, saatve wo aurat jo zachgi mein mar jaye.</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: historical report. Attribution: al-Qasim ibn Muhammad (successor); Muhammad ibn Kab al Quradhi (successor). The source transmits Qasim’s bereavement and Muhammad ibn Kab’s consoling story about unnamed people of Bani Israil. It is preserved as an attributed historical story, not Prophetic speech or independently established history.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Yahya ibn Said that al-Qasim ibn Muhammad said, &quot;One of my wives died and Muhammad ibn Kab al Quradhi came to console me about her. He told me of one among the Bani Israil who was a diligent, worshipping, knowing and understanding man who had a wife that he admired and loved, and she died. He grieved over her intensely and lamented her until he withdrew into a house and locked himself in, hidden from everyone, and no-one visited him. A woman heard about him and went to him, saying, &#x27;I need him to give me an opinion. Nothing will satisfy me except what he says about it.&#x27; Everyone went away, but she stuck to his door and said, &#x27;I must see him.&#x27; Someone said to him, &#x27;There is a woman who wishes to ask your opinion about something,&#x27; and she insisted, &#x27;I will only talk to him about it.&#x27; When everyone had gone away, and she still had not left his door, he said, &#x27;Let her in.&#x27; So she went in and saw him and said, &#x27;I have come to ask your opinion about something.&#x27; He said, &#x27;What is it?&#x27; She said, &#x27;I borrowed a piece of jewellery from a neighbour of mine, and I have worn it and used it for a long time. Then they sent to me for it. Should I let them have it back?&#x27; He said, &#x27;Yes, by Allah.&#x27; She said, &#x27;I have had it for a long time.&#x27; He said, &#x27;It is more correct for you to return it to them, since they have lent it to you for such a long time.&#x27; She said, &#x27;Yes. May Allah have mercy on you. Do you then grieve over what Allah has lent you and then taken from you, when He has a greater right to it than you?&#x27; Then he saw the situation he was in, and Allah helped him by her words</p>

<p dir="ltr">Qasim bin Muhammad se riwayat hai ke meri zauja mar gayi. So aaye Muhammad bin Ka&#x27;b Qurzi ta&#x27;ziyat dene mujhko aur kaha ke Bani Israel mein ek shakhs faqeeh, aalim, aabid, mujtahid tha aur uski ek biwi thi jis par woh nihayat farefta tha aur usko bahut chahta tha. Ittefaq se woh aurat mar gayi toh us shakhs ko nihayat ranj hua aur bada afsos hua aur woh ek ghar mein darwaza band karke baith raha aur logon se mulaqat chhod di, toh uske paas koi na jata tha. Ek aurat ne yeh qissa suna aur uske darwaze par jakar kaha ke mujhko ek masla poochna hai, main isi se poochungi, baghair is se mile hue yeh kaam nahi ho sakta. Toh aur jitne log aaye the woh chale gaye aur woh aurat darwaze par jami rahi aur kaha ke baghair is se taye kiye koi ilaaj nahi hai. So ek shakhs ne andar jakar usko ittila di aur bayan kiya ke ek aurat masla poochne ko tum se aayi hai aur woh kehti hai ke main tum se milna chahti hoon, toh sab log chale gaye magar woh aurat darwaza chhod kar nahi jati. Tab us shakhs ne kaha: &#x27;Achha, usko aane do.&#x27; Pas aayi woh aurat uske paas aur kaha ke main ek masla tujh se poochne ko aayi hoon.
