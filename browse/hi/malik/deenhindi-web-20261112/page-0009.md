<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: companion report. Attribution: Umar ibn al- Khattab (companion). Umar is reported teaching the tashahhud. The source-supplied prayer formula and any accompanying phonetic rendering remain one source report, not additional occurrences.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Ibn Shihab from Urwa ibn az- Zubayr from Abd ar-Rahman ibn Abd al-Qari that he heard Umar ibn al- Khattab say, while he was teaching people the tashahhud from the mimbar, &quot;Say, Greetings belong to Allah. Pure actions belong to Allah. Good words and prayers belong to Allah. Peace on you, Prophet, and the mercy of Allah and His blessings. Peace be upon us and on the slaves of Allah who are salihun. I testify that there is no god except Allah. And I testify that Muhammad is His slave and His messenger.&quot; &#x27;At-tahiyatu lillah, az-zakiyatu lillah, at-tayibatu wa&#x27;s-salawatu lillah. As-salamu alayka ayyuha&#x27;nnabiyyu wa rahmatu&#x27;llahi wa barakatuhu. As-salamu alayna wa ala ibadi&#x27;llahi s-salihin. Ash-hadu an la ilaha illa &#x27;llah wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan abduhu wa rasuluh</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: companion report. Attribution: Abdullah ibn Umar (companion). The source describes Ibn Umar&#x27;s tashahhud and greetings. Supplied formulas, shortened wording and phonetic renderings remain part of one report and are not completed, normalized or counted again.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Nafi that Abdullah ibn Umar used to say the tashahhud saying, &quot;In the name of Allah. Greetings belong to Allah. Prayers belong to Allah. Pure actions belong to Allah. Peace be on the Prophet and the mercy of Allah and His blessings. Peace be on us and on the slaves of Allah who are salihun. I testify that there is no god except Allah. I testify that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.&quot; &quot;Bismillah, at-tahiyatu lillah, as-salawatu lillah, az-zakiyatu lillah. As-salamu ala&#x27;n-nabiyyi wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuhu. As-salamu alayna wa ala ibadi&#x27;llahi&#x27;s-salihin. Shahidtu an la ilaha illallah. Shahidtu anna Muhammadu&#x27;r-rasulu&#x27;llah.&quot; He used to say this after the first two rakas and he would make supplication with whatever seemed fit to him when the tashahhud was completed. When he sat at the end of the prayer, he did the tashahhud in a similar manner, except that after the tashahhud he made supplication with whatever seemed fit to him. When he had completed the tashahhud and intended to say the taslim, he said, &quot;Peace be on the Prophet and His mercy and blessings. Peace be upon us and on the slaves of Allah who are salihun.&quot; &quot;As- salamu ala&#x27;n-nabiyyi wa rahmatu&#x27;llahi wa barakatuhu. As-salamu alayna wa ala ibadi&#x27;llahi&#x27;ssalihin .&quot; He then said, &quot;Peace be upon you&quot; to his right, and would return the greeting to the imam, and if anyone said &quot;Peace be upon you&quot; from his left he would return the greeting to him</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Sumayy, the mawla of Abu Bakr ibn Abd ar-Rahman, from Abu Salih as-Sammani from Abu Hurayra that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;If someone does ghusl for major ritual impurity on the day of jumua and then goes in the first part of the time, it is as if he had offered up a camel. If he goes in the second part of the time, it is as if he had offered up a cow. If he goes in the third part of the time, it is as if he had offered up a horned ram. If he goes in the fourth part of the time, it is as if he had offered up a hen. If he goes in the fifth part of the time, it is as if he had offered up an egg. And when the imam comes out, the angels settle down listening to the dhikr (remembrance of Allah)</p>
