<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">وَحَدَّثَنِي مَالِكٌ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ أَبِي أُمَامَةَ بْنِ سَهْلِ بْنِ حُنَيْفٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَبَّاسٍ، عَنْ خَالِدِ بْنِ الْوَلِيدِ بْنِ الْمُغِيرَةِ، أَنَّهُ دَخَلَ مَعَ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بَيْتَ مَيْمُونَةَ زَوْجِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَأُتِيَ بِضَبٍّ مَحْنُوذٍ فَأَهْوَى إِلَيْهِ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِيَدِهِ ‏.‏ فَقَالَ بَعْضُ النِّسْوَةِ اللاَّتِي فِي بَيْتِ مَيْمُونَةَ أَخْبِرُوا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِمَا يُرِيدُ أَنْ يَأْكُلَ مِنْهُ ‏.‏ فَقِيلَ هُوَ ضَبٌّ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ‏.‏ فَرَفَعَ يَدَهُ فَقُلْتُ أَحَرَامٌ هُوَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ فَقَالَ ‏ &quot;‏ لاَ وَلَكِنَّهُ لَمْ يَكُنْ بِأَرْضِ قَوْمِي فَأَجِدُنِي أَعَافُهُ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ قَالَ خَالِدٌ فَاجْتَرَرْتُهُ فَأَكَلْتُهُ وَرَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَنْظُرُ ‏.‏</p>

<p dir="rtl">[Muwatta Malik · 1772]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Malik related to me from Ibn Shihab from Abu Umama ibn Sahl ibn Hunayf from Abdullah ibn Abbas that Khalid ibn al-Walid ibn al-Mughira entered the house of Maimuna, the wife of the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, with the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and he was brought a roasted lizard. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, stretched his hand toward it. One of the women who was in Maimuna&#x27;s house said, &quot;Tell the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, what he means to eat.&quot; Someone said, &quot;It is a lizard, Messenger of Allah.&quot; He withdrew his hand. Khalid said, &quot;Is it haram, Messenger of Allah?&quot; He said, &quot;No, but there were none in my people&#x27;s land, and I find that I dislike them.&quot; Khalid added, &quot;I chewed and ate it while the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was looking</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Muwatta Malik · 1772]</p>

<p dir="auto">ख़ालिद बिन वलीद बिन मुग़ीरा रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) के साथ मैमूना के घर में गए, वहाँ एक गोह भुना हुआ आया। रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने उसकी तरफ़ हाथ उठाया खाने को, औरतों ने कहा रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) को बता दो जिसका यह गोश्त है, आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने हाथ खींच लिया। मैंने कहा क्या हराम है या रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम)? आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने फ़रमाया नहीं, लेकिन यह मेरे मुल्क में नहीं होता इस वास्ते मुझे इसके खाने से कराहत आती है। ख़ालिद ने कहा मैंने इसको अपनी तरफ़ खींच कर खाया और रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) देख रहे थे।</p>

<p dir="auto">[Muwatta Malik · 1772]</p>

<p dir="ltr">Khalid bin Waleed bin Mughira Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ke saath Maimoona ke ghar mein gaye, wahan ek goh bhuna hua aaya. Khalid ne kaha maine isko apni taraf khench kar khaya aur Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) dekh rahe the.</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Malik related to me from Safiyy, the mawla of Ibn Aflah that Abu&#x27;s-Saib, the mawla of Hisham ibn Zuhra said, &quot;I went to Abu Said al-Khudri and found him praying. I sat to wait for him until he finished the prayer. I heard a movement under a bed in his room, and it was a snake. I stood up to kill it, and Abu Said gestured to me to sit. When he was finished he pointed to a room in the house and said, &#x27;Do you see this room?&#x27; I said, &#x27;Yes.&#x27; He said, &#x27;There was a young boy in it who had just got married. He went out with the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, to al-Khandaq, (the ditch which the muslims dug in the 5th year of the Hijra to defend Madina against the Quraysh and their allies). When he was there, the youth came and asked his permission, saying, &quot;Messenger of Allah. Give me permission to return to my family.&quot; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, gave him permission and said, &quot;Take your weapons with you, for I fear the Banu Quraydha tribe. They may harm you.&quot; The youth went to his family and found his wife standing between the two doors. He lifted his spear to stab her as jealousy had been aroused in him. She said, &quot;Don&#x27;t be hasty until you go in and see what is in your house.&quot; He entered and found a snake coiled up on his bed. He transfixed it with his spear and then went out with it and pitched it into the house. The snake stirred on the end of the spear and the youth fell dead. No one knew which of them died first, the snake or the youth. That was mentioned to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and he said, &quot;There are jinn in Madina who have become muslim. When you see one of them, call out to it for three days. If it appears after that, then kill it, for it is a shaytan</p>

Toh jab tum kisi saanp ko dekho toh teen roz tak use aagah kiya karo, agar baad uske bhi nikle toh usko maar daalo kyunki wo shaitan hai.&#x27;</p>
