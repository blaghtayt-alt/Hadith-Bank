<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">

<p dir="rtl">وَحَدَّثَنِي عَنْ مَالِكٍ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ، أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ - وَهُوَ عَلَى الْمِنْبَرِ وَهُوَ يَذْكُرُ الصَّدَقَةَ وَالتَّعَفُّفَ عَنِ الْمَسْأَلَةِ - ‏ &quot;‏ الْيَدُ الْعُلْيَا خَيْرٌ مِنَ الْيَدِ السُّفْلَى وَالْيَدُ الْعُلْيَا هِيَ الْمُنْفِقَةُ وَالسُّفْلَى هِيَ السَّائِلَةُ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Zayd ibn Aslam from Ata ibn Yasar that the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, sent a gift to Umar ibn al-Khattab, and Umar returned it. The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;Why did you return it?&quot; He said, &quot;Messenger of Allah, didn&#x27;t you tell us that it is better for us not to take anything from anyone?&quot; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;That is by asking. Provision which Allah gives you is different from asking.&quot; Umar ibn al-Khattab said, &quot;By the One in whose hand my self is, I will not ask anything from anyone, and anything that comes to me without my asking for it, I will accept</p>

<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Attar bin Yasar se riwayat hai ki Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne Hazrat Umar (Radi Allahu Anhu) ke paas kuch maal bheja, Hazrat Umar ne usko pher diya. Poocha tumne kyun pher diya? Hazrat Umar ne kaha: Ya Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) aapne farmaya hai ki behtar woh shakhs hai jo kisi se kuch na le. Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya iska matlab yeh hai ki maang kar kuch na le aur jo bin maange mile woh Allah ka diya hua hai. Hazrat Umar ne kaha qasam us zaat ki jiske haath mein meri jaan hai, main ab kisi se kuch na maangunga aur jo bin maange mere paas aayega usko lunga.</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Zayd ibn Aslam from Ata ibn Yasar that a man of the Banu Asad said, &quot;My family and I dismounted to rest at Baqi. My family said to me, &#x27;Go to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and ask him for something that we can eat,&#x27; and they began to mention their need. I went to the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, and found that a man was asking for something, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, was saying, &#x27;I do not have anything to give you.&#x27; The man turned away from him in anger, saying, &#x27;By my life! You give to whomever you wish.&#x27; The Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &#x27;He is angry with me because I do not have anything to give him. Whoever asks of you while he has an uqiya or its like, has asked with importunity.&#x27; &quot; The man continued, &quot;I said to myself about a camel that we had, &#x27;It is better than an uqiya.&#x27; (Malik explained that an uqiya was forty dirhams.) So I returned and did not ask him for anything, and the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, sent me barley and raisins after that. He gave us from his share until Allah, the Mighty, the Majestic gave us relief</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Malik related to me from Ibn Shihab from Muhammad ibn Jubayr ibn Mutim that the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, said, &quot;I have five names. I am Muhammad. I am Ahmad. I am al-Mahi (the effacer), by whom Allah effaces kufr. I am al-Hashir (the gatherer), before whom people are gathered. I am al-Aqib (the last)</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Muwatta Malik · 1858]</p>
