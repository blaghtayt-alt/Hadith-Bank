<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: prophetic report. Attribution: The Messenger of Allah in Abu Ayyub al-Ansaris witnessed combined prayer. The complete field supplies the combined maghrib and isha at Muzdalifa during the Farewell Pilgrimage.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Yahya related to me from Malik from Yahya ibn Said from Adi ibn Thabit al-Ansari that Abdullah ibn Yazid al-Khatmi told him that Abu Ayyub al-Ansari told him that he prayed maghrib and isha together at Muzdalifa during the farewell hajj, with the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace</p>
