<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from &#x27;Amr bin Shuaib, from his father, that his grandfather said:&quot;The Messenger of Allah was asked: &#x27;For how much is the hand (of the thief) to be cut off?&#x27; He said: &#x27;The hand (of the thief) is not to be cut off for (stealing) fruit on the tree, but if (the fruit) has been taken to the place where it is stored to dry, then the (thief&#x27;s) hand is to be cut off (if what is stolen is equivalent to) the price of a shield. The (thief&#x27;s) hand is not to be cut off for a sheep (stolen) from the grazing land, but if it had been put in the pen, then the (thief&#x27;s) hand is to be cut off (if what is stolen is equivalent to) the price of a shield</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan an-Nasa&#x27;i · 4957]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from &#x27;Amr bin Shuaib, from his father, that his grandfather &#x27;Abdullah bin &#x27;Amr, that the Messenger of Allah wsa asked about fruit on the tree. He said:&quot;Whatever a needy person takes without putting any in his pocket (and taking it away), there is no penalty on him. But whoever takes anything away, he must pay a penalty of twice its value, and be punished. Whoever steals something after it has been stored properly, and its value is equal to that of a shield, his hand must be cut off. Whoever steals something worth less than that, he must pay a penalty of twice its value and be punished</p>
