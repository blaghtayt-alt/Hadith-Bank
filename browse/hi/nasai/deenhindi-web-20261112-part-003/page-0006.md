<p dir="auto">Report type: companion report. Attribution: &#x27;Umar bin Al-Khattab (companion). This numbered source report retains the named Companion&#x27;s words or reported conduct under that attribution; it is not relabelled as the Prophet&#x27;s speech.</p>

It was revealed to the Messenger of Allah [SAW] at &#x27;Arafat, on a Friday</p>
