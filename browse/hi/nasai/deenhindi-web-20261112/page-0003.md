<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from &#x27;Abdullah bin &#x27;Abdullah bin &#x27;Umar that his father said: &quot;The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) was asked about water and how some animals and carnivorous beasts might drink from it. He said: &#x27;If the water is more than two Qullahs, it will not carry filth.&#x27;&quot;[1] [1]It comes with some explanation in Sunan At Timidhi &#x27;Abdah (one of the narrators) said: &quot;Muhammad bin Ishaq said: &#x27;A Qullah refers to Jirar (These are two nouns describing large casks that are used to hold water), and a Qullah is the thing that drinking water is held in.&quot;&#x27; At-Tirmidhi said: &quot;This is the saying of Ash Shafa&#x27;i, Ahmad and Ishaq. They say that when the water is two Qullahs then nothing makes it impure, as long as it does not change its smell, and its taste. And they say, it is approximately fifty Qirbahs (waterskins)</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan an-Nasa&#x27;i · 52]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">أَخْبَرَنَا يَحْيَى بْنُ حَبِيبِ بْنِ عَرَبِيٍّ، عَنْ حَمَّادٍ، وَالْحَارِثُ بْنُ مِسْكِينٍ، قِرَاءَةً عَلَيْهِ وَأَنَا أَسْمَعُ، عَنِ ابْنِ الْقَاسِمِ، حَدَّثَنِي مَالِكٌ، ح وَأَخْبَرَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ مَنْصُورٍ، قَالَ أَنْبَأَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ الْمُبَارَكِ، - وَاللَّفْظُ لَهُ - عَنْ يَحْيَى بْنِ سَعِيدٍ، عَنْ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ عَلْقَمَةَ بْنِ وَقَّاصٍ، عَنْ عُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ، - رضى الله عنه - قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏ &quot;‏ إِنَّمَا الأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّةِ وَإِنَّمَا لاِمْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى فَمَنْ كَانَتْ هِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى اللَّهِ وَإِلَى رَسُولِهِ فَهِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى اللَّهِ وَإِلَى رَسُولِهِ وَمَنْ كَانَتْ هِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى دُنْيَا يُصِيبُهَا أَوِ امْرَأَةٍ يَنْكِحُهَا فَهِجْرَتُهُ إِلَى مَا هَاجَرَ إِلَيْهِ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that &#x27;Umar bin Al-Khattab (may Allah be pleased with him) said: &quot;The Messenger of Allah said: &#x27;Actions are only done with intentions, and every man shall have what he intended. Thus he whose emigration was for Allah and His Messenger, his emigration was for Allah and His Messenger, and he whose emigration was to achieve some worldly benefit or to take some woman in marriage, his emigration was for that which he intended</p>
