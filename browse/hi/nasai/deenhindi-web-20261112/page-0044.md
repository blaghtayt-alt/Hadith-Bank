<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: historical report. Attribution: Mutarrif describing Ali&#x27;s prayer and Imran&#x27;s comparison with Muhammad&#x27;s prayer. The actual demonstrated actions belong to Ali and the comparison to Imran; their speaker/actor identities remain explicit.</p>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Mutarrif said: &quot;Imran bin Husain and I prayed behind Ali bin Abi Talib. When he prostrated he said the Takbir, and when he raised his head from prostration he said the takbir, and when he stood up following two rak&#x27;ahs he said the takbir, and when he had finished praying, &#x27;Imran took my hand and said: &#x27;This reminded me of- he said a word meaning- the prayer of Muhammad (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him)</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan an-Nasa&#x27;i · 1082]</p>
