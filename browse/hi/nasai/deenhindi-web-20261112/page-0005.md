<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from Al-Hakam, from his father, that when the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) performed Wudu&#x27;, he would take a handful of water and do this with it. Shu&#x27;bah described it: &quot;He would sprinkle his private parts with it.&quot; [1] Shaikh Ibn As-Sunni said: &quot;Al-Hakam (one of the narrators) is Ibn Sufyan Ath-Thaqafi. [1] The purpose is to ward off devilish whispers lest the person think any emission has taken place, and thus think that his Wudu&#x27; has been invalidated</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan an-Nasa&#x27;i · 134]</p>

<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Asim bin Sufyan Saqafi se riwayat hai ke woh log Ghazwa-e-Salasil ke liye nikle, lekin yeh ghazwa unse faut ho gaya, toh woh sarhad hi pe jame rahe, phir Muawiya (Radi Allahu Anhu) ke paas laut aaye, unke paas Abu Ayyub aur Uqba bin Amir (Radi Allahu Anhum) maujood thay, toh Asim kehne lage: Abu Ayyub! Is saal hum log ghazwe mein shareek nahi ho sake hain, aur humein bataya gaya hai ke jo chaar masajid mein namaz padh le uske gunah baksh diye jayenge? Toh unhone kaha: Bhatije! Main tumhein is se aasan cheez batata hoon, maine Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se suna hai aap farma rahe thay: Jo wuzu kare isi tarah jaise use hukum diya gaya hai aur namaz padhe isi tarah jaise use hukum diya gaya hai, toh uske woh gunah maaf kar diye jayenge jinhe usne is se pehle kiya ho. Uqba! Aise hi hai na? Unhone kaha: Haan (aise hi hai).</p>
