<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Umm Salamah narrated: &quot;While I was lying down with the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) under a blanket, my period came so I slipped away and put on the clothes I used to wear when I was menstruating.
