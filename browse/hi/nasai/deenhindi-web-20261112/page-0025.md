<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from Nafi&#x27; bin Jubair, from his father, that the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said during a journey: &quot;Who will watch out for dawn for us, so that we do not sleep and miss the dawn prayer?&quot; Bilal said: &#x27;I will.&#x27; He turned to face the direction where the sun woke them up, then they got up.
