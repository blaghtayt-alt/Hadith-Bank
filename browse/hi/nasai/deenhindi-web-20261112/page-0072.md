<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: prophetic report. Attribution: The source narrator reporting Muhammad&#x27;s explicitly attributed speech or action; narrator framing remains part of the account. Abu Salamah asks Aishah explicitly about the Messenger&#x27;s night prayer, giving the antecedent for her full reply. Preserve the source&#x27;s thirteen total and its specified sequence.</p>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from Abu Salamah that :He asked Aishah about the prayer of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) at night. She said: &quot;He used to pray thirteen rak&#x27;ahs. He would pray eight rak&#x27;ahs then pray witr, then pray two rak&#x27;ahs sitting down. When he wanted to bow he would stand and bow, and he prayed two rak&#x27;ahs between the adhan and iqamah of subh prayer</p>
