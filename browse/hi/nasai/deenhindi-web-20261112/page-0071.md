<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: prophetic report. Attribution: The source narrator reporting Muhammad&#x27;s explicitly attributed speech or action; narrator framing remains part of the account. Abu Salamah asks Aishah about the Messenger&#x27;s night prayer, and she supplies the sequence including sitting, standing to bow and the dawn pair. Preserve the printed thirteen total and all details.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Salamah bin Abdur-Rahman narrated that :He asked Aishah about the prayer of the Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) at night. She said: &quot;He used to pray thirteen rak&#x27;ahs: nine rak&#x27;ahs standing, one of which was witr, and two rak&#x27;ahs sitting. When he wanted to bow he would stand up, and bow and prostrate, and he did that after witr. Then when he heard the call for Subh, he stood up and prayed two brief rak&#x27;ahs</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan an-Nasa&#x27;i · 1756]</p>
