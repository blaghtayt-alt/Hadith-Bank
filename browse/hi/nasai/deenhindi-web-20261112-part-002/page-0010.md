<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from wa&#x27;il bin Hujr that:the Prophet sent a collector and he came to a man who brought him a slim, recently-weaned camel. The Prophet said: &quot;We sent to Zakah collector of Allah and His Messenger, and so-and-so gave him a slim, recently-weaned camel. O Allah, do not bless him nor his camels!&quot; News of that reached the man, so he came with a beautiful she-camel and said: &quot;I repent to Allah and to His Prophet. &quot; The Prophet said: &quot;O Allah, bless him and his camels!&quot; (Daif)</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan an-Nasa&#x27;i · 2458]</p>

<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">वाइल बिन हुज्र (रज़ियल्लाहु अन्हु) कहते हैं कि रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने एक शख़्स को ज़कात की वसूली पर भेजा, वह एक शख़्स के पास आया, उस ने उसे ऊँट का एक दुबला बच्चा दिया। तो नबी-ए-अकरम (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने फ़रमाया: हम ने अल्लाह और उस के रसूल की तरफ़ से मुहस्सिल-ए-ज़कात को भेजा, फ़लां शख़्स ने उसे एक दुबला कमज़ोर, दूध छोड़ा हुआ बच्चा दिया। ऐ अल्लाह! तू इस में और इस के ऊँटों में बरकत न दे, ये बात उस आदमी को मालूम हुई तो वह एक अच्छी ऊँटनी ले कर आया, और कहने लगा: मैं अल्लाह अज़्ज़ा वजल्ल से तौबा करता हूँ और अल्लाह के नबी-ए-अकरम (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) के सामने अपनी इतात का इज़हार करता हूँ, तो आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने फ़रमाया: ऐ अल्लाह! तू इस में और इस के ऊँटों में बरकत फ़रमा।</p>

<p dir="ltr">Wail bin Hujr (Radi Allahu Anhu) kehte hain ke Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne ek shakhs ko zakat ki wasooli par bheja, woh ek shakhs ke paas aaya, us ne use oont ka ek dubla bacha diya. To Nabi-e-Akram (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya: Hum ne Allah aur us ke Rasool ki taraf se muhassil-e-zakat ko bheja, falan shakhs ne use ek dubla kamzor, doodh choda hua bacha diya. Aye Allah! Tu is mein aur is ke oonton mein barkat na de, ye baat us aadmi ko maloom hui to woh ek achi oontni le kar aaya, aur kehne laga: Main Allah Azzawajal se tauba karta hoon aur Allah ke Nabi-e-Akram (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ke samne apni itaat ka izhaar karta hoon, to Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya: Aye Allah! Tu is mein aur is ke oonton mein barkat farma.</p>

Two men came on a camel and said: We are the messengers of the Messengers of Allah, we come to take the Sadaqah of your sheep. I said: What do I have to give? They said: A sheep. So I went to a sheep that I knew was filled with milk and was fat, and brought it out to them. <h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Muslim bin Thufna kehte hain ke Ibn Alqama ne mere walid ko apni qaum ki &#x27;araafat&#x27; (nigrani) par muqarrar kiya, aur unhe is baat ka hukum bhi diya ke woh un se zakat wasool kar ke layen. Toh mere walid ne mujhe unke kuch logon ke paas bheja ke main un se zakat le kar aa jaaun. Toh main (ghar se) nikla yahan tak ke ek budhay buzurg ke paas pahuncha jinhe &#x27;Sair&#x27; kaha jata tha. Toh maine un se kaha ke mere walid ne mujhe aap ke paas aap ki bakriyon ki zakat lane ke liye bheja hai. Unhone kaha: Bhatije! Tum kis tarah ki zakat lete ho? Maine kaha: Hum chunte aur chhaantte hain yahan tak ke hum bakriyon ke thanon ko naapte-o-tatolte hain (yani umda maal dhoond kar lete hain). Unhone kaha: Bhatije! Main tumhe ek hadith sunata hoon. Main Rasoolullah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ke zamane mein in ghaatiyon mein se kisi ek ghaati mein apni bakriyon ke saath tha, mere paas ek oont par sawaar do shakhs aaye, aur unhone kaha ke hum Rasoolullah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ke faristada hain. Hum aap ke paas aaye hain ke aap hamein apni bakriyon ki zakat ada karen. Maine poocha: Mere upar in bakriyon mein kitni zakat hai? Toh un dono ne kaha: Ek bakri. Maine doodh aur charbi se bhari ek ek moti-tazi bakri ka qasd kiya jis ki jagah mujhe maloom thi. Main use (rewad mein se) nikal kar unke paas laya toh unhone kaha: &#x27;Shaafi&#x27; hai (&#x27;Shaafi&#x27; gaabhan bakri ko kehte hain) aur Rasoolullah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne hamein gaabhan janwar lene se roka hai. Phir maine ek aur bakri la kar pesh karne ka irada kiya jo is se kamtar thi jis ne abhi koi bachcha nahi jana tha lekin hamal ke qabil ho gayi thi. Maine use (gallay se) nikal kar un dono ko pesh kiya, unhone kaha: Haan, yeh hamein qabool hai. Phir humne use utha kar unhe pakda diya. Unhone use apne saath oont par laad liya, phir le kar chale gaye.</p>
