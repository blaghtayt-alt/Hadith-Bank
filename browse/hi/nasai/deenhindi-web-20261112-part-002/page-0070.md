<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that &#x27;Adiyy bin Hatim said:&quot;I asked the Messenger of Allah about hunting with the Mirad. He said: &#x27;whatever is struck with the sharp edge, eat, and whatever is hit with its broad side, it is an animal killed by a blow. &quot;&quot; He said: &quot;And I asked him about hunting dogs. He said: &#x27;If you release your dog and mention the name of Allah over it, then eat.,&#x27; I said: &#x27;Even it he kills it?&#x27; He said: &#x27;Even if he kills it?&quot; He said: &#x27;Even if he kills it. But if he has eaten some of it, then do not eat. And if you find another dog with your dog and he has killed (the game), then do not eat, for you only said the name of Allah over your dog, not over your dog, not over any other</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan an-Nasa&#x27;i · 4274]</p>

<p dir="ltr">Adiyy bin Hatim radiyallahu anhu se riwayat hai ke main ne Rasool Allah Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam se &#x27;Mirad&#x27; (dhaar-daar aalay) ke sath shikar karne ke bare mein poocha.
