<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: companion report. Attribution: Ibn &#x27;Abbas (companion). This transmitted Ibn Abbas account gives a substantive ruling about fasting capacity, concessions and non-abrogation. Preserve its coherent Quranic discussion within his attribution, not as Prophetic speech.</p>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from &#x27;Ata from Ibn &#x27;Abbas:concerning this verse &quot;And as for those who can fast with difficulty, (a choice either to fast or) to feed a Miskin (poor person) (for every day).&quot; That for those who can fast with difficulty means they find it hard; to feed a Miskin means feeding one poor person for each day. But whoever does good of his own accord means feeding another poor person. This is not abrogated, and it is bette for him. And: that &quot;you fast is better for you&quot; means there is no concession regarding this except for those who are not able to fast, or who are incurably sick</p>
