## Narration 1328

[Open this narration online](https://blaghtayt-alt.github.io/Hadith-Bank/?occurrence=deenhindi-nasai%3A3711)

<p dir="auto"><strong>Chapter 33</strong></p>

<p><strong>Report or saying</strong> · The source attribution is explained separately for each language reading.</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>
