<p dir="ltr">it was narrated that Sharik bin Shihab said:&quot;I used to wish that I could meet a man among the Companions of the Prophet [SAW] and ask him about the Khawarij. Then I met Abu Barzah on the day of &#x27;Id, with a number of his companions. I said to him: &#x27;Did you hear the Messenger of Allah [SAW] mention the Khawarij?&#x27; He said: &#x27;Yes. Some wealth was brought to the Messenger of Allah [SAW] and he distributed it to those on his right and on his left, but he did not give anything to those who were behind him. Then a man stood behind him and said: &quot;O Muhammad! You have not been just in your division!&quot; He was a man with black patchy (shaved) hair, wearing two white garments. So Allah&#x27;s Messenger [SAW] became very angry and said: &quot;By Allah!
