<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from Zaid bin Khalid that the Prophet (ﷺ) was asked about a lost camel. :He turned red, and he said: “What does it have to do with you? <p dir="auto">Report type: prophetic report. Attribution: Muhammad, as explicitly attributed in this complete source field. Exact English source independently read. <p dir="ltr">It was narrated from Miqdad bin &#x27;Amr:That he went out one day to Al-Baqi&#x27;, which is the graveyard, to relieve himself. People used to go out to relieve themselves only every two or three days, and their faces was like that of a camel (because of hunger and rough food). Then he entered a ruin and while he was squatting to relieve himself, he saw a rat bringing a Dinar out of a hole, then it went in and brought out another, until it had brought out seventeen Dinars. Then it brought out a piece of red rag.Miqdad said: “I picked up the rag and found another Dinar inside it, thus completing eighteen Dinar. I took them out and brought them to the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ), and told him what had happened. I said, &#x27;Take its Sadaqah (charity), O Messenger of Allah (ﷺ).&#x27; He said: &#x27;Take them back, for no Sadaqah is due on them. May Allah (SWT) bless them for you.&#x27; Then he said: &#x27;Perhaps you put your hand in the hole?&#x27; I said: &#x27;No, by the One Who has honored you with the truth.”</p>
