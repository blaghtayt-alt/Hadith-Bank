<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from &#x27;Amr bin Shu&#x27;aib, from his father, from his grandfather, that :Huwayyisah and Muhayyisah , the sons of Mas&#x27;ud, and &#x27;Abdullah and &#x27;Abdur-Rahman the sons of Sahl, went out to search for food in Khaibar. &#x27;Abdullah was attacked and killed, and mention of that was made to the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ).
