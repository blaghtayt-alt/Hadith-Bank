<summary>English · notes and commentary</summary>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from Ibn`Abbas that `Umar bin Khattab said:“I fear that after a long time has passed, some will say: &#x27;I do not find (the sentence of) stoning in the Book of Allah (ﷺ),&#x27; and they will go astray by abandoning one of the obligations enjoined by Allah (SWT). Rather stoning is a must if a man is married (or previously married) and proof is established, or if pregnancy results or if he admits it. I have read it (in the Quran). “And if an old man and an old woman commit adultery, stone them both.” </p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Bara&#x27; bin Azib said:“The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) passed by a Jew with a blackened face who had been flogged. He called them and said: &#x27;Is this the punishment for the adulterer that you find in your Book?&#x27; They said: &#x27;Yes.&#x27; Then he called one of their scholars and said: &#x27;I adjure you by Allah (SWT) Who sent down the Tawrah (Torah) to Musa! Is this the punishment for the adulterer that you find in your Book?&#x27; He said: &#x27;No; if you had not adjured me by Allah (SWT), I would not have told you. The punishment for the adulterer that we find in our Book is stoning, but many of our nobles were being stoned (because of the prevalence of adultery among them), so if we caught one of our nobles (committing adultery), we would let him go; but if we caught one of the weak among us, we would carry out the punishment on him. We said: “Come, let us agree upon something that we may impose on both noble and weak alike.” So we agreed to blacken the face and whip them, instead of stoning.&#x27; The Prophet (ﷺ) &#x27;O Allah (SWT), I am the first of those who revive your command which they had killed off,&#x27; and he issued orders that (the man) be stoned.”</p>
