<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا هَارُونُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ الْحَمَّالُ، حَدَّثَنَا هَاشِمُ بْنُ الْقَاسِمِ، حَدَّثَنَا زِيَادُ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُلاَثَةَ، عَنْ مُوسَى بْنِ مُحَمَّدِ بْنِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ، عَنْ أَبِيهِ، عَنْ جَابِرٍ، وَأَنَسِ بْنِ مَالِكٍ، أَنَّ النَّبِيَّ ـ صلى الله عليه وسلم ـ كَانَ إِذَا دَعَا عَلَى الْجَرَادِ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ اللَّهُمَّ أَهْلِكْ كِبَارَهُ وَاقْتُلْ صِغَارَهُ وَأَفْسِدْ بَيْضَهُ وَاقْطَعْ دَابِرَهُ وَخُذْ بِأَفْوَاهِهَا عَنْ مَعَايِشِنَا وَأَرْزَاقِنَا إِنَّكَ سَمِيعُ الدُّعَاءِ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ فَقَالَ رَجُلٌ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ كَيْفَ تَدْعُو عَلَى جُنْدٍ مِنْ أَجْنَادِ اللَّهِ بِقَطْعِ دَابِرِهِ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ إِنَّ الْجَرَادَ نَثْرَةُ الْحُوتِ فِي الْبَحْرِ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ قَالَ هَاشِمٌ قَالَ زِيَادٌ فَحَدَّثَنِي مَنْ رَأَى الْحُوتَ يَنْثُرُهُ ‏.‏</p>

<p dir="rtl">[Sunan Ibn Majah · 3221]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from Jabir and Anas bin Malik that whenever the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) supplicated against the locusts, he said:“O Allah, destroy their large ones and kill their small ones, spoil their eggs and root them out. Take their mouths away from our livelihood and provision, for You are the One Who hears the prayers.” A man said: “O Messenger of Allah, are you praying against one of the troops of Allah, that they may be rooted out?” He said: “Locusts were sneezed out by the fish in the sea.”</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Ibn Majah · 3221]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ur-Latn">
<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Jabir aur Anas bin Malik (Radi Allahu Anhum) se riwayat hai ke jab Nabi Akram (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) tiddiyon (locusts) ke liye bad-dua karte to farmate: &#x27;Aye Allah! Bari tiddiyon ko halak kar de, choti tiddiyon ko maar daal, unke ande kharab kar de, aur unki asal (jar) ko kaat daal aur unke munh ko hamari roziyon aur ghallon se pakad le (inhe un tak pahunchne na de), beshak tu duaon ka sunne wala hai&#x27;. Ek shakhs ne arz kiya: &#x27;Allah ke Rasool! Aap kaise Allah ki ek fauj ki asal aur jar katne ke liye bad-dua farma rahe hain?&#x27; Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya: &#x27;Tiddi dariya ki machhli ki chhink hai&#x27;. Hashim kehte hain ke Ziyad (rawi hadith) ne kaha: &#x27;To mujhse aise shakhs ne bayan kiya jisne machhli se use (tiddi ko) chhinkte dekha&#x27;.</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Ibn Majah · 3221]</p>
