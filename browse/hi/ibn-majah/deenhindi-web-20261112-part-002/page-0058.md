<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا أَبُو بَكْرِ بْنُ أَبِي شَيْبَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا مُعَاذُ بْنُ مُعَاذٍ، عَنِ ابْنِ جُرَيْجٍ، عَنْ عَطَاءٍ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ، قَالَتْ كَانَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ـ صلى الله عليه وسلم ـ إِذَا رَأَى مَخِيلَةً تَلَوَّنَ وَجْهُهُ وَتَغَيَّرَ وَدَخَلَ وَخَرَجَ وَأَقْبَلَ وَأَدْبَرَ فَإِذَا أَمْطَرَتْ سُرِّيَ عَنْهُ ‏.‏ قَالَ فَذَكَرَتْ لَهُ عَائِشَةُ بَعْضَ مَا رَأَتْ مِنْهُ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ وَمَا يُدْرِيكِ لَعَلَّهُ كَمَا قَالَ قَوْمُ هُودٍ ‏{فَلَمَّا رَأَوْهُ عَارِضًا مُسْتَقْبِلَ أَوْدِيَتِهِمْ قَالُوا هَذَا عَارِضٌ مُمْطِرُنَا بَلْ هُوَ مَا اسْتَعْجَلْتُمْ بِهِ }‏ ‏&quot;‏ ‏.‏ الآيَةَ</p>

<p dir="rtl">[Sunan Ibn Majah · 3891]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that ‘Aishah said:“If the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) saw a cloud that looked as if it was bringing rain, the color of his face would change, and he would go in and out and walk to and fro. Then, if it rained, he would feel relieved.” ‘Aishah mentioned to him what she had seen him do, and he said: “How do you know? Perhaps it would be as the people of Hud said: ‘Then, when they saw it as a dense cloud coming towards their valleys, they said: “This is a cloud bringing us rain!” Nay, but it is that (torment) which you were asking to be hastened.” [46:]</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Ibn Majah · 3891]</p>

<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Umm-ul-Momineen Aisha (Radi Allahu Anha) kehti hain ke Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) jab badal ko dekhte to (taraddud aur pareshani ki wajah se) aap ke chehra mubarak ka rang badal jata, kabhi andar tashreef le jate kabhi bahar, kabhi aage jate kabhi peeche, phir jab barish hone lagti to aap ki ye kaifiyat khatam ho jati. Aisha (Radi Allahu Anha) ne aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se aap ki is kaifiyat ka zikr kiya jise unhon ne dekha, to aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya: Ae Aisha tujhe kya maloom? Ho sakta hai ye wahi ho jise dekh kar qaum-e-Hood ne kaha tha: &#x27;Falamma ra&#x27;awhu aridhan mustaqbila awdiyatihim qalu hadha aridun mumtiruna bal huwa ma ista&#x27;jaltum bihi&#x27; (Surah Al-Ahqaf: 24), yani jab un logon ne badal ko apni wadiyon ki taraf aate dekha to kehne lage: Ye badal hai jo hum par pani barsaega, (nahi is mein pani nahi tha) balkay wo cheez (yani azab) hai jis ki tum jaldi macha rahe thay.</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from Ibn ‘Umar that the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said:“Whoever unexpectedly comes across a person suffering a calamity, and says: Al-hamdu Lillahil-ladhi ‘afani mim-mabtalaka bihi, wa faddalani ‘ala kathirin mimman khalaqa tafdila (Praise is to Allah Who has kept me safe from that which has afflicted you and preferred me over many of those whom He has created), will be kept safe from that calamity, no matter what it is.’”</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Ibn Majah · 3892]</p>
