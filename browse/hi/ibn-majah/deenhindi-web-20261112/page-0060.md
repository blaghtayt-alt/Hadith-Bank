<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Bilal bin Yahya said:“If one of the members of his family died, Hudhaifah would say: ‘Do not inform anyone of it, for I am afraid that that would be a public death announcement. I heard the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) with these two ears of mine forbidding making public death announcements.’”</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Ibn Majah · 1476]</p>
