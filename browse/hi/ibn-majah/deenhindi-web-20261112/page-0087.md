<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Abu Sa&#x27;eed Al-Khudri said:&quot;The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) sent us, thirty horsemen, on a military campaign. Then their leader was stung by a scorpion and they said: &#x27;Is there anyone among you who can recite Ruqyah for a scorpion sting?&#x27; I said: &#x27;Yes, I can, but I will not recite Ruqyah for him until you give us some sheep.&#x27; They said: &#x27;We will give you thirty sheep.&#x27; So we accepted them, and I recited Al-Hamd (i.e. Al-Fatihah) over him seven times.
