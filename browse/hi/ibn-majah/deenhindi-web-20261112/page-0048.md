<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Sa’d bin Hisham said:“I asked ‘Aishah: ‘O Mother of the Believers! Tell me about the Witr of the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ).’ She said: ‘We used to keep his tooth stick and water for ablution ready for him. Then he would call upon his Lord and remember Allah and praise Him and supplicate to Him. Then he would get up without saying the Salam. Then he would stand up and pray the ninth Rak’ah. Then he would sit and remember Allah and praise Him, and supplicate to his Lord and send blessing upon His Prophet. Then he would say Salam that we could hear. Then he would pray two Rak’ah after the Salam, while he was sitting down. That was eleven Rak’ah. When the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) grew older and had gained weight, he would pray Witr with seven Rak’ah and then pray two more Rak’ah after he had said the Salam.’”</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا أَحْمَدُ بْنُ سِنَانٍ، حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنُ مَهْدِيٍّ، عَنْ مَالِكِ بْنِ أَنَسٍ، عَنْ أَبِي بَكْرِ بْنِ عُمَرَ بْنِ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عُمَرَ بْنِ الْخَطَّابِ، عَنْ سَعِيدِ بْنِ يَسَارٍ، قَالَ كُنْتُ مَعَ ابْنِ عُمَرَ فَتَخَلَّفْتُ فَأَوْتَرْتُ فَقَالَ مَا خَلَفَكَ قُلْتُ أَوْتَرْتُ ‏.‏ فَقَالَ أَمَا لَكَ فِي رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ـ صلى الله عليه وسلم ـ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ قُلْتُ بَلَى ‏.‏ قَالَ فَإِنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ ـ صلى الله عليه وسلم ـ كَانَ يُوتِرُ عَلَى بَعِيرِهِ ‏.‏</p>

<p dir="rtl">[Sunan Ibn Majah · 1200]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated that Sa’eed bin Yasar said:“I was with Ibn ‘Umar and I lagged behind and prayed Witr. He said: ‘What kept you?’ I said: ‘I was praying Witr.’ He said: ‘Do you not have the best of examples in the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ)?’ I said: ‘Of course.’ He said: ‘The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) used to pray Witr while riding his camel.’”</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Ibn Majah · 1200]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="hi">
<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">सईद बिन यासार कहते हैं के मैं अब्दुल्लाह बिन उमर (रज़ियल्लाहु अन्हुमा) के साथ था, मैं पीछे हो गया, और नमाज़े-वित्र अदा की, उन्होंने पूछा: तुम पीछे क्यों रह गए? मैं ने कहा: मैं वित्र पढ़ रहा था, उन्होंने कहा: क्या तुम्हारे लिए रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) की ज़ाते-गिरामी में उसवा-ए-हसना नहीं है? मैं ने कहा: क्यों नहीं, कहा: रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) अपने ऊँट ही पर वित्र पढ़ लेते थे।</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ur-Latn">
<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Saeed bin Yasar kehte hain ke main Abdullah bin Umar (Radi Allahu Anhuma) ke sath tha, main peeche ho gaya, aur namaz-e-Witr ada ki, unhone poocha: Tum peeche kyun reh gaye? Main ne kaha: Main Witr parh raha tha, unhone kaha: Kya tumhare liye Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki zaat-e-girami mein Uswa-e-Hasana nahi hai? Main ne kaha: Kyun nahi, kaha: Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) apne oont hi par Witr parh lete the.</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sunan Ibn Majah · 1200]</p>
