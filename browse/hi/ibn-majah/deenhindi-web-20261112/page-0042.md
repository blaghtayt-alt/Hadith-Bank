<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">It was narrated from Ibn ‘Abbas that the Prophet (ﷺ) visited a man and said:“What do you long for?” He said: “I long for wheat bread.” The Prophet (ﷺ) said: “Whoever has any wheat bread, let him send it to his brother.” Then the Prophet (ﷺ) said: “If any sick person among you longs for something, then feed him.”</p>
