<p class="counterpart-note">Source report 1</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Amr bin Dinar: We asked Ibn `Umar whether a man who had performed the Tawaf of the Ka`ba but had not performed the Tawaf between As-Safa and Al-Marwa yet, was permitted to have sexual relation with his wife. He replied, &quot;The Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) arrived (at Mecca) and circumambulated the Ka`ba seven times and then offered a two rak`at prayer behind Maqam-Ibrahim and then performed the going (Tawaf) between As-Safa and Al-Marwa (seven times) (and verily, in Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) you have a good example.&quot; </p>

<p class="counterpart-note">Source report 2</p>

<p dir="ltr">And we asked Jabir bin `Abdullah (the same question) and he replied, &quot;He should not go near her till he has finished the going (Tawaf) between As-Safa and Al-Marwa</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا مُحَمَّدُ بْنُ بَشَّارٍ، حَدَّثَنَا غُنْدَرٌ، حَدَّثَنَا شُعْبَةُ، عَنْ قَيْسِ بْنِ مُسْلِمٍ، عَنْ طَارِقِ بْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ أَبِي مُوسَى الأَشْعَرِيِّ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ قَدِمْتُ عَلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِالْبَطْحَاءِ وَهُوَ مُنِيخٌ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ أَحَجَجْتَ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ قُلْتُ نَعَمْ‏.‏ قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ بِمَا أَهْلَلْتَ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ قُلْتُ لَبَّيْكَ بِإِهْلاَلٍ كَإِهْلاَلِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَ ‏&quot;‏ أَحْسَنْتَ‏.‏ طُفْ بِالْبَيْتِ وَبِالصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةِ ثُمَّ أَحِلَّ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ فَطُفْتُ بِالْبَيْتِ، وَبِالصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةِ، ثُمَّ أَتَيْتُ امْرَأَةً مِنْ قَيْسٍ، فَفَلَتْ رَأْسِي، ثُمَّ أَهْلَلْتُ بِالْحَجِّ‏.‏ فَكُنْتُ أُفْتِي بِهِ، حَتَّى كَانَ فِي خِلاَفَةِ عُمَرَ فَقَالَ إِنْ أَخَذْنَا بِكِتَابِ اللَّهِ فَإِنَّهُ يَأْمُرُنَا بِالتَّمَامِ، وَإِنْ أَخَذْنَا بِقَوْلِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَإِنَّهُ لَمْ يَحِلَّ حَتَّى يَبْلُغَ الْهَدْىُ مَحِلَّهُ‏.‏</p>

<p dir="rtl">[Sahih Al-Bukhari · 1795]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Musa Al-Ash`ari: I came to the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) at Al-Batha&#x27; while his camel was kneeling down and he asked me, &quot;Have you intended to perform the Hajj?&quot; I replied in the affirmative. He asked me, &#x27;With what intention have you assumed Ihram?&quot; I replied, &quot;I have assumed Ihram with the same intention as that of the Prophet. He said, &quot;You have done well. Perform the Tawaf of the Ka`ba and (the Sai) between As-Safa and Al- Marwa and then finish the Ihram.&quot; So, I performed the Tawaf around the Ka`ba and the Sai) between As-Safa and Al-Marwa and then went to a woman of the tribe of Qais who cleaned my head from lice. Later I assumed the Ihram for Hajj. I used to give the verdict of doing the same till the caliphate of `Umar who said, &quot;If you follow the Holy Book then it orders you to remain in the state of Ihram till you finish from Hajj, if you follow the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) then he did not finish his Ihram till the Hadi (sacrifice) had reached its place of slaughtering (Hajj-al-Qiran)</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Al-Bukhari · 1795]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Report type: companion historical report. Attribution: Ibn Umar with his sons, through Nafi. English field fully read: The feared obstruction, recalled Prophetic example and Ibn Umar&#x27;s own intentions are retained as a coherent Companion history.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Nafi`:That Ubaidullah bin `Abdullah and Salim bin `Abdullah informed him that they told Ibn `Umar when Ibn Az-Zubair was attacked by the army, saying &quot;There is no harm for you if you did not perform Hajj this year. We are afraid that you may be prevented from reaching the Ka`ba.&quot; Ibn `Umar said &quot;We set out with Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) and the non-believers of Quraish prevented us from reaching the Ka`ba, and so the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) slaughtered his Hadi and got his head shaved.&quot; Ibn `Umar added, &quot;I make you witnesses that I have made `Umra obligatory for me. And, Allah willing, I will go and then if the way to Ka`ba is clear, I will perform the Tawaf, but if I am prevented from going to the Ka`ba then I will do the same as the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) did while I was in his company.&quot; Ibn `Umar then assumed Ihram for Umra from Dhul-Hulaifa and proceeded for a while and said, &quot;The conditions of `Umra and Hajj are similar and I make you witnesses that I have made `Umra and Hajj obligatory for myself.&quot; So, he did not finish the Ihram till the day of Nahr (slaughtering) came, and he slaughtered his Hadi. He used to say, &quot;I will not finish the Ihram till I perform the Tawaf, one Tawaf on the day of entering Mecca (i.e. of Safa and Marwa for both `Umra and Hajj)</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Al-Bukhari · 1807]</p>

<p dir="ltr">Hum se Abdullah bin Muhammad bin Asma ne bayan kiya, kaha hum se Juwairiyah ne Nafi se bayan kiya, unhein Ubaidullah bin Abdullah aur Salim bin Abdullah ne khabar di ke jin dinon Abdullah bin Zubair (Radi Allahu Anhuma) par Hajjaj ki lashkar kashi ho rahi thi toh Abdullah bin Umar (Radi Allahu Anhuma) se logon ne kaha (kyunke aap Makkah jana chahte thay) ke agar aap is saal Hajj na karein toh koi nuqsan nahi kyunke darr is ka hai ke kahin aap ko Baitullah pahunchne se rok na diya jaye. Main zaroor jaunga aur agar mujhe Baitullah tak pahunchne ka rasta mil gaya toh tawaf karunga, lekin agar mujhe rok diya gaya toh main bhi wahi kaam karunga jo Nabi Kareem (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne kiya tha, main us waqt bhi aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ke sath maujood tha chunancha aap ne Zul-Hulaifah se Umrah ka ehram bandha phir thodi door chal kar farmaya ke Hajj aur Umrah toh ek hi hain, ab main bhi tumhein gawah banata hoon ke main ne Umrah ke sath Hajj bhi apne upar wajib qarar de liya hai, aap ne Hajj aur Umrah dono se ek sath farigh ho kar hi daswin Zil-Hajj ko ehram khola aur qurbani ki. Aap farmate thay ke jab tak haaji Makkah pahunch kar ek tawaf-e-ziyarat na kar le poora ehram na kholna chahiye.</p>
