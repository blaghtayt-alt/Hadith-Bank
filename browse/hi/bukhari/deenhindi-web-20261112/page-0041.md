<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">وَقَالَ أَبُو مَعْمَرٍ حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ الْوَارِثِ، حَدَّثَنَا أَيُّوبُ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، قَالَ كَانَ ابْنُ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ إِذَا صَلَّى بِالْغَدَاةِ بِذِي الْحُلَيْفَةِ أَمَرَ بِرَاحِلَتِهِ فَرُحِلَتْ ثُمَّ رَكِبَ، فَإِذَا اسْتَوَتْ بِهِ اسْتَقْبَلَ الْقِبْلَةَ قَائِمًا، ثُمَّ يُلَبِّي حَتَّى يَبْلُغَ الْمَحْرَمَ، ثُمَّ يُمْسِكُ حَتَّى إِذَا جَاءَ ذَا طُوًى بَاتَ بِهِ حَتَّى يُصْبِحَ، فَإِذَا صَلَّى الْغَدَاةَ اغْتَسَلَ، وَزَعَمَ أَنَّ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَعَلَ ذَلِكَ‏.‏ تَابَعَهُ إِسْمَاعِيلُ عَنْ أَيُّوبَ فِي الْغَسْلِ‏.‏</p>

<p dir="rtl">[Sahih Al-Bukhari · 1553]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Nafi&#x27;, &#x27;Whenever Ibn &#x27;Umar finished his morning Salat at Dhul-Hulaifa he would get his Rahila (mount) prepared. Then, he would ride on it, and after it had stood up straight (ready to set out), he would face Al-Qiblah (the Ka&#x27;bah at Makkah) while sitting (on his mount) and recite Talbiya. When he had reached the boundaries of the Haram (or Makkah), he would stop recitation of Talbiya till he reached Dhi-Tuwa (near Makkah) where he would pass the night till it was dawn. After offering the morning Salat, he would take a bath. He claimed that Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) had done the same</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Al-Bukhari · 1553]</p>

<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">और अबू मुअ़म्मर ने कहा कि हम से अब्दुल वारिस ने बयान किया, उन्होंने कहा कि हम से अय्यूब सखतियानी ने नाफे से बयान किया, उन्होंने कहा कि अब्दुल्लाह बिन उमर (रदी अल्लाहु अन्हुमा) जब ज़ुल-हुलाइफा में सुबह की नमाज़ पढ़ चुके तो अपनी ऊंटनी पर पालन लगाने का हुकुम फरमाया, सवारी लाई गई तो आप उस पर सवार हुए और जब वो आपको लेकर खड़ी हो गई तो आप खड़े हो कर क़िब्ला-रू हो गए और फिर लब्बैक कहना शुरू किया ता-अनके हरम में दाखिल हो गए। वहां पहुंच कर आपने लब्बैक कहना बंद कर दिया। फिर ज़ी-तवा में तशरीफ ला कर रात वहीं गुज़ारते सुबह होती तो नमाज़ पढ़ते और गुस्ल करते (फिर मक्का में दाखिल होते)। आप यकीन के साथ यह जानते थे कि रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने भी इसी तरह किया था। अब्दुल वारिस की तरह इस हदीस को इस्माइल ने भी अय्यूब से रिवायत किया। इसमें गुस्ल का ज़िक्र है।</p>

<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Aur Abu Mu&#x27;ammar ne kaha ki hum se Abdul Warith ne bayan kiya, unhone kaha ki hum se Ayyub Sakhtiyani ne Nafi se bayan kiya, unhone kaha ki Abdullah bin Umar (Radi Allahu Anhuma) jab Zul-Hulaifa mein subah ki namaz padh chuke toh apni oontni par paalan lagane ka hukum farmaya, sawari layi gayi toh aap us par sawaar hue aur jab woh aapko lekar khadi ho gayi toh aap khade ho kar qibla-ru ho gaye aur phir labbaik kehna shuru kiya ta-ankeh haram mein dakhil ho gaye. Aap yaqeen ke saath yeh jaante the ki Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne bhi isi tarah kiya tha. Abdul Warith ki tarah is hadith ko Isma&#x27;il ne bhi Ayyub se riwayat kiya. Ismein ghusal ka zikr hai.</p>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا سُلَيْمَانُ بْنُ دَاوُدَ أَبُو الرَّبِيعِ، حَدَّثَنَا فُلَيْحٌ، عَنْ نَافِعٍ، قَالَ كَانَ ابْنُ عُمَرَ ـ رضى الله عنهما ـ إِذَا أَرَادَ الْخُرُوجَ إِلَى مَكَّةَ ادَّهَنَ بِدُهْنٍ لَيْسَ لَهُ رَائِحَةٌ طَيِّبَةٌ، ثُمَّ يَأْتِي مَسْجِدَ الْحُلَيْفَةِ فَيُصَلِّي ثُمَّ يَرْكَبُ، وَإِذَا اسْتَوَتْ بِهِ رَاحِلَتُهُ قَائِمَةً أَحْرَمَ، ثُمَّ قَالَ هَكَذَا رَأَيْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَفْعَلُ‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Nafi`:Whenever Ibn `Umar intended to go to Mecca he used to oil himself with a sort of oil that had no pleasant smell, then he would go to the Mosque of Al-Hulaifa and offer the prayer, and then ride. When he mounted well on his Mount and the Mount stood up straight, he would proclaim the intention of assuming Ihram, and he used to say that he had seen the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) doing the same</p>

<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مَسْلَمَةَ، حَدَّثَنَا مَالِكٌ، عَنِ ابْنِ شِهَابٍ، عَنْ عُرْوَةَ بْنِ الزُّبَيْرِ، عَنْ عَائِشَةَ ـ رضى الله عنها ـ زَوْجِ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم قَالَتْ خَرَجْنَا مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فِي حَجَّةِ الْوَدَاعِ، فَأَهْلَلْنَا بِعُمْرَةٍ ثُمَّ قَالَ النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏&quot;‏ مَنْ كَانَ مَعَهُ هَدْىٌ فَلْيُهِلَّ بِالْحَجِّ مَعَ الْعُمْرَةِ، ثُمَّ لاَ يَحِلَّ حَتَّى يَحِلَّ مِنْهُمَا جَمِيعًا ‏&quot;‏ فَقَدِمْتُ مَكَّةَ وَأَنَا حَائِضٌ، وَلَمْ أَطُفْ بِالْبَيْتِ وَلاَ بَيْنَ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةِ، فَشَكَوْتُ ذَلِكَ إِلَى النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ انْقُضِي رَأْسَكِ وَامْتَشِطِي، وَأَهِلِّي بِالْحَجِّ، وَدَعِي الْعُمْرَةَ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ فَفَعَلْتُ فَلَمَّا قَضَيْنَا الْحَجَّ أَرْسَلَنِي النَّبِيُّ صلى الله عليه وسلم مَعَ عَبْدِ الرَّحْمَنِ بْنِ أَبِي بَكْرٍ إِلَى التَّنْعِيمِ فَاعْتَمَرْتُ فَقَالَ ‏&quot;‏ هَذِهِ مَكَانَ عُمْرَتِكِ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ قَالَتْ فَطَافَ الَّذِينَ كَانُوا أَهَلُّوا بِالْعُمْرَةِ بِالْبَيْتِ وَبَيْنَ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةِ، ثُمَّ حَلُّوا، ثُمَّ طَافُوا طَوَافًا وَاحِدًا بَعْدَ أَنْ رَجَعُوا مِنْ مِنًى، وَأَمَّا الَّذِينَ جَمَعُوا الْحَجَّ وَالْعُمْرَةَ فَإِنَّمَا طَافُوا طَوَافًا وَاحِدًا‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Aisha: (the wife of the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) We set out with the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) in his last Hajj and we assumed Ihram for Umra. The Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) then said, &quot;Whoever has the Hadi with him should assume Ihram for Hajj along with `Umra and should not finish the Ihram till he finishes both.&quot; I was menstruating when I reached Mecca, and so I neither did Tawaf round the Ka`ba nor Tawaf between Safa and Marwa. I complained about that to the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) on which he replied, &quot;Undo and comb your head hair, and assume Ihram for Hajj (only) and leave the Umra.&quot; So, I did so. When we had performed the Hajj, the Prophet sent me with my brother `Abdur-Rahman bin Abu Bakr to Tan`im. So I performed the `Umra. The Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said to me, &quot;This `Umra is instead of your missed one.&quot; Those who had assumed Ihram for `Umra (Hajj-at-Tamattu) performed Tawaf round the Ka`ba and between Safa and Marwa and then finished their Ihram. After returning from Mina, they performed another Tawaf (between Safa and Marwa). Those who had assumed Ihram for Hajj and `Umra together (Hajj-al-Qiran) performed only one Tawaf (between Safa and Marwa)</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ur-Latn">
<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Hum se Abdullah bin Maslama Qaa&#x27;nabi ne bayan kiya, kaha ke humein Imam Malik ne Ibn-e-Shihab se khabar di, unhein Urwa bin Zubair ne, un se Nabi Kareem (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki zauja mutahhara Aisha (Radi Allahu Anha) ne bayan kiya ke hum Hajjatul Wida mein Nabi Kareem (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ke saath rawaana hue. Pehle hum ne Umrah ka ehram baandha lekin Nabi Kareem (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya ke jis ke saath qurbani ho to usey Umrah ke saath Hajj ka bhi ehram baandh lena chahiye. Aisa shakhs darmiyan mein halal nahi ho sakta balkay Hajj aur Umrah dono se ek saath halal hoga. Main bhi Makkah aayi thi us waqt main haa&#x27;iza ho gayi, is liye na Baitullah ka tawaaf kar saki aur na Safa aur Marwa ki sa&#x27;i. Main ne is ke mutaliq Nabi Kareem (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se shikwa kiya to Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya ke apna sar khol daal, kangha kar aur Umrah chhod kar Hajj ka ehram baandh le. Chunancha main ne aisa hi kiya phir jab hum Hajj se farigh ho gaye to Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne mujhe mere bhai Abdur Rahman bin Abi Bakr ke saath Tan&#x27;eem bheja. Main ne wahan se Umrah ka ehram baandha (aur Umrah ada kiya). Nabi Kareem (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya ke yeh tumhare us Umrah ke badle mein hai (jise tum ne chhod diya tha). Aisha (Radi Allahu Anha) ne bayan kiya ke jin logon ne (Hajjatul Wida mein) sirf Umrah ka ehram baandha tha, woh Baitullah ka tawaaf, Safa aur Marwa ki sa&#x27;i kar ke halal ho gaye. The Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) added, &quot;Whoever has garlanded his Hady is not allowed to finish the Ihram till the Hady has reached its destination (has been sacrificed)&quot;. Then on the night of Tarwiya (8th Dhul Hijjah, in the afternoon) he ordered us to assume Ihram for Hajj and when we had performed all the ceremonies of Hajj, we came and performed Tawaf round the Ka&#x27;bah and (Sa&#x27;y) between As-Safa and Al-Marwa, and then our Hajj was complete, and we had to sacrifice a Hady according to the statement of Allah: &quot;... He must slaughter a Hady such as he can afford, but if he cannot afford it, he should observe Saum (fasts) three days during the Hajj and seven days after his return (to his home)….&quot; (V. 2: 196). And the sacrifice of the sheep is sufficient. So, the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) and his Companions joined the two religious deeds, (i.e. Hajj and &#x27;Umra) in one year, for Allah revealed (the permissibility) of such practice in His book and in the Sunna (legal ways) of His Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) and rendered it permissible for all the people except those living in Makkah. Allah says: &quot;This is for him whose family is not present at the Al-Masjid-Al-Haram, (i.e. non resident of Makkah).&quot; The months of Hajj which Allah mentioned in His book are: Shawwal, Dhul-Qa&#x27;da and Dhul-Hijjah. Whoever performed Hajj-at-Tamattu&#x27; in those months, then slaughtering or fasting is compulsory for him. The words: 1. Ar-Rafatha means sexual intercourse. 2. Al-Fasuq means all kinds of sin, and 3. Al-Jidal means to dispute</p>
