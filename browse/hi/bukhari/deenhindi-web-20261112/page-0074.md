<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Abdullah bin Abu Qatada: My father set out (for Mecca) in the year of Al-Hudaibiya, and his companions assumed Ihram, but he did not. At that time the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) was informed that an enemy wanted to attack him, so the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) proceeded onwards. While my father was among his companions, some of them laughed among themselves. (My father said), &quot;I looked up and saw an onager. I attacked, stabbed and caught it. I then sought my companions&#x27; help but they refused to help me. (Later) we all ate its meat. We were afraid that we might be left behind (separated) from the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) so I went in search of the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) and made my horse to run at a galloping speed at times and let it go slow at an ordinary speed at other times till I met a man from the tribe of Bani Ghifar at midnight. I asked him, &quot;Where did you leave the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) ?&quot; He replied, &quot;I left him at Ta&#x27;hin and he had the intention of having the midday rest at As-Suqya. I followed the trace and joined the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) and said, &#x27;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him)! Your people (companions) send you their compliments, and (ask for) Allah&#x27;s Blessings upon you. They are afraid lest they may be left behind; so please wait for them.&#x27; I added, &#x27;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him)! I hunted an onager and some of its meat is with me. The Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) told the people to eat it though all of them were in the state of Ihram</p>

Main is par jhapta aur neezay se usay thanda kar diya. Main ne apne sathiyon ki madad chahi thi lekin unhon ne inkar kar diya tha, phir hum ne gosht khaya. Ab humein yeh dar hua ke kahin (Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se) door na ho jayen, chunancha main ne aap ko talash karna shuru kar diya kabhi apne ghoray tez kar deta aur kabhi aahista, aakhir raat gaye Banu Ghifar ke ek shakhs se mulaqat ho gayi. Main ne poocha ke Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) kahan hain? Unhon ne bataya ke jab main aap se juda hua to aap maqam Ta&#x27;han mein thay aur aap ka irada tha ke maqam Suqya mein pohanch kar dopehar ka aaram karenge. Gharz main Nabi Kareem (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki khidmat mein hazir ho gaya aur main ne arz ki ya Rasool Allah! Aap ke as-haab aap par salam aur Allah ki rehmat bhejte hain. Unhein yeh dar hai ke kahin woh bohat peechay na reh jayen. Is liye aap thehar kar un ka intezar karein, phir main ne kaha ya Rasool Allah! main ne ek jungli gadha shikar kiya tha aur us ka kuch bacha hua gosht ab bhi mere paas majood hai, Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne logon se khane ke liye farmaya halankeh woh sab ehram bandhay hue thay.</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا سَعِيدُ بْنُ الرَّبِيعِ، حَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ الْمُبَارَكِ، عَنْ يَحْيَى، عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ أَبِي قَتَادَةَ، أَنَّ أَبَاهُ، حَدَّثَهُ قَالَ انْطَلَقْنَا مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم عَامَ الْحُدَيْبِيَةِ فَأَحْرَمَ أَصْحَابُهُ، وَلَمْ أُحْرِمْ، فَأُنْبِئْنَا بِعَدُوٍّ بِغَيْقَةَ فَتَوَجَّهْنَا نَحْوَهُمْ، فَبَصُرَ أَصْحَابِي بِحِمَارِ وَحْشٍ، فَجَعَلَ بَعْضُهُمْ يَضْحَكُ إِلَى بَعْضٍ، فَنَظَرْتُ فَرَأَيْتُهُ فَحَمَلْتُ عَلَيْهِ الْفَرَسَ، فَطَعَنْتُهُ، فَأَثْبَتُّهُ، فَاسْتَعَنْتُهُمْ، فَأَبَوْا أَنْ يُعِينُونِي، فَأَكَلْنَا مِنْهُ، ثُمَّ لَحِقْتُ بِرَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَخَشِينَا أَنْ نُقْتَطَعَ، أَرْفَعُ فَرَسِي شَأْوًا، وَأَسِيرُ عَلَيْهِ شَأْوًا، فَلَقِيتُ رَجُلاً مِنْ بَنِي غِفَارٍ فِي جَوْفِ اللَّيْلِ فَقُلْتُ أَيْنَ تَرَكْتَ رَسُولَ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم فَقَالَ تَرَكْتُهُ بِتَعْهِنَ وَهُوَ قَائِلٌ السُّقْيَا‏.‏ فَلَحِقْتُ بِرَسُولِ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم حَتَّى أَتَيْتُهُ فَقُلْتُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، إِنَّ أَصْحَابَكَ أَرْسَلُوا يَقْرَءُونَ عَلَيْكَ السَّلاَمَ وَرَحْمَةَ اللَّهِ وَبَرَكَاتِهِ، وَإِنَّهُمْ قَدْ خَشُوا أَنْ يَقْتَطِعَهُمُ الْعُدُوُّ دُونَكَ، فَانْظُرْهُمْ، فَفَعَلَ فَقُلْتُ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ، إِنَّا اصَّدْنَا حِمَارَ وَحْشٍ، وَإِنَّ عِنْدَنَا فَاضِلَةً‏.‏ فَقَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم لأَصْحَابِهِ ‏ &quot;‏ كُلُوا ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ وَهُمْ مُحْرِمُونَ‏.‏</p>

<p dir="rtl">[Sahih Al-Bukhari · 1822]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Abdullah bin Abu Qatada: That his father said &quot;We proceeded with the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) in the year of Al-Hudaibiya and his companions assumed Ihram but I did not. We were informed that some enemies were at Ghaiqa and so we went on towards them. My companions saw an onager and some of them started laughing among themselves. I looked and saw it. I chased it with my horse and stabbed and caught it. I wanted some help from my companions but they refused. (I slaughtered it all alone). We all ate from it (i.e. its meat). Then I followed Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) lest we should be left behind. At times I urged my horse to run at a galloping speed and at other times at an ordinary slow speed. On the way I met a man from the tribe of Bani Ghifar at midnight. I asked him where he had left Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) . The man replied that he had left the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) at a place called Ta&#x27;hin and he had the intention of having the midday rest at As-Suqya. So, I followed Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) till I reached him and said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him)! I have been sent by my companions who send you their greetings and compliments and ask for Allah&#x27;s Mercy and Blessings upon you. They were afraid lest the enemy might intervene between you and them; so please wait for them.&quot; So he did. Then I said, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him)! We have hunted an onager and have some of it (i.e. its meat) left over.&quot; Allah&#x27;s Messenger (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) told his companions to eat the meat although all of them were in a state of Ihram</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Al-Bukhari · 1822]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="hi">
<h3>Hindi</h3>

<p dir="auto">हम से सईद बिन रबी ने बयां किया अब्दुल्लाह बिन आबि क़तादा ने करीम (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) के साथ चले उन के साथियों ने तो एहराम बांध लिया था लेकिन उन का बयान था के मैं ने ऐहराम नहीं बांधा था. हमें हैगा में दुश्मन के मजूद होने की इत्तिला मिली इस लिए हम उन की तलाश में (नबी करीम (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) के हुक्म के मुताबिक़) निकले फिर मेरे साथियों ने जोर-कहर देखा और एक दूसरे को देख कर हँसने लगे मैं ने जो नज़र उठाई तो उसे देख लिया घोराय पर (सवार हो कर) उस पर झपटा और उसे ज़ख़्मी कर के ठंडा कर दिया. मैं ने अपने साथियों से कुछ इम्दाद चाही लेकिन उन्हों ने इंकार कर दिया फिर हम सब ने उसे खाया और उस के बाद मैं रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) की खिदमत में हाज़िर हुआ (पहले) हमें दर हुआ कि कहीं हम नबी करीम (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) से दूर न रह जाएँ इस लिए मैं कभी अपना घोड़ा तेज़ कर देता और कभी आहिस्ता आखिर मेरी मुलाक़ात एक बानी हिफ़ार के आदमी से आधी रात में हुई मैं ने पुछा के रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) कहाँ हैं? उन्हों ने बताया के मैं आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) से ता&#x27;हाँ नमी जगह में अलग हुआ था और आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) का इरादा यह था कि दोपहर को मक़ाम सुक्या में आराम करेंगे फिर जब मैं रसूल अल्लाह (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) की खिदमत में हाज़िर हुआ तो मैं ने अर्ज़ की या रसूल अल्लाह! आप के आस-हाब ने आप को सलाम कहा है और उन्हें डर है कि कहीं दुश्मन आप के और उन के दरमियान हाईल न हो जाये इस लिए आप उन का इंतज़ार कीजिये चुनांचा आप ने ऐसा ही किया गौर-कहर का शिकार किया और कुछ बचा हुआ गोश्त अब भी मजूद है इस पर आप (सल्लल्लाहु अलैहि वसल्लम) ने अपने अस-हाब से फ़रमाया के खाओ हालाँकिह वह सब एहराम बांधे हुए थे.</p>

<p dir="auto">[Sahih Al-Bukhari · 1822]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ur-Latn">
<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Hum se Saeed bin Rabee ne bayan kiya, kaha hum se Ali bin Mubarak ne bayan kiya, un se Yahya bin Abi Kaseer ne, un se Abdullah bin Abi Qatada ne, kaha un se un ke baap ne bayan kiya unhon ne kaha ke hum Sulh Hudaibiya ke mauqay par Nabi Kareem (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ke sath chalay un ke sathiyon ne to ehram bandh liya tha lekin un ka bayan tha ke main ne ehram nahi bandha tha. Hamein Ghaiga mein dushman ke majood hone ki ittila mili is liye hum un ki talash mein (Nabi Kareem (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ke hukm ke mutabiq) niklay phir mere sathiyon ne gor-khar dekha aur ek dusre ko dekh kar hansne lagay main ne jo nazar uthayi to usay dekh liya ghoray par (sawar ho kar) us par jhapta aur usay zakhmi kar ke thanda kar diya. Main ne apne sathiyon se kuch imdad chahi lekin unhon ne inkar kar diya phir hum sab ne usay khaya aur us ke baad main Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki khidmat mein hazir hua (pehle) humein dar hua ke kahin hum Nabi Kareem (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se door na reh jayen is liye main kabhi apna ghora tez kar deta aur kabhi aahista aakhir meri mulaqat ek Bani Ghifar ke aadmi se aadhi raat mein hui main ne poocha ke Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) kahan hain? Unhon ne bataya ke main Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) se Ta&#x27;han nami jagah mein alag hua tha aur Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ka irada yeh tha ke dopehar ko maqam Suqya mein aaram karenge phir jab main Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki khidmat mein hazir hua to main ne arz ki ya Rasool Allah! Aap ke as-haab ne aap ko salam kaha hai aur unhein dar hai ke kahin dushman aap ke aur un ke darmiyan hail na ho jaye is liye aap un ka intezar kijiye chunancha aap ne aisa hi kiya, main ne yeh bhi arz ki ke ya Rasool Allah! main ne ek gor-khar ka shikar kiya aur kuch bacha hua gosht ab bhi majood hai is par Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne apne as-haab se farmaya ke khao halankeh woh sab ehram bandhay hue thay.</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Al-Bukhari · 1822]</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="ar">
<h3>Arabic</h3>

<p dir="rtl">حَدَّثَنَا عَبْدُ اللَّهِ بْنُ مُحَمَّدٍ، حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ، حَدَّثَنَا صَالِحُ بْنُ كَيْسَانَ، عَنْ أَبِي مُحَمَّدٍ، نَافِعٍ مَوْلَى أَبِي قَتَادَةَ سَمِعَ أَبَا قَتَادَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ كُنَّا مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِالْقَاحَةِ مِنَ الْمَدِينَةِ عَلَى ثَلاَثٍ ح‏.‏ وَحَدَّثَنَا عَلِيُّ بْنُ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ حَدَّثَنَا سُفْيَانُ حَدَّثَنَا صَالِحُ بْنُ كَيْسَانَ عَنْ أَبِي مُحَمَّدٍ عَنْ أَبِي قَتَادَةَ ـ رضى الله عنه ـ قَالَ كُنَّا مَعَ النَّبِيِّ صلى الله عليه وسلم بِالْقَاحَةِ، وَمِنَّا الْمُحْرِمُ، وَمِنَّا غَيْرُ الْمُحْرِمِ، فَرَأَيْتُ أَصْحَابِي يَتَرَاءَوْنَ شَيْئًا فَنَظَرْتُ، فَإِذَا حِمَارُ وَحْشٍ ـ يَعْنِي وَقَعَ سَوْطُهُ ـ فَقَالُوا لاَ نُعِينُكَ عَلَيْهِ بِشَىْءٍ، إِنَّا مُحْرِمُونَ‏.‏ فَتَنَاوَلْتُهُ فَأَخَذْتُهُ، ثُمَّ أَتَيْتُ الْحِمَارَ مِنْ وَرَاءِ أَكَمَةٍ، فَعَقَرْتُهُ، فَأَتَيْتُ بِهِ أَصْحَابِي، فَقَالَ بَعْضُهُمْ كُلُوا‏.‏ وَقَالَ بَعْضُهُمْ لاَ تَأْكُلُوا‏.‏ فَأَتَيْتُ النَّبِيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم وَهُوَ أَمَامَنَا، فَسَأَلْتُهُ فَقَالَ ‏ &quot;‏ كُلُوهُ حَلاَلٌ ‏&quot;‏‏.‏ قَالَ لَنَا عَمْرٌو اذْهَبُوا إِلَى صَالِحٍ فَسَلُوهُ عَنْ هَذَا وَغَيْرِهِ، وَقَدِمَ عَلَيْنَا هَا هُنَا‏.‏</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Qatada: We were in the company of the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) at a place called Al-Qaha (which is at a distance of three stages of journey from Medina). Abu Qatada narrated through another group of narrators: We were in the company of the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) at a place called Al-Qaha and some of us had assumed Ihram while the others had not. I noticed that some of my companions were watching something, so I looked up and saw an onager. (I rode my horse and took the spear and whip) but my whip fell down (and I asked them to pick it up for me) but they said, &quot;We will not help you by any means as we are in a state of Ihram.&quot; So, I picked up the whip myself and attacked the onager from behind a hillock and slaughtered it and brought it to my companions. Some of them said, &quot;Eat it.&quot; While some others said, &quot;Do not eat it.&quot; So, I went to the Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) who was ahead of us and asked him about it, He replied, &quot;Eat it as it is Halal (i.e. it is legal to eat it)</p>

The Prophet (peace and blessings of Allah be upon him) said to them, &quot;Proceed along the seashore till we meet all together.&quot; So, they took the route of the seashore, and when they started all of them assumed Ihram except Abu Qatada. While they were proceeding on, his companions saw a group of onagers. Abu Qatada chased the onagers and attacked and wounded a sheonager. <section class="language-reading" lang="ur-Latn">
<h3>Roman Urdu</h3>

<p dir="ltr">Hum se Musa bin Ismail ne bayan kiya, kaha hum se Abu Awana ne bayan kiya, un se Usman bin Mohib ne bayan kiya, kaha ke mujhe Abdullah bin Abi Qatada (Radi Allahu Anhu) ne khabar di aur unhein un ke walid Abu Qatada ne khabar di ke Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) (hajj ka) irada kar ke niklay. Sahaba (Ridwan Allahu Alaihim) bhi aap ke sath thay. Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne sahaba ki ek jamaat ko jis mein Abu Qatada (Radi Allahu Anhu) bhi thay yeh hidayat de kar rastay se wapas bheja ke tum log darya ke kinaray kinaray ho kar jao, (aur dushman ka pata lagao) phir hum se aa milo. Chunancha yeh jamaat darya ke kinaray kinaray chali, wapsi mein sab ne ehram bandh liya tha lekin Abu Qatada (Radi Allahu Anhu) ne abhi ehram nahi bandha tha. Yeh qafila chal raha tha ke kayi gor-khar dikhayi diye, Abu Qatada ne un par hamla kiya aur ek mada ka shikar kar liya, phir ek jagah thehar kar sab ne us ka gosht khaya aur sath hi yeh khayal bhi aaya ke kya hum muhram hone ke bawajood shikar ka gosht bhi kha sakte hain? Chunancha jo kuch gosht bacha woh hum sath laye aur jab Rasool Allah (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ki khidmat mein pohanchay to arz ki ya Rasool Allah! Hum sab log to muhram thay lekin Abu Qatada (Radi Allahu Anhu) ne ehram nahi bandha tha phir hum ne gor-khar dekhe aur Abu Qatada (Radi Allahu Anhu) ne un par hamla kar ke ek mada ka shikar kar liya, us ke baad ek jagah hum ne qayam kiya aur us ka gosht khaya phir khayal aaya ke kya hum muhram hone ke bawajood shikar ka gosht kha bhi sakte hain? Is liye jo kuch gosht baqi bacha hai woh hum sath laye hain. Aap ne poocha kya tum mein se kisi ne Abu Qatada (Radi Allahu Anhu) ko shikar karne ke liye kaha tha? Ya kisi ne is shikar ki taraf ishara kiya tha? Sab ne kaha nahi. Is par Aap (Sallallahu Alaihi Wasallam) ne farmaya ke phir bacha hua gosht bhi kha lo.</p>

<p dir="ltr">[Sahih Al-Bukhari · 1824]</p>
