<p dir="auto">Reviewed source parallel; wording can differ between editions.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: A man asked the Messenger of Allah (may Allah&#x27;s peace and blessings be upon him), saying: O Messenger of Allah, we travel by sea, and we carry with us a little water.
