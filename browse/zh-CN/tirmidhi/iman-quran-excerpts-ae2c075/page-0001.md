<h3>English · full narration</h3>

<p dir="auto">Related publisher version: Arabic wording or revision differs from the linked source version. Full narration; the selected source preserves an excerpt or reported meaning.</p>

<p dir="auto">Zirr ibn Hubaysh reported: I went to Safwān ibn ‘Assāl al-Murādi to ask him about wiping over leather socks. He said: What brought you here, O Zirr? I said: Pursuit of knowledge. He said: Indeed, the angels lower their wings for the seeker of knowledge out of pleasure for what he seeks. I said: I entertain some doubts regarding wiping over leather socks after defecation or urination. Now since you are one of the Prophet&#x27;s Companions, I have come to ask you whether you heard him say anything about this. He replied: Yes. He used to command us, when we were on a journey or were travellers, not to remove our leather socks for three days and nights—except in the case of Janābah (major ritual impurity). In other cases such as defecation, urination, or sleep, it is sufficient to wipe over leather socks. Then I asked him: &quot;Did you hear him say anything about love?&quot; He replied: Yes. While we were with the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) on a journey, a Bedouin called out in a loud voice: O Muhammad! The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) replied in the same tone: &quot;Here I am!&quot; We said to him (the Bedouin): Woe to you! Lower your voice, for you are in the Prophet&#x27;s presence, where you are not allowed to do so. He said: By Allah, I will not lower my voice. The Bedouin said: What about a person who loves some people but cannot catch up with them? The Prophet (ﷺ) replied: &quot;A person shall be with those whom he loves on the Day of Judgment.&quot; He kept speaking to us, and during his speech he mentioned a gate in the direction of the west, the width of which a rider crosses its width in forty or seventy years.</p>

<p dir="auto">[Jami al-Tirmidhi no. 3535; Sunan Ibn Majah no. 478; Musnad Ahmad no. 18095]</p>
