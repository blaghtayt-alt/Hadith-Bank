<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English · full narration</h3>

<p dir="auto">Reviewed source parallel; wording can differ between editions. Full narration; the selected source preserves an excerpt or reported meaning. The collection number is a reviewed Arabic-source cross-reference; HadeethEnc’s bibliography does not supply a hadith number.</p>

<p dir="auto">Abu Sa‘īd al-Khudri (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: Once the Messenger of Allah (may Allah&#x27;s peace and blessings be upon him) was leading his Companions in prayer when all of a sudden he removed his sandals and placed them on his left. When the people saw that, they threw off their sandals too. When the Messenger of Allah (may Allah&#x27;s peace and blessings be upon him) finished his prayer he said: &quot;What made you all throw off your sandals?&quot; They said: &quot;We saw you throw off your sandals, so we threw off ours.&quot; The Messenger of Allah (may Allah&#x27;s peace and blessings be upon him) said: &quot;Jibrīl (may Allah&#x27;s peace and blessings be upon him) came to me and told me that they had some filth on them (or he said &#x27;dirt&#x27;).&quot; Then the Prophet (may Allah&#x27;s peace and blessings be upon him) said: &quot;When anyone of you comes to the mosque, he should look (at his sandals). If he sees any filth or dirt on his sandals, he should wipe it off and pray in them.&quot;</p>

<p dir="auto">[Sunan Abi Dawud no. 650]</p>

<h3>Chinese (Simplified)</h3>

<p dir="auto">艾布·赛义德传述：穆圣正在领门弟子们礼拜之际，他脱下自己鞋放在左边，大家看见便都扔掉鞋。礼完拜后，穆圣说：“你们为啥扔掉鞋？”大家回答说：“我们见你脱鞋，我们便也脱了鞋。”穆圣说；“哲布利莱降临我说鞋上有污秽。”又接着说：“如果你们来于清真寺时当看鞋子，有污秽则当拭净后穿上礼拜。”</p>
