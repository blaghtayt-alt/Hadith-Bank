<h3>English · full narration</h3>

<p dir="auto">‘Abd Khayr reported: ‘Ali ibn Abi Tālib (may Allah be pleased with him) came to us after he had prayed. He asked for water and we said: &quot;What is he going to do with the water as he has (already) prayed? He only wants to teach us.&quot; A container of water and a basin were brought to him. He poured some water onto his right hand and washed his hands three times. Then he rinsed his mouth and nose three times, using the hand with which he took the water. Then he washed his face three times and he washed his right arm three times and his left arm three times. Then he put his hand in the container and wiped his head once. Then he washed his right foot three times and his left foot three times. Then he said: &#x27;Whoever wanted to learn how the Messenger of Allah (may Allah&#x27;s peace and blessings be upon him) performed ablution, this is it.</p>
