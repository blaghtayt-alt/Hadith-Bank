<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Abu Hazim narrates Abu Huraira&#x27;s ablution and answer including a saying attributed only to my Friend in this English field. The honorific is retained without supplying an absent name.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Abu Hazim reported:I was (standing) behind Abu Huraira and he was performing the ablution for prayer. He extended the (washing) of his hand that it went up to his armpit. I said to him: O Abu Huraira, what is this ablution? He said: O of the tribe of Faruukh, you are here; if I knew that you were here, I would have never performed ablution like this; I have heard my Friend (ﷺ) say. In a believer adornment would reach the places where ablution reaches</p>
