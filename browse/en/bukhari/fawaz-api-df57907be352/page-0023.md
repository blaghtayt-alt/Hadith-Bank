<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Al-Hasan transmits Maqil&#x27;s account of his sister&#x27;s remarriage and the associated prohibition on preventing it.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Al-Hasan:concerning the Verse: &#x27;Do not prevent them&#x27; (2.232) Ma&#x27;qil bin Yasar told me that it was revealed in his connection. He said, &quot;I married my sister to a man and he divorced her, and when her days of &#x27;Idda (three menstrual periods) were over, the man came again and asked for her hand, but I said to him, &#x27;I married her to you and made her your bed (your wife) and favored you with her, but you divorced her. Now you come to ask for her hand again? No, by Allah, she will never go back to you (again)!&#x27; That man was not a bad man and his wife wanted to go back to him. So Allah revealed this Verse: &#x27;Do not prevent them.&#x27; (2.232) So I said, &#x27;Now I will do it (let her go back to him), O Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ).&quot;So he married her to him again</p>

<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Aisha explains the guardian&#x27;s obstruction of an orphan girl&#x27;s marriage and the cited verse.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Aisha:(regarding His Statement): &#x27;They ask your instruction concerning the women. Say: Allah instructs you about them ...&#x27; (4.127) It is about the female orphan who is under the guardianship of a man with whom she shares her property and he does not want to marry her and dislikes that someone else should marry her, lest he should share the property with him, so he prevents her from marrying. So Allah forbade such a guardian to do so (i.e. to prevent her from marrying)</p>

<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Urwa transmits Aisha&#x27;s extended explanation of orphan marriage, mahr and the later instruction verse.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated &#x27;Urwa bin Az-Zubair:that he asked `Aisha, saying to her, &quot;O Mother! (In what connection was this Verse revealed): &#x27;If you fear that you shall not be able to deal justly with orphan girls (to the end of the verse) that your right hands possess?&quot; (4.3) Aisha said, &quot;O my nephew! It was about the female orphan under the protection of her guardian who was interested in her beauty and wealth and wanted to marry her with a little or reduced Mahr. So such guardians were forbidden to marry female orphans unless they deal with them justly and give their full Mahr; and they were ordered to marry women other than them.&quot;`Aisha added, &quot;(Later) the people asked Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ), for instructions, and then Allah revealed: &#x27;They ask your instruction concerning the women . . . And yet whom you desire to marry.&#x27; (4.127) So Allah revealed to them in this Verse that-if a female orphan had wealth and beauty, they desired to marry her and were interested in her noble descent and the reduction of her Mahr; but if she was not desired by them because of her lack in fortune and beauty they left her and married some other woman. So, as they used to leave her when they had no interest in her, they had no right to marry her if they had the desire to do so, unless they deal justly with her and gave her a full amount of Mahr</p>
