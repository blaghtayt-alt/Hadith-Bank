<p dir="auto">This source entry preserves 3 separately attributed reports in their original order. They remain one provider entry, with their distinct narrator introductions and source references. Distinct statements of Mujahid, Ibn Abbas and Ata concerning widow residence remain separate source components.</p>

<p class="counterpart-note">Source report 1</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Mujahid:(regarding the Verse): &#x27;If any of you dies and leaves wives behind,&#x27; That was the period of the &#x27;Iddah which the widow was obliged to spend in the house of the late husband. Then Allah revealed: And those of you who die and leave wives should bequeath for their wives a year&#x27;s maintenance and residence without turning them out, but if they leave, there is no blame on you for what they do of themselves, provided it is honorable (i.e. lawful marriage) (2.240) Mujahid said: Allah has ordered that a widow has the right to stay for seven months and twenty days with her husband&#x27;s relatives through her husband&#x27;s will and testament so that she will complete the period of one year (of &#x27;Iddah). But the widow has the right to stay that extra period or go out of her husband&#x27;s house as is indicated by the statement of Allah: &#x27;But if they leave there is no blame on you,... &#x27; (2.240)</p>

<p class="counterpart-note">Source report 2</p>

<p dir="ltr"> Ibn `Abbas said: The above Verse has cancelled the order of spending the period of the &#x27;Iddah at her late husband&#x27;s house, and so she could spend her period of the &#x27;Iddah wherever she likes. And Allah says: &#x27;Without turning them out.&#x27;</p>

<p class="counterpart-note">Source report 3</p>

<p dir="ltr"> &#x27;Ata said: If she would, she could spend her period of the &#x27;Iddah at her husband&#x27;s house, and live there according to her (husband&#x27;s) will and testament, and if she would, she could go out (of her husband&#x27;s house) as Allah says: &#x27;There is no blame on you for what they do of themselves.&#x27; (2.240) &#x27;Ata added: Then the Verses of inheritance were revealed and the order of residence (for the widow) was cancelled, and she could spend her period of the &#x27;Iddah wherever she would like, and she was no longer entitled to be accommodated by her husband&#x27;s family</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Huraira:I used to accompany Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) to fill my stomach; and that was when I did not eat baked bread, nor wear silk. Neither a male nor a female slave used to serve me, and I used to bind stones over my belly and ask somebody to recite a Qur&#x27;anic Verse for me though I knew it, so that he might take me to his house and feed me. Ja`far bin Abi Talib was very kind to the poor, and he used to take us and feed us with what ever was available in his house, (and if nothing was available), he used to give us the empty (honey or butter) skin which we would tear and lick whatever was in it</p>
