<p dir="ltr">Narrated Abu Huraira:The people used to say, &quot;Abu Huraira narrates too many narrations.&quot; In fact I used to keep close to Allah&#x27;s Messenger (ﷺ) and was satisfied with what filled my stomach. I ate no leavened bread and dressed no decorated striped clothes, and never did a man or a woman serve me, and I often used to press my belly against gravel because of hunger, and I used to ask a man to recite a Qur&#x27;anic Verse to me although I knew it, so that he would take me to his home and feed me. And the most generous of all the people to the poor was Ja`far bin Abi Talib. He used to take us to his home and offer us what was available therein. He would even offer us an empty folded leather container (of butter) which we would split and lick whatever was in it</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated `Aisha:On the day of the battle of Uhud the pagans were defeated completely. Then Satan shouted loudly, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s slaves! Beware the ones behind you!&quot; So the front files attacked the back ones. Then Hudhaifa looked and saw his father, and said loudly, &quot;O Allah&#x27;s slaves! My father! My father!&quot; By Allah, they did not stop till they killed him (i.e. Hudaifa&#x27;s father). Hudhaifa said, &quot;May Allah forgive you!&quot; The sub-narrator said, &quot;By Allah, because of what Hudhaifa said, he remained in a good state till he met Allah (i.e. died)</p>
