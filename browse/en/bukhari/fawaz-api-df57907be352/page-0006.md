<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English</h3>

<p dir="auto">Abdur Rahman recounts Umar gathering Ramadan worshippers behind one reciter and Umar&#x27;s remarks. The final explanation of the preferred time remains a source note.</p>

<p dir="ltr">Abdur Rahman bin &#x27;Abdul Qari said, &quot;I went out in the company of &#x27;Umar bin Al-Khattab one night in Ramadan to the mosque and found the people praying in different groups. A man praying alone or a man praying with a little group behind him. So, &#x27;Umar said, &#x27;In my opinion I would better collect these (people) under the leadership of one Qari (Reciter) (i.e. let them pray in congregation!)&#x27;. So, he made up his mind to congregate them behind Ubai bin Ka&#x27;b. Then on another night I went again in his company and the people were praying behind their reciter. On that, &#x27;Umar remarked, &#x27;What an excellent Bid&#x27;a (i.e. innovation in religion) this is; but the prayer which they do not perform, but sleep at its time is better than the one they are offering.&#x27;</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Khabbab:I was a blacksmith and did some work for Al-`As bin Wail. When he owed me some money for my work, I went to him to ask for that amount. He said, &quot;I will not pay you unless you disbelieve in Muhammad.&quot; I said, &quot;By Allah! I will never do that till you die and be resurrected.&quot; He said, &quot;Will I be dead and then resurrected after my death?&quot; I said, &quot;Yes.&quot; He said, &quot;There I will have property and offspring and then I will pay you your due.&quot; Then Allah revealed. &#x27;Have you seen him who disbelieved in Our signs, and yet says: I will be given property and offspring?</p>
