<p dir="auto">This source entry preserves two separately attributed reports in their original order. They remain one provider entry, with their distinct narrator introductions and source references. Independent provider occurrence containing both burial accounts; retain both components and the separate identity.</p>

<p class="counterpart-note">Source report 1</p>

<p dir="ltr">Narrated Hisham&#x27;s father:`Aisha said to `Abdullah bin Az-Zubair, &quot;Bury me with my female companions (i.e. the wives of the Prophet) and do not bury me with the Prophet (ﷺ) in the house, for I do not like to be regarded as sanctified (just for being buried there).&#x27;&#x27;</p>

<p class="counterpart-note">Source report 2</p>

<p dir="ltr"> Narrated Hisham&#x27;s father: `Umar sent a message to `Aisha, saying, &quot;Will you allow me to be buried with my two companions (the Prophet (ﷺ) and Abu Bakr) ?&quot; She said, &quot;Yes, by Allah.&quot; though it was her habit that if a man from among the companions (of the Prophet (ﷺ) ) sent her a message asking her to allow him to be buried there, she would say, &quot;No, by Allah, I will never give permission to anyone to be buried with them</p>

Allah has told you that the people of the Scriptures have changed some of Allah&#x27;s Books and distorted it and wrote something with their own hands and said, &#x27;This is from Allah, so as to have a minor gain for it. Won&#x27;t the knowledge that has come to you stop you from asking them? No, by Allah, we have never seen a man from them asking you about that (the Book Al-Qur&#x27;an ) which has been revealed to you</p>
