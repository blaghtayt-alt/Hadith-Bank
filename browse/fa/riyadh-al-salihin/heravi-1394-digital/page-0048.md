<p dir="auto">Jābir (may Allah be pleased with him) reported that the Messenger of Allah (may Allah&#x27;s peace and blessings be upon him) was asked: &quot;Which part of the prayer is the best?&quot; He replied: &quot;The long standing.&quot;</p>
