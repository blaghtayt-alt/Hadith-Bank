<section class="language-reading" lang="en">
<h3>English · full narration</h3>

<p dir="auto">Full narration; the selected source preserves an excerpt or reported meaning.</p>

<p dir="auto">Abu Mūsa al-Ash‘ari (may Allah be pleased with him) reported that the Messenger of Allah (may Allah&#x27;s peace and blessings be upon him) said: &quot;The example of a believer who recites the Qur&#x27;an is like that of a citron; it smells and tastes good. And the example of a believer who does not recite the Qur&#x27;an is like that of a date; it has no smell and tastes good. And the example of a hypocrite who recites the Qur&#x27;an is like that of a basil; it smells good but tastes bitter. And the example of a hypocrite who does not recite the Qur&#x27;an is like that of a colocynth; it has no smell and tastes bitter.&quot;</p>

<p dir="auto">[Sahih al-Bukhari no. 5427; Sahih Muslim no. 797]</p>

‘Abdullāh ibn ‘Umar (may Allah be pleased with him) reported that the Prophet (may Allah&#x27;s peace and blessings be upon him) said: &quot;There should be no envy except in two (cases): a man whom Allah has given (knowledge of) the Qur&#x27;an, and he recites it during the night and day, and a man whom Allah has given wealth, and he spends it during the night and day (in a rightful way).”</p>

<p dir="auto">Abu Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him) reported: The Messenger of Allah (may Allah&#x27;s peace and blessings be upon him) said: &quot;Whoever relieves a believer from one of the hardships of this world, Allah will relieve him from one of the hardships of the Day of Judgment. Whoever treads a path seeking knowledge, Allah will make easy for him a path to Paradise. No group of people gather in one of the houses of Allah, reciting the Book of Allah and studying it among themselves, except that tranquility descends upon them, mercy envelops them, the angels surround them, and Allah mentions them to those near Him.
